2. **Plates and dead ends.** `PLACEHOLDER_*` plates close electrical D02, waste dispatch and the condenser's D01 service door. The condenser bay is underground with no walkable access. Propose (and, if the owner agrees, build) real connections or sealed closures, with the measurements.

Measure with renders at walking height in every junction (reactor stub, turbine entry, electrical, cooling, waste, dock, concourse, spine) and fix readability without editing source room files (local overrides in the build script).

7. **Unity.** `AGENTS.md` and `unity-validation` rule: there may be no runnable Unity project. If there is not, stop at an export package and a written readiness checklist; do not install packages or pick infrastructure.
8. **Open items you may find:** the gantry (no attachment spec exists; do not invent one), the plan's dropped features (outer ring, yard passage, medical direct door, fuel-corridor service hall), S02_CLEAN and S03_WASTE on the fuel corridor, the hall's own door labels, the AAA finishes' reach-check problems.
