# Independent correction-cycle review — R11

**Reviewer:** independent GPT-6 Luna critic

**Scope:** all 11 completed R11 fixed views, compared with R10 and reviewed against the refinery brief, Critical Shift art direction, owner rubric, and actual Spawn quality-reference renders. Technical evidence includes `build_R11.json`, `validation_R11.json`, and `critics/technical_R11.md`.

**Decision:** **R11 improves small authored details and support construction, but the product-hose assembly has a significant source-verified obstruction. The cycle remains below the owner's ≥99-per-category and weighted ≥99 acceptance threshold.** Text marks now sit on their intended faces, the crusher roof has explicit bearing supports, and the control enclosures carry captions. However, the product hose intersects four solid fittings that should provide an open path. Its visual presentation also remains dominated by boxy machinery, clean broad surfaces and a generic sorter arch. Scores reflect current R11 pixels and audit only.

## R10-to-R11 change assessment

**Improvements confirmed in the R11 images and audit:**

- The PV tag lettering and approval mark remain seated on the tag, and its wire reaches the clip and paper in `CAM_HERO_DETAIL`.
- The original enclosure faces have captions. `CAM_MATERIAL`, `CAM_PINCH` and `CAM_DISPATCH` show small labels associated with the controls; the source checks confirm 26 engraved labels on their angled faces and 35 printed marks on their target sheets.
- `CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER` shows the crusher jaw opening, teeth and folded surround; the feed roof has shaped bearing strips and an explicit rear-wall support record.
- The PV handwheel meshes include localized steel on their upper gripping arcs, improving the material cue without adding decals.
- The nook radio has two contact soles and its mug is separated from the body. The parts moved together and meet the tabletop in the independent audit.

**Regressions or unresolved defects:**

- The product connection added in R10 is now occluded by four solid fittings. The hex compression nut is capped; 60/390 hose vertices are inside its closed volume with 20 surface triangle-pair overlaps. The capped neck sleeve contains 23/390 hose vertices and has 48 triangle-pair overlaps. The solid processor and dryer flange disks each contain 17/390 hose vertices and block their centerline rays. This is a significant machine-assembly defect despite `validation_R11.json` reporting PASS.
- Caption placement is physically correct, but several legends remain minute and low-contrast from the camera distance (`CAM_ENTRY`/`CAM_PROCESS`); geometric placement does not make them gameplay-readable.
- The broad process line still shares rectangular shells, simple supports and similar controls. The reader support redesign is visible, but the hood and central optical-plate silhouette remain largely rectangular.
- The reddish working-edge polish is attached to the tabletop assembly, but part of it extends past the timber bounds and reads as a loose strip in `CAM_WORK_NOOK`.
- Main route illumination is warm and quite even, while the practical diffusers clip bright; improve the exposure balance without removing or hiding the physical light sources.

## Camera-by-camera defects

- **CAM_ENTRY:** the main process row and open KEEP CLEAR floor remain easy to parse. The large `02` wall mark still dominates a quiet wall panel, while the machine-specific labels remain tiny in this wide composition. Preserve the circulation area as authored negative space.
- **CAM_MAIN_ROUTE:** the clear route and process zone remain separated. The floor dash line, grated drain and equipment plinths still run as competing parallel bands; simplify their hierarchy and align their contrast. The nearest ceiling fixture faces approach clipping.
- **CAM_PROCESS:** the newly captioned controls and tank/dryer connection give more process cues. Several legends are too small at this scale, and the hose path crosses closed fitting volumes according to the audit. The neighboring machines still share simple cabinet and plinth construction.
- **CAM_REVERSE:** the cart, receiving gear, cleanup equipment and conveyor landing support the material-flow story. The two map ports look black in this isolated camera; treat them as the open interfaces they are, and use assembled-map context to assess what lies beyond rather than adding a false closure.
- **CAM_PINCH:** captioned crusher controls and the open jaw improve specificity. The pale reader hood is supported by two dark folded rear cantilever webs, visible around image x≈680 px and x≈900 px in the 960 px-wide frame. The separate dark members below the belt are conveyor supports, not additional reader posts. A central dark optical cradle/plate hangs beneath the rectangular hood. Refine that hood and sensor housing into a thinner formed bridge with a recessed optical bar while preserving the pair of rear supports, belt sightline and clearance.
- **CAM_MATERIAL:** the metal work surface, transparent guard, work cylinder and control function read clearly. The press remains a broad orange pedestal under a plate, and the new small captions do not change its silhouette. Form the ram/frame as a more legible load-bearing assembly and restrain the broad panel brightness.
- **CAM_ASSEMBLY:** bench, filter cabinet, screen/shelf and foreground controller create a service station. The pale foreground control face pulls strongly against the darker machinery; the right open port remains a large dark interface field. Keep the opening open and evaluate it in the actual connected map.
- **CAM_DISPATCH:** canister cart and physical wall task lamp are grounded and useful. The status/control plate has numerous fine labels and buttons that are small at distance; the large port is still black in the room-only view and the surrounding wall remains quiet.
- **CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER:** the ore receiver, diagonal slat conveyor and more defined open crusher mouth make the intake sequence clear. The conveyor support remains simple and generic; the new label text on the nearest black control plate is too small to help from this wide view.
- **CAM_WORK_NOOK:** the roster sheet and PV note add a plausible shift-work cue, while radio, mug and food stay in one compact cluster. A thin reddish working-edge polish extends past the timber top's right bounds; trim it flush with the tabletop or model a complete wrapped edge. Some paper text remains too pale at this distance.
- **CAM_HERO_DETAIL:** the signed tag and connected wire are restored, the PV wheel wear is subtle, and the vessel/hatch remain an effective hero. The dark dryer coupling reads as a capped stub; source inspection finds its solid flanges, nut and sleeve intersecting the hose bore. The hose route should pass through annular fittings with the centerline kept clear.

## Highest-impact remaining fixes

1. Rebuild the hex nut, neck sleeve and both new flanges as annular fittings with aligned through-bores. Recheck the full hose centerline and evaluated surface intersection in `CAM_HERO_DETAIL`/`CAM_PROCESS`; a support-contact PASS cannot substitute for the open fluid path.
2. Refine the sorter in `CAM_PINCH`: keep its two visible rear cantilever supports—the dark left and right webs from hood to belt—and form the pale roof into a thinner bridge with a recessed optical bar, visible cable route and small service access. Do not count the separate conveyor legs beneath the belt as reader supports; preserve the compact height and belt sightline.
3. Give the long conveyor and press more specific load-bearing construction: formed side rails, believable drive/tension hardware, guarded return path, and an exposed press frame/ram with contact wear localized at the working edges.
4. Tune label scale and value by view distance. Keep the detailed enclosure engravings for close work, but simplify the wide-view stage identifiers so they do not become tiny visual noise; reduce the dominant `02` wall mark and retain only useful, readable signage.
5. Trim the working-edge polish to the tabletop bounds in `CAM_WORK_NOOK` and tune the bright practical surfaces, while preserving the real light housings, open map ports and clean route negative space.
