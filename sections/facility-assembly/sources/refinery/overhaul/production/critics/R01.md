## Camera-specific pixel defects

- **CAM_ENTRY:** the large empty foreground slab occupies most of the lower frame; the equipment line reads as a row of box housings and exposed stands. The blank light-colored hanging panel near the center is visually ambiguous and breaks the intended process read.
- **CAM_MAIN_ROUTE:** the vessel, green casing and right-side press have distinct colors but share boxy construction and similar scale. Several identical dark machine bases and repeated control posts flatten hierarchy; substantial piping is hidden behind or terminates at the equipment without a clear process read.
- **CAM_PROCESS:** the green panel's small, low-contrast labels and repeated round controls are neither legible nor distinctive. The vessel's bolted disc is a broad flat grey plate; the surrounding bases merge into a dark band. Faint wall graphics compete with little but remain too faint to contribute.
- **CAM_REVERSE:** large empty floor dominates the near field. The personnel and mine thresholds read as almost-black rectangles; the receiving hopper and conveyor overlap the right side, while little worker or destination information occupies the far wall.
- **CAM_PINCH:** a large control panel projects into the foreground over the conveyor crossing, visually crowding the sorter throat. The overhead orange conveyor and supports obscure parts of the processing equipment; the view feels like a cluster of obstructions even though numerical route-obstruction checks pass.
- **CAM_MATERIAL:** the press glass appears pale and opaque, weakening the safety-guard read. The green controls float as a wide rectangular plate on thin legs, and the table is a generic slab/frame. The dark base cabinets at left have no readable operation or wear.
- **CAM_ASSEMBLY:** the assembly, dryer and inspection stations repeat the same rectangle-on-four-legs language. The black doorway at right becomes a void and crops against the worktable; workstation tops have little evidence of tools, parts or handling.
- **CAM_DISPATCH:** the doorway is a black void without a visible receiving context. The cylinder cart is the clearest dressing cluster, but the cupboard and blank right wall make dispatch feel under-authored; the cropped foreground inspection station competes with it.
- **CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER:** the high diagonal conveyor gives the line direction, but consumes the central silhouette and draws attention from the crusher; large foreground floor and bare west wall make the broad room feel under-dressed.
- **CAM_WORK_NOOK:** the mug/radio/food grouping is purposeful and worth keeping. The board is mostly blank at this distance and one paper has an empty black strip; the tabletop cluster looks arranged rather than naturally interrupted, and the tight crop loses its relationship to the production floor.
- **CAM_HERO_DETAIL:** the tank's smooth surface, identical gauge construction and large featureless bolted plate do not reveal what processing is happening. The equipment label is laid over the upper silhouette, and the pale/metallic pipes entering at frame right feel disconnected from the vessel because their destination is cropped.

## Highest-impact corrections

1. Redesign the crusher/sorter/processor/dryer/inspection silhouettes as distinct functional machines with purposeful supports, guards, access doors and visible material transitions. Remove the repeated box-on-stand pattern and choose sharper edge treatments based on folded plate, cast housing or tube construction.
2. Recompose wide views around the route and operation: reduce the visual weight of empty near-floor, separate the control panel from conveyor throats, and make openings show credible destination depth or readable threshold construction instead of black voids.
3. Make the pressure vessel communicate a process: replace the oversized blank disc with a legible access/drive/filter assembly, vary gauges/valves by purpose, and show believable pipe entry/exit. Give adjacent machines their own primary silhouette rather than relying on palette shifts.
4. Add a few specific maintenance traces at the actual work points—used service tools, a staged replacement part, a handled sample record, localized floor/hand-contact wear—so the broad cameras register an active staffed facility. Keep `CAM_WORK_NOOK`'s human cluster but integrate it with the production space.
5. Tune material/light response at gameplay distance: give enamel, bare metal, rubber, glass and floor visibly different response; make the safety glass read as glass; add selective wear; and retain practical-only light while easing the near-black doorway transitions and strengthening the intended focal hierarchy.
