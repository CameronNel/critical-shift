# R14 Blender technical audit

**Scope:** Read-only inspection of checkpoint `checkpoints/R14.blend`, its build and validation reports, `revision_R14.py`, evaluated geometry in Blender 5.2, and the completed eleven fixed-view renders. Checkpoint SHA-256 `2022da98a834d0e744b0e466854562ec35764a8349f02b9594c38af4f0009624` matches `build_R14.json` and `validation_R14.json`. I opened the checkpoint with auto-execution disabled and ran temporary diagnostics under `/tmp`; I did not save or edit the scene, source, or author scripts. I reviewed the relevant visible views, including `CAM_HERO_DETAIL`, `CAM_PINCH`, `CAM_MATERIAL`, `CAM_PROCESS`, `CAM_DISPATCH`, and `CAM_WORK_NOOK`. This is a technical review, not a 99/100 art acceptance or a runtime certification.

## Blocker: original sensor fasteners stop 1 mm short

`ART_Sorter_sensor_head_fasteners` has evaluated world bounds ending at **y=4.393283 m**. The replaced sensor frame's front bearing plane is at **y=4.394283 m**. The fastener mesh therefore stops **1.000 mm** short of the frame, leaving a visible physical gap at every original fastener contact. This is independently measurable in the evaluated meshes and is not covered by the frame's support checks. The R15 author change translating this fastener group **+1 mm in world Y** is the direct correction; R14 should retain this as a blocker until that candidate is checked.

## Protected boundaries, light and mesh checks

Validation is PASS with no issues: all **29 protected interfaces** are unchanged; world strength is **0**; all **21 lights are interior AREA fixtures** with actual fixture lenses; there are **zero unintended blocked aperture samples** (the center receiver sample is explicitly intended). It reports **201/201 new supports**, **133/133 inherited support-screen entries**, **38/38 print checks**, and **965/965 closed-mesh winding checks** passing. Independent target rays for the new optical, press, paper and caliper contacts above agree with those records. Evaluated triangle count is **420,822**, including visible mesh, curve and font geometry; route obstruction list is empty.

**Audit limits:** Contact rays, local bounds, selected mesh containment probes, closed-mesh winding and the validation samples do not prove exhaustive self-intersection or every possible buried volume. The R14 validation/build records do not claim a fresh-process cold-start equivalence check; R13's prior cold-start result does not establish R14 reproducibility. R14 has one actionable seating defect: the sensor fasteners require the measured 1 mm correction described above.
