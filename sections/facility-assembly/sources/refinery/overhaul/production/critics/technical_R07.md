## Normals and stored checks

I independently recomputed signed volume on evaluated visible authored meshes. **894 closed meshes** were evaluable and none had negative volume; 14 open or nonmanifold meshes were excluded from the signed-volume test. The stored validator reports **891/891** closed authored meshes PASS. For the repaired PV05 cylindrical body I compared custom loop normals against the radial direction at **320** body loops: maximum angular deviation is **0.025°**, mean **0.003°**. This confirms the analytic radial normals are aligned with the body rather than being pulled by profile joins.

Stored validation is PASS with 29 protected interfaces unchanged, **107/107** new support contacts PASS, **145/145** inherited support anchors PASS, zero sampled route obstructions and no listed issues. Independent support-gap ranges are **−2.000 to 4.283 mm** for new records and **−0.00006 to 2.500 mm** for inherited anchors; the press, dryer and thermal contacts cited above were checked individually. The four inherited cart-wheel anchors still resolve to their rails at under **0.00002 mm** gap and 0° deviation.

No material technical defect emerged in the scoped R07 geometry. The validator itself states the remaining boundaries: support anchors, inherited anchor/bounding-face checks, and closed-mesh winding do not exhaustively test self-intersection, buried volume or runtime collisions. This audit makes no visual-score, 99/100 art-acceptance or performance claim.
