## Replaced support structures

The compact sorter reader now has four continuous foot-to-upright-to-hood contacts. All four uprights meet their existing optical foot brackets at z=1.353 m and the folded hood at z=1.942 m. All eight explicit foot-to-upright and hood-to-upright support entries pass; inherited foot contacts also pass. The narrow hood spans x=−3.16 to −2.34 m, y=4.54 to 5.40 m, z=1.942 to 2.108 m. I found no unsupported gap in that reader stack.

The pressure-vessel bearing yokes have real concave cuts matching the vessel surface, and both yoke bases meet the original PV05 service plinth at z=0.400 m. The stored support checks report zero gap for both bases. The checkpoint contains baked mesh results (36 vertices/20 faces and 32 vertices/18 faces), with the concave seat contours present; the mesh winding checks pass. BVH surface overlap at the yoke/body seats is the expected coincident bearing interface. The source order does warrant cleanup: `plate()` adds bevel and weighted-normal modifiers before R08 appends and applies its Exact Boolean. This is the reported “Boolean not first modifier” stack warning. The cut geometry survived in the checkpoint, so I found no resulting seat defect, but build the boolean mesh before adding bevel/normal modifiers to keep future rebuilds deterministic and warning-free.

The new overhead fixture clamps meet the underside of `RF1 | I girder lower flange.002`; each clamp top is at z=4.5475 m, the flange underside. The suspension stays meet the clamps above and reflectors below. All fixture apertures and emitters clear the scene: I independently cast the same 5 points per light out to 150 mm and found **0 blocked rays in 105 samples**. The inspection-sample center ray terminates on the intended `Inspection_sample_fuel_retention_band.001` at 81.20 mm; its other four rays clear. All 21 lights remain in-room AREA lights with physical lens objects, within 5 mm of their lenses, and axis-to-lens-normal angle at or below 1.27°. World strength remains 0 and the validation lists no missing used images or libraries.

There are two small support details to finish in this fixture assembly. Each diffuser's top is at z=3.645 m and the suspended reflector's underside at z=3.646 m, leaving a **1 mm air gap**; the diffuser has only a `support_group` custom property, with no retainer or explicit support record. The second stay also touches its housing but has no support-registry contact record. The assembly is held by its other measured stay, so the main suspension path is sound, but add a lip/clip that physically captures each lens and register the complete support path.

The new hearing-defender mount lands on the actual `Crusher_southeast_service_mount`: the plate-to-casting support ray measures **0.284 mm**. The retaining hook and headband meet with **1 mm overlap**, forming a continuous support path for the ear defenders. The four cups/pads are positioned on the band; I found no detached hearing-protection root.

The signed service tag and its details are parented with world transforms preserved to the interactive `Processor_sealed_hatch`. The spring clip meets the hatch bridge dog with 1 mm overlap. However, the wire is physically disconnected at both ends: its upper tip stops **0.13 mm** below the clip and its lower tip stops **0.63 mm** above the tag paper. The tag is attached to the hatch transform but not connected to its clip by the modeled wire; extend the wire into both parts or add a closed loop/grommet. The “CHECKED”, shift, and approval-ink details sit **1.5–2.3 mm** in front of the paper face (paper front y=4.026 m; details extend from y=4.0235 to 4.0243 m). Move the lettering and approval mark onto the paper face or model them as a thin printed/decal layer.

## Two measured 1 mm load-path gaps

The PV ledge clearance is real. `Processor_service_ledge_top` has a flat underside at **z=0.826 m**; both replacement cantilever ribs top out at **z=0.825 m**. That leaves a **1.00 mm vertical air gap** between the worktop and each intended supporting rib. The rib-to-plinth contact itself is good (the rib penetrates the plinth by 1 mm), but the “PV supported service shelf” registry anchors are placed on the rib top, not the underside of the actual worktop. They therefore validate the target at zero gap without measuring the source worktop. Extend each rib or add a short physical cap/bridge that contacts the underside while preserving any intended clearance to nearby equipment; bind the check to the worktop geometry.

The replacement filter cabinet stack is good from floor to worktop: toe-to-floor, cabinet-to-toe and the old service worktop-to-cabinet all resolve at **0 mm**. The cabinet access sheet itself is **1.00 mm** off the cabinet front (sheet y=3.804–3.818 m; cabinet face y=3.819 m). Its support record passes within the 5 mm tolerance but no hinge, latch or mounting tab bridges this gap. Seat the sheet to the cabinet or add actual door hardware so it is not a free-floating panel.

These are concrete contact gaps rather than evidence of broad interpenetration. The validator's support tolerance admits both, and its anchors are not bound to the complete source mesh; do not treat those PASS entries as proof that the panels bear on their nominated targets.
