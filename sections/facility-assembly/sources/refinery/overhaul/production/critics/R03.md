## R02-to-R03 change assessment

**Improvements confirmed in pixels:**

- The orphaned ochre parts that protruded through the floor in R02 are absent across the completed R03 camera set. The slab reads cleanly again.
- Small practical fixtures are visibly attached above the two thresholds in `CAM_REVERSE`; the added local light reinforces the built doorway and reduces the sense of a bare opening.
- `CAM_REVERSE` has a more developed service/safety grouping near the receiving area. The addition helps purpose, though its small scale limits its storytelling value in the wide composition.
- `CAM_WORK_NOOK` shows additional ledger marks, but its page graphics remain too small and visually glitchy to read as useful paperwork.
- `CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER` reveals material in the cropped receiving hopper, adding a small but appropriate input-state cue.

**R02 defects that remain:**

- The cart remains foreground/aisle dressing in `CAM_ENTRY` and `CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER`; move it to a clearly parked service bay outside the primary route and composition.
- `CAM_PINCH` still gives most of its foreground to a large angled control panel, crowding the conveyor throat. Retain the ergonomic tilt while reducing its visual and spatial dominance.
- The central vessel still relies on a large featureless bolted disc, similar gauges and pipes cropped before their destination. The new green drum adds variety but does not yet establish a readable process mechanism.
- Doorway voids remain nearly black in `CAM_DISPATCH`, `CAM_ASSEMBLY` and the reverse view, even with added lintel fixtures.
- Broad views retain a substantial empty foreground slab and long bare areas between machinery. The new service/safety details are too small to address the large-scale balance or human presence.

## Highest-impact remaining corrections

1. Redesign the tank access and process interfaces so the hero machinery explains material and flow: a specific door/drive/filter arrangement, differentiated gauges and valves, and visible pipe entry/exit. Give the dryer and downstream machines distinct construction and support logic beyond color changes.
2. Park the maintenance cart beside the production line with a clear functional relationship to a service point, outside the walking route and foreground of entry/cross-room views. Reduce the tilted control panel's size or relocate it to clear the sorter throat.
3. Recompose the wide shots around the process and circulation. Fill selected negative spaces with purposeful work clusters and contact wear, and make door thresholds show credible depth or a readable receiving space rather than a black void.
4. Clean up the nook ledger graphics so pages read as intentional shift records at the work camera; add a few high-value work props in process/inspection views rather than relying on small detail that disappears at gameplay distance.
5. Add restrained localized wear and material response differences to the enamel, exposed metal, rubber, glass and concrete, preserving the current practical-only light setup and its improved threshold fixtures.
