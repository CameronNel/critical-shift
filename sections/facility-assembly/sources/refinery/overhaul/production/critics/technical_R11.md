## Repair required: visible product fittings obstruct the hose path

The R11 visible hose connection contains four capped solid fittings where the hose path needs through-bores:

- `RF1 | Product hose hex compression nut` is a capped six-sided cylinder centered at (2.506, 4.908, 1.449) m, 77 mm long and 103 mm nominal radius. It has no axial bore. On the evaluated hose mesh, **60 of 390 vertices lie inside its closed volume**, and its surface BVH overlaps the hose surface in **20 triangle pairs**.
- `RF1 | Product coupling neck sleeve` is also capped and solid (70 mm radius, about 29 mm long). **23/390 hose vertices lie inside** its closed volume and there are **48** hose/sleeve triangle-overlap pairs. Its nearest hose-surface sample is only **0.14 mm** away, consistent with a tight intersection rather than a clear through path.
- `RF1 | Processor product coupling` and `RF1 | Dryer product inlet flange` are new closed disks, not annuli. Their evaluated diameters are **292 mm** and **310 mm**, thicknesses **30 mm** and **35 mm**. A ray along each flange's world X axis through its center hits the solid flange face; **17/390 hose vertices lie inside each flange**, with **15** and **17** overlapping hose/flange triangle pairs respectively. By comparison, the original `Processor_product_nozzle_flange` has a clear center ray through its annular opening.

These are external fittings on the visible hose route, so their bores should remain open where the product line passes. Rebuild the sleeve/nut as hollow fittings and add aligned central openings to the two new flanges. Keep the hose centerline clear through the connected assembly. The present support checks establish external seating to the original nozzle/drum, but do not check these bores or detect the fitting/hose intersections.

It is **`RF1 | Nook timber working-edge polish`**, not a separate source bench part. Camera rays through render pixels (590, 405) and (595, 405) hit this object at world x=0.556 m and 0.535 m, y=−6.1331 m, z=0.9745–0.9767 m; rays at x=600 and beyond hit `South_right` instead.

The polish bounds are x=0.4785–0.5615 m, y=−6.1361…−6.1331 m, z=0.9685–0.9775 m. The tabletop begins at x=0.5800 m and ends at z=0.9800 m. Thus the polish is **18.5 mm outside the tabletop’s minimum X edge**, at the tabletop’s front Y edge and **2.5–11.5 mm below** its upper surface. It has no contact with the table. Clamping its X span to the tabletop, as planned for R12, addresses the visible fragment.
