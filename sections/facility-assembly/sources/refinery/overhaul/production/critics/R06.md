# Independent correction-cycle review — R06

**Reviewer:** independent GPT-6 Luna critic

**Scope:** all 11 completed R06 fixed views, compared with R05 and reviewed against the refinery brief, Critical Shift art direction, owner rubric, and Spawn quality-reference renders.

The room remains well below the ≥99-per-category and weighted ≥99 acceptance threshold.** No future R07 work receives credit.

## R05-to-R06 change assessment

**Improvements confirmed in R06 pixels and source evidence:**

- The inspection scanner is now visibly built into the bench assembly (`CAM_ASSEMBLY`). Source validation adds foot pads beneath both scanner cheeks; the 22.5 mm unregistered gap found in the R05 audit is replaced by supported cheek-to-pad and pad-to-worktop contacts.
- A dedicated sorter scan head and status bar make the scan operation more explicit (`CAM_ENTRY`, `CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER`). Its scale and camera occlusion need correction.
- The KEEP CLEAR stencil and circulation stripes remain in place, with the central route free of the old cart.
- The practical fixtures remain physically present, inside the module, and supported by named lenses; world strength remains zero.
- Numerical coverage improves: R06 validates 79 new supports, 157 inherited supports and 845 closed-mesh winding records, all passing.

**Defects that remain or appear:**

- The new sorter scan housing overwhelms `CAM_PINCH`. Its broad cream body hides the machine throat, belt and nearby clock/grade elements. The bright bar and “04” label clarify its role but do not justify its current mass and placement.
- `CAM_HERO_DETAIL` reveals broad, irregular dark shading on the orange pressure-vessel body, plus a side-collar gauge that remains too large relative to the other gauges. These effects reduce the finish of the central hero.
- New bright task-light faces are conspicuous diagonal shapes in the entry/process views. They are validated as physical, in-room fixtures but their supports and relationship to ceiling structure are hard to read in pixels.
- The independent R06 technical audit measures occlusion not caught by validation: most sampled rays from all four wall-service emitters hit their housings, three of five press-task rays hit the assembly crown, and the thermal cover hold-down/cover assembly lacks contact/support continuity.
- The black open-port read remains unresolved in `CAM_DISPATCH` and `CAM_ASSEMBLY`; keep the ports open, but provide readable inner returns and a practical source that shapes the threshold.
- The line still repeats box housings, flat controls and similar stands; the scanner adds a new object but does not fix the broader machine family language.

## Camera-by-camera defects

- **CAM_ENTRY:** aisle marking and removed cart keep the foreground readable. The small sorter scanner at the back is now identifiable, though the two tilted bright fixtures draw the eye upward from the process line.
- **CAM_MAIN_ROUTE:** the line remains easy to follow and floor stripes clarify the walk zone. One bright angled light and the repeated machine supports reduce hierarchy; the large drain/channel strip continues as a competing pattern.
- **CAM_PROCESS:** access hatch and green drum are legible, but bright task heads cross above the hero. Tank face remains smooth and the adjacent machinery is still mostly cabinets and exposed stands.
- **CAM_REVERSE:** the safety/work zone and KEEP CLEAR text are readable. Both connected openings are still black; the conveyor still forms a dominant diagonal across the wall and makes the reverse view feel sparse in its lower half.
- **CAM_PINCH:** the new SO-04 scanner housing fills the upper-right half of the image, obscures much of the sorter frame and crowds the crossing. Rework its scale and mount so it reads as a sensor over the belt instead of a large suspended cabinet.
- **CAM_MATERIAL:** the glass and control hierarchies remain useful. The control panel's large buttons are clear but the underlying table and panel still have minimal wear and generic supports.
- **CAM_ASSEMBLY:** the inspection scanner now looks supported and integrated with the workbench. The assembly has useful storage and fixtures, but the black open port and repeated box/leg construction remain visually dominant.
- **CAM_DISPATCH:** the cylinder cart and rejection station read, while the connected port remains almost entirely black despite a visible wall lamp. The downstream destination cannot be read in the standalone render.
- **CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER:** ore in the hopper, aisle lines and machine labels clarify flow. The left hopper/conveyor still dominates the silhouette and large bare wall/floor fields remain.
- **CAM_WORK_NOOK:** shift board graphics are cleaner and the mug/radio/food cluster remains coherent. Text and small details remain too subtle to carry into gameplay-distance views.
- **CAM_HERO_DETAIL:** the hatch is purposeful, but broad dark patches on the orange vessel surface look like irregular shading/artifacting; the side-collar gauge is oversized and appears attached to the vent assembly without a clear bracket.

## Highest-impact remaining fixes

1. Reduce the SO-04 scanner head to the smallest housing that can contain the sensor and status display, mount it on a legible support/guard, and check `CAM_PINCH`, `CAM_ENTRY` and `CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER` together so the scan function stays visible without hiding the belt throat.
2. Diagnose the tank shading in `CAM_HERO_DETAIL` at the material, normal and occluder level. Remove the blotchy dark patches while preserving vessel curvature and practical-light shadow; do not flatten the body by removing all form shading.
3. Remount or reduce the side-collar gauge so it reads as a specific instrument rather than an oversized disk on the exhaust. Keep gauges and controls distinct by function.
4. Make the visible task-light fixtures read as fixtures at gameplay distance: give each diffuser a clear housing, support/bracket and mounting relation to the ceiling/service structure, and tune its aim/intensity so it does not become a white diagonal focal competitor.
5. Keep open ports and all existing interface contracts intact while improving standalone threshold read with in-room jamb returns and practical light that illuminates local frame surfaces. Render the assembled map connection when evaluating beyond the threshold.
6. Open the wall-lamp lens apertures so area-light rays clear the housing, relocate the press-task source to a clear emitting aperture under the crown, and give the thermal cover visible hold-down contacts and a declared support path. Verify with emitted-ray samples and measured contact tests, then compare the same fixed views.
7. Replace repeated machine box/support language with authored primary silhouettes for crusher, sorter, dryer and inspection stages; add restrained material breakup and handling wear where workers contact machines, tools and the marked route.
