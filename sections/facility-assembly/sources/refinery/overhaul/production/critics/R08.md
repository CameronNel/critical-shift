## R07-to-R08 change assessment

**Improvements confirmed in the current R08 pixels/source:**

- The SO-04 reader is much shorter and more compact than R07. It remains an obvious scanner, but no longer overwhelms the belt/crossing in `CAM_PINCH`.
- The hero practicals now hang from the front ceiling girder on visible stems, rather than lighting the vessel from behind. The tank reads with more even form shading in `CAM_HERO_DETAIL`; the R07 cast-shadow blotches are materially reduced.
- The temperature gauge is smaller and the service tag adds authored worker activity at the PV hatch.
- The processor support has concave body-bearing yokes, the prior table frame is removed, and the ledge now reads as cantilevered. A dedicated filter cabinet replaces one repeated table-like storage stand.
- The press's sharper console corners and cast hydraulic base/sole make its support construction more convincing (`CAM_MATERIAL`, `CAM_ASSEMBLY`).
- The dryer saddle/retention arrangement has a continuous measured support path. New physical-light aperture ray checks cover every in-room fixture.

**Defects that remain or appear:**

- The broad machine language still repeats: cabinets with green control plates, simple stands and mostly pristine planes. R08 has repaired specific high-value assets without bringing every processing stage to a comparable shape and material standard.
- The scanner hood still occupies a significant part of `CAM_PINCH`; its stanchions and cream shell draw more attention than the material passing through it.
- The route in `CAM_MAIN_ROUTE` is less explicit than `CAM_ENTRY`: the foreground remains a broad unmarked slab, and the drain edge, machine supports and route marking form competing parallel lines.
- Open ports remain black in standalone views even with local fixtures, although preserving the open connection is correct for the module contract.
- The independent audit found multiple small but real gaps/contact defects that the current support tolerance admits: PV ledge ribs 1 mm below the worktop, filter access sheet 1 mm off the cabinet face, diffuser lenses with 1 mm air gaps and no retainer records, and a service-tag wire physically disconnected at both ends. Tag writing and approval marks also sit 1.5–2.3 mm off the paper face.
- The source retains a Boolean modifier-order warning before bevel/weighted-normal modifiers. Current baked geometry is correct, but the build source should be reordered for reliable rebuilds.

## Camera-by-camera defects

- **CAM_ENTRY:** the process row is calmer than R07 and the front-girder lamp stems are clear. The central floor remains mostly blank despite the KEEP CLEAR stencil; the two pendants are visually bright enough to pull attention from the row.
- **CAM_MAIN_ROUTE:** task lights and process supports are better organized. The lower foreground is a wide quiet slab; drain grating, aisle lines and dark machine bases compete as parallel patterns.
- **CAM_PROCESS:** the PV body/yokes/ledge and green filter unit have stronger construction. The hose ends and downstream drum still crop tightly; the panel text remains too small for gameplay reading.
- **CAM_REVERSE:** the route and central safety zone remain clear, but the two open doorways are almost black and the diagonal conveyor continues to dominate the wall.
- **CAM_PINCH:** the reader is much better scaled than R07, but its large hood and four posts still crowd the transfer. The control panel and beam support also take up a large foreground area.
- **CAM_MATERIAL:** the cast press base and sharper console corners read well. The broad tabletop and press guard remain very clean; the worktop-to-rib support gap is not visible at this distance but is measured in the source audit.
- **CAM_ASSEMBLY:** the new filter cabinet and reduced table repetition help. The tall black right-hand opening remains a visual void; the access-sheet gap and diffuser retention gaps are technical defects in this work area.
- **CAM_DISPATCH:** the process console and cylinders are readable, but the black port still occupies much of the background and lacks visible destination context in the standalone image.
- **CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER:** the ore load, conveyor and sorter give a clear input/transfer chain. The hopper/conveyor still consume the left silhouette, and the upper wall remains quiet and under-detailed.
- **CAM_WORK_NOOK:** the shift board is clean and purposeful; radio, mug, food and sample detail are coherent but concentrated in a small nook. Broad worker presence remains limited.
- **CAM_HERO_DETAIL:** front practicals reduce shadow blotches; the hatch, smaller temperature instrument and signed tag form a stronger hero close-up. Tag lettering is slightly floated from the paper face and the wire ends do not physically connect according to the audit; these are small but real finish defects.

## Highest-impact remaining fixes

1. Give the reader hood more negative space in `CAM_PINCH`: reduce its shell depth/width further or place the structure higher on a slender, legible support while preserving sensor coverage and required route clearance.
2. Close the measured 1 mm support gaps on the PV service ledge ribs and filter access sheet. Capture each diffuser with a physical clip/lip and include it in the support registry. Extend the service-tag wire into its clip and paper loop, and seat the printed marks directly on the paper face.
3. Reorder the press base's Boolean work before adding bevel/normal modifiers in source so clean rebuilds do not rely on baked mesh results or modifier warnings.
4. Extend the R08's improved shape/material treatment to the crusher, hopper, dryer and sorter. Replace repeated square panel/leg structures with distinct guarded frames, service faces, and process-specific supports; add restrained wear at handles, controls and material transfer points.
5. Refine route graphics into one quiet, consistent aisle system; keep the KEEP CLEAR cue while reducing competition with the high-contrast drain and machinery base line. Use a meaningful localized work cluster where the large main-route floor is otherwise empty.
6. Preserve the module's open map ports, but use the assembled-map views to judge what lies beyond them. In standalone views, strengthen inner jamb and floor-return readability with the already present in-room practical fixtures, without closing the ports or adding external/world illumination.
