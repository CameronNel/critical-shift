## R01-to-R02 change assessment

**Visible improvements:**

- The replacement dryer shell creates a clear horizontal drum silhouette where R01 showed a more cabinet-like block (`CAM_ENTRY`, `CAM_MAIN_ROUTE`, `CAM_PROCESS`, `CAM_ASSEMBLY`). This is the most meaningful improvement to process-equipment differentiation.
- Controls now face the operator more directly. The ergonomic intent reads in medium views, although the wide control panel still takes too much foreground space in `CAM_PINCH`.
- The press glazing is more transparent and gives a better safety-guard read in `CAM_MATERIAL`.
- The wheeled service cart communicates maintenance activity more directly than R01's largely empty floor, though placement needs work.
- The dark gridded channel edge establishes a service/drain line and adds a useful floor construction cue in `CAM_MAIN_ROUTE` and `CAM_PROCESS`.

**Regressions / defects:**

- **Visible orphaned floor pieces:** small ochre pegs protrude from the slab in `CAM_ENTRY`, `CAM_MAIN_ROUTE`, `CAM_REVERSE` and `CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER`. They look like debris or misaligned hazard pins, not purposeful floor hardware. This defect appears in multiple fixed views and is a technical regression even though the automated validation lists no issue.
- **Cart placement:** `CAM_ENTRY` and `CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER` show the service cart in the open foreground, where it distracts from the main process line and suggests an aisle obstacle. `CAM_REVERSE` places it at the far edge, where it is less intrusive.
- **Pinch composition:** the large tilted control panel in `CAM_PINCH` is more legible as an operator-facing control, but its increased prominence worsens the close-up crowding against the conveyor crossing.
- The new drain/channel detail is visually useful at distance, but its dark repeated grate can become a competing high-contrast stripe beneath the machine line rather than an integrated drain in some framings.

## Remaining high-impact fixes

1. Remove or correctly reattach the orphaned ochre dryer-door details. Recheck all eleven cameras for any detached pieces after shell replacements; the R02 images show the floor artifacts despite validation passing.
2. Relocate the service cart to a clearly parked service bay beside the process line, outside the clear primary route and the foreground composition of `CAM_ENTRY` / `CAM_MINE_TO_CRUSHER`.
3. Reduce the size or move the tilted panel in `CAM_PINCH`; keep the operator-facing angle while restoring breathing room around the conveyor throat and the processing silhouette.
4. Continue distinct functional shaping for the pressure vessel and downstream units. Give the vessel a readable access/drive/filter assembly in place of its large blank plate, make piping destinations visible, and vary control types by machine function.
5. Extend purposeful worker and maintenance traces beyond the nook and cart, then refine threshold depth, floor response and selective wear so broad views convey an active, maintained facility rather than clean display equipment.
