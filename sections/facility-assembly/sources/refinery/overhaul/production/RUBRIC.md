# Owner's refinery acceptance rubric

Independent GPT-6 Luna scores each category 0–100. Final acceptance requires **at least 99 in every category**, no critical veto, all mandatory views passing, four full correction cycles after initial visual completion, final two cycles stable, and fresh-process validation. User thresholds supersede older90/93 rules. Never invent or round scores.

Categories: route readability (weight20%), art direction and silhouettes (20%), hero process equipment (15%), materials and anti-plastic quality (15%), practical lighting and atmosphere (10%), purposeful dressing and worker storytelling (10%), technical cleanliness and reproducibility (10%). Weighted overall must also reach99. Technical scores require numerical/source evidence, not images alone.

Automatic vetoes: primitive/blockout dominance; generic glossy material response; photoreal surface noise; excessive text/screens; arbitrary clutter; floating dressing; blocked routes; changed module interfaces; any unmotivated light or nonzero world illumination.

Style-slice gate before full expansion: average>=8/10 with no veto across authored hero shape, architecture, materials, practical lighting and a visible work/dressing cluster. This allows expansion; it does not waive the final99 requirement.
