## Non-blocking refinements to carry forward

- `DETAIL_OCRU`, main pad: its broad center is still very regular. One restrained compression change at an edge and slightly clearer soft thickness would improve tactile quality. Do not add dense wrinkle noise or disturb its clean gameplay silhouette.
- `HERO_OCRU`, support guides: repeated projecting stages are visually chunky. A future refinement can tune their profile and show an even cleaner guide/actuator relationship. The present load path is already readable; this is no longer the stacked-block veto.
- `DETAIL_SUPPLIES`, linen and wrapped packages: retain the new fold hierarchy. A small amount of corner tension/compression and material-specific use wear would strengthen the neglected-clinic story. The current broad forms already identify their purpose.
- Final red view: keep watching upholstery/deck value separation during expansion. Adding more red emissive cues or increasing the existing light would risk the former material collapse. The current visible carriage/rail distinction is adequate.

The closed object below the bed remains subordinate and has no exposed body content. Its edge catches more light than previously, but it is still low within the mechanism composition. The separate barely-visible note cannot be reliably identified from this fixed set; do not brighten or enlarge it merely for review. These are not expansion blockers on the available evidence.

Proceed with expansion using this OCRU / bench construction and material language while preserving the room footprint, interfaces, single red focal practical and dim warm clinical work zone. Full-room acceptance still requires the remaining fixed views, layout/support-contact checks and the existing production protocol; this report supplies only the style-slice decision.
