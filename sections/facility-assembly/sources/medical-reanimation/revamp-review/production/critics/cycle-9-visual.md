## Remaining defects

1. **Sink construction — HERO_WASH / HERO_SUPPLIES.** The basin remains a shallow grey box with a tube-like tap. Its folded rim is readable, but the bowl/drain construction is weak. Refine those primary/secondary forms inside the existing footprint.
2. **Pad surfaces — HERO_CART / DETAIL_OCRU / HERO_RECOVERY.** Several cushions are smooth slabs with limited broad compression or seam character. A few restrained folds, compression cues and local wear would improve tactile quality without texture noise.
3. **Secondary shadows — HERO_DECON / HERO_CABINET / HERO_RESERVE / PINCH.** Hose connections, stock and reserve interiors are dark enough that material separation is weak. Preserve current readability; any refinement should use a restrained existing accent/bounce or broad material value, with the red bed and warm supply practical retaining hierarchy.
4. **Physical neglect — HERO_WASH / WALL_EAST / WALL_WEST / DETAIL_SUPPLIES.** Poster/log storytelling exceeds equipment wear. Some repair patches and floor marks are flat polygon shapes; counter scratches are sparse straight lines. Refine a few plausible contact/repair areas and stain boundaries while keeping quiet walls and route space.

None is a critical visual blocker in this set. No dominant generic low-poly, repeated bevelled-box, uniform plastic, flat-lighting, excessive-signage, random-clutter or photoreal-noise veto is present. The sink and pads are localized weaknesses rather than the dominant room language.
