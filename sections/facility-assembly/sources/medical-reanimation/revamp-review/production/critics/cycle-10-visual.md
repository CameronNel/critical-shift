## Remaining defects

These are nonblocking observations, not critical failures.

1. **Secondary shadow compression:** `HERO_DECON`, `HERO_CABINET` and `PINCH` lose some upper fitting and interior-stock detail into black. A faint local grazing response or small value separation could preserve that construction without lifting the whole room or competing with the bed. No geometry or light measurement was performed.
2. **Red spill compresses material nuance:** `HERO_OCRU` and `DETAIL_OCRU` reduce cushion-versus-metal color separation. Slightly more muted vinyl shading and neutral carriage response could retain the red practical while improving tactile identity. This is a pixel observation, not a shader diagnosis.
3. **Orderly secondary work areas:** `HERO_SUPPLIES`, `DETAIL_SUPPLIES` and `HERO_CARTRIDGES` retain a clean arrangement beside the darker story cues. Optional localized evidence of interrupted use could deepen neglect while preserving functional grouping and negative space.
