# Independent full-room review — Electrical Room R1

**Decision: Not accepted.** I inspected the actual pixels from all fourteen inherited fixed cameras in `renders/R1/`, together with the R1 manifest and objective validation report. The room has a strong, readable electrical-equipment vocabulary and the bench slice's improvements carry into the full module. It still misses the required **>98/100 in every category**. Spatial presentation, construction consistency, material/lighting character and purposeful dressing have visible deficits; strengths in hero machinery and the bounded technical checks do not offset them.

Scores use the reworked spawn room as 100. The spawn reference's defining traits are clear circulation, differentiated assembled forms, distinct material families, visible practical light hierarchy, and restrained but convincing evidence of people working. These scores are whole-room R1 evidence only.

## View-specific correction targets

1. **C01_Entry, C03_Reverse, C04_Route, W01_Turbine_Return, W02_Waste_Approach:** The portal opening is near-black across most of its area, including in the dedicated W02 head-on view. Give the threshold a visible continuation into the adjoining environment or a designed, readable light transition, and retain the frame/reveal depth. Do not leave the fixed-camera evidence dominated by a featureless void. The actual assembled-map corridor proof can establish the neighboring context; judge the result from the unchanged portal cameras.
2. **C01_Entry, C03_Reverse, W03_Reserve_Return:** Keep the 2.4 m aisle open, but bring the broad floor and far wall into the visual composition with restrained route/use cues tied to the equipment and portal thresholds. At present, central circulation reads as an oversized empty field. Add authored wayfinding, localized floor wear or threshold treatment at the margins rather than placing arbitrary props in the protected route.
3. **C02_Hero, W04_Switchgear_Approach:** Reduce the repeated sameness across the bank faces. Preserve the credible shared cabinet system while giving selected bays a distinct panel state, actual replacement history or function-specific details. The little rust/chip marks repeat in similar clusters around handles and lower edges; vary them according to touch, service and runoff or remove the repetitive pattern.
4. **C07_Reserve_Bay, W03_Reserve_Return:** Lift the internal reserve-rack readability and give the open compartments clearer structural/material separation. The three nearly identical rack faces and dark shelves presently merge at overview distance. Keep recess shadows, but allow the repeated rails/insulators and reserve contents to read as deliberately built storage.
5. **C10_Transfer:** Separate the lower labels from the horizontal bar that crosses them. The “COOLING,” “HOLD” and “MEDICAL” labels are visibly obscured. Adjust the cassette/label relationship so wording remains legible from this saved camera while preserving physical hierarchy among handles, selector and indicators.
6. **C09_Workbench, W03_Reserve_Return:** The high conduit run's left termination is difficult to read in C09 and its span appears as a thin line across the wall; make its wall entry/termination and attachment legible from both views. The bench itself now meets the slice style-proof standard; this is a full-room integration detail, not a request to rework the bench.
7. **C01/C03/C04/C09/W03:** Differentiate the large wall, floor and equipment fields with controlled, broad material response and use history. Keep quiet areas, but introduce more than hue-only blocks and repeated specks. The reference relies on clear tactile differences, localized history and practical light—not blanket grunge or extra signage.

The R1 bench is a real improvement and the restrained industrial palette is appropriate. The overall module has good machinery concepts, useful negative space and a technically clean bounded report, but the dark portals, empty broad fields, repeated cabinet/rack treatment, transfer-label collision and weak wide-room use story are concrete blockers to the required visual standard. Re-review the corrected source through these same fourteen cameras and any assembled-map context views, then reassess independently; none of the planned corrections is credited in these R1 scores.
