## View-specific remaining blockers

1. **W01_Turbine_Return and W02_Waste_Approach:** The wired grid is visible in each leaf's inset panel, but its surface reads very dark and opaque in these full-resolution pixels; there is no convincing through-glass depth/reflection cue. Improve the glass transmission and seat the wire behind the glass surface so the inserts read as wired vision panels. Preserve the checked outer leaf bounds and existing camera poses. The open interface itself is resolved in integrated EI_W01/EI_W02; do not add a fake standalone backdrop.
2. **C01_Entry and C04_Route (also EI_C04_Route):** The thin blue diagonal line/sliver at the upper-right ceiling/wall return has no legible functional connection and is unlike the surrounding routed services. Determine whether it is geometry or a render/edge artifact; remove or resolve it so the saved route view has no unexplained luminous line.
3. **C01_Entry and C04_Route:** The dark capped cylinder below the two hanging loops at the far-right wall sits directly on the floor without an obvious support or relationship. Present it in a cradle/strap with its purpose readable from these overview views, or clear it from the floor. Do not count an unseen inventory description as visual evidence.

- **Conduit termination:** Resolved. C09 shows the high service run entering a visible wall box at left and reaching the small display at right. It reads as a routed wall service rather than the open-ended line noted in R1.
- **Cabinet wear/material repetition:** Improved. C02/W04 show fewer repeated rust/chip clusters and more panel-to-panel variation. The bank remains modular, which is appropriate for switchgear; its repetition is not by itself a blocker.
- **South-wall use evidence:** Improved. C03/C06 show records, rescue equipment and a mounted extinguisher with useful support and placement. The test station remains a focused work cluster in C08/C09.

The room is substantially stronger than R1 and demonstrates that the expansion has carried the validated style slice into the wider module. The connected thresholds, practical lighting, machinery, bench and reserve forms all read well. The low-transmission grid panels and unsupported floor cylinder are specific visual blockers to 99 in their categories, and the upper-right line in the route camera remains unexplained. R2 therefore does not meet the unchanged requirement that every final category exceed 98/100. These scores are based on the formal 14-view batch and five integrated context images; any later diagnostic images can add evidence but do not erase these visible R2 pixels.
