**Decision:** Hold expansion. The repaired bay is a substantial improvement over its baseline and has a usable foundation, but it does not yet meet the spawn-room reference standard or the required >98/100 style gate in every category. This is a slice-only visual review; it does not assess the unbuilt room or technical validation.

## Visible corrections before expansion

1. **Give the test components a readable identity.** In C09, the two cream horizontal cylinders on dark bases (center of worktop) and the smaller group behind them have smooth, nearly featureless bodies; their copper-colored end posts do not explain their function. Add the minimum construction cues that distinguish the intended parts from generic cylinders—ends, bands/spacers, terminals or other appropriate connection geometry—and check that the silhouette reads at C09 distance. Do not rely on a label to identify them.
2. **Make the glove cluster unmistakably insulating gloves.** In C08, the pale forms just behind the two larger components read as an overlapping stack of short rounded pieces. Establish a cuff opening, paired glove shape and broad folded fabric/rubber volume, with contact to the bench, so they read as PPE rather than loose parts.
3. **Separate the warm material families.** At C08, the brown worktop and reddish-brown tool board occupy large adjacent fields with similar warm hue. Shift their value, hue or roughness response enough to make the phenolic top distinct from the coated tool board under the existing light; preserve the restrained industrial palette. Add only broad, localized use variation to these surfaces and the large wall fields, not uniform noise.
4. **Improve the wall's construction read.** The rear upper beige panels and lower dark panels are dominated by long, thin seam lines. Add a restrained but actual reveal/return or backing depth at a representative panel joint and around the tool-board mounting, so the relationship reads as assembled from C09. Keep quiet wall area and do not turn every joint into a black outline.
5. **Make the lighting hierarchy legible across both views.** Keep the task lamp as a local key, reduce the pervasive amber tint on the wall and metal, and introduce a clearer neutral/cool fill or secondary practical relationship. Retain the soft shadows and visible grounding; ensure C08 includes the lamp head or enough context to judge its light source.
6. **Add one or two specific signs of recent work.** C08 has a few small marks near the front edge, but they are difficult to read as contact wear; the arranged contents otherwise look freshly staged. Make a small, plausible handling trace around the active work area (for example, localized wear at tool contact or a used lead/component placed in relation to the open test case). Keep the rest tidy and quiet; avoid arbitrary debris.

The C09 baseline's pale board, simple hooks, uniform tan worktop and visually bare frame have been materially improved: the replacement has a more specific bench, useful work light, distinct mat and fuller repair context. That progress is real, but the current slice still falls short of the calibrated spawn reference in construction specificity, cool/warm control and lived-in purposeful evidence. Re-render the same C08/C09 cameras after correction and reassess this gate before expanding the room.
