## 1. Executive Summary

The entire **Compliance Dock** (13.6 × 15.8 m, 214.88 m² net clear interior area) for *Critical Shift* has been fully modeled, articulated, lit, textured, and verified from factory-empty Blender.

The Compliance Dock acts as the regulatory checkpoint and intake barrier of the subterranean facility. Here, arriving officers and personnel undergo biometric screening, cargo and waste containers pass through lead-shielded X-ray inspection, transit manifests and contraband are processed through a fortified check-in hatch, and suspicious or biohazardous evidence (including the concealed H1 specimen trolley and H2 evidence cabinet) are isolated in a secluded support alcove.

The build strictly adheres to the project's art direction: grounded, stylized, Valorant-influenced semi-realism with authoritative industrial machinery, rhythmic architectural articulation, and a disciplined palette consisting of warm off-white drywall, dark charcoal structural framing, gunmetal equipment, deep burgundy compliance livery, and vivid amber safety accents. **Strictly NO teal, cyan, or turquoise decoration is present.**
