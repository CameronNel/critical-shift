# F13 Luna contact construction preflight

**Result: no definite new contact or aperture defect found in the bounded surface review.** This is a technical geometry/contact review only. It assigns no art score, rubric score, overall score, or acceptance decision.

I compared root’s proposed f13 backing, clips, scanner edges and seams, and P2 guide shoes against the frozen f12 native in Blender 5.2.2 LTS. The native hash was `73b85e9a84b577d2059a07371abd366666536800eb45c9e56a7d7788a21b77a3` before and after the probe. The independent f12 report covers all 1,077 inherited objects and records no protected matrix changes. I used direct rays into the evaluated actual f12 support surfaces; no full authoring build ran to completion, no native was saved, and no image or art quality was reviewed.

The reassurance notice’s actual face ends at Y=3.5915 m. The proposed backing begins at Y=3.5915 m, giving a shared face. The read-only rays hit the actual f12 joined notice island. The proposed backing is analytically bounded from the repair code at Y=3.5915–3.5995 m; its min-Y face equals the measured notice max-Y face at Y=3.5915 m. The separate ray checks are target-surface evidence, while the proposed backing contact is a face-plane comparison. The actual glazing front is Y=3.6074998 m; both clip-position rays hit it there at Y=3.6074998 m against the analytical proposed clip segment at Y=3.6075 m. The folded clip return meets the analytically bounded backing face at Y=3.5995 m. Both text bounds remain inside the notice/backing area, with the glyph plane 0.08 mm in front of the notice. These measurements close the former floating-notice gap.

For the scanner, the planned four edge backs and six horizontal seams end at Y=7.180 m. All 20 rail rays and 30 seam rays hit the actual column rear skin at Y=7.1799998 m; the largest measured plane offset is below 0.2 micrometres. The seam samples cover both ends and quarter points of their proposed 150 mm widths at all three seam heights. The prior chamfer separation is removed while the parts stay on the straight rear skin.

Each P2 shoe’s expected bounds use its retained center at X=±2.23 m, with Y=15.710–15.728 m and Z centers 0.20/3.20 m. The four footprints remain within X=±2.285 m, leaving 15 mm to the protected ±2.30 m aperture edge. Direct rays at the central bearing band hit the actual steel closure at Y=15.7279997 m. Comparing those hits with the proposed heel at Y=15.728 m gives sub-micrometre plane rounding and a distinct exposed front plane at Y=15.710 m. The steel closure itself is 46 mm wide against the 110 mm shoe heel, so the outer portion of each heel overhangs that patch. Static contact proves a compression interface at the measured strip; it does not establish retention under shear/tension, load rating, gate motion, or runtime collision. I found no measured aperture breach or loss of contact in this repair.
