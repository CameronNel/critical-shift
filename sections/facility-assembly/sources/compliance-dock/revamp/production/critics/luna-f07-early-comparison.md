# f07 early visual comparison — C01–C05

This is an informal pixel comparison of the five currently available f07 renders against the matching f06 renders. It does not score or accept the room. No builder claims or technical state were used as visual evidence.

## Frame-by-frame findings

- **C01_ENTRY — improved, with a contrast regression.** Thin light-metal divisions/edge strips on the scanner uprights give the frame more assembly logic than the broad uninterrupted blue faces in f06. The nearby cart-gate faces have shifted to charcoal/gray; their panel boundaries now merge into the black aperture and dark support, so the gate is less distinct at this distance. The rear arrival gate also loses much of the blue rail pattern that helped it read in f06.
- **C02_HERO_DOCK — improved, with a contrast regression.** The scanner’s vertical edge divisions are visible and make its posts feel less like single slabs. New/revised dark faces on the bypass gate still blend into the voids around them, and the door/gate behind the arch has lost its bright blue graphic structure. The major room composition and route are unchanged.
- **C03_CHECKIN_COUNTER — visually unchanged.** The counter, transaction objects, sign, rear louver and lighting appear the same in the available renders. The handoff remains hard to understand at room scale; the scene still reads as a broad counter with a few small loose items.
- **C04_SCANNER_APPROACH — improved, but too low contrast.** The scanner posts now have narrow metallic divisions and panel breaks. The large adjacent gate panel also has a more defined inset outline. Those details are barely legible because the new surfaces are dark and sit against other dark surfaces. The gate and wall behind the scanner remain very subdued.
- **C05_CONVEYOR_LEAD_TUNNEL — improved construction, darker focal read.** The tunnel now has a clearer external portal/frame and the inspected case has visible top latches, both useful construction cues absent in f06. However, the broad charcoal frame dominates around an almost black opening; the blue housing and inner opening lose separation. The case remains a simple large cuboid with a broad flat red face, and its label still reads as tiny marks rather than a clear material/handling cue.

## Three highest-impact refinements

1. **Restore tonal separation around the moving gate and scanner panels.** Keep every opening, panel envelope and placement fixed. Use a clearer three-part value/material grouping for structure, moving leaves and recessed aperture: a restrained mid-value painted-metal gate face, lighter edge/return detail, and a dark but visible seal/recess. The new seams should read at C01/C02/C04 distance without turning into bright pinstripes. Recover enough contrast on the rear gate's blue rails for its paired leaves to read in C01/C02.
2. **Light the cargo portal as a built inspection unit.** Keep the current portal, aperture and conveyor dimensions. Separate the outer frame from the tunnel interior with a less uniformly dark material response, visible inner returns/seals and a restrained local light that reaches the aperture lip and first roller section. Preserve the deep tunnel interior; expose just enough surface to show its depth and fabricated layers. The new frame should support the opening instead of visually swallowing it.
3. **Finish the test case as a handled object and carry that material language through the existing check-in props.** Keep the crate envelope and counter arrangement unchanged. Give the crate face a subtle material/value break, corner protection or shallow formed edge, readable closure hardware and localized handling wear; enlarge/reposition no parts. The new latches are a good start but currently small against the blank case. At check-in, where no visible f07 change occurred, clarify the existing paper/tray/stamp relationship through material contrast and a single deliberate handoff grouping. Use light and roughness/value differences before adding more small props.
