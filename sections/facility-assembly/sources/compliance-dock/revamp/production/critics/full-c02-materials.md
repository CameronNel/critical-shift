## Category 4 — 94/100

The main material families are distinguishable. The trolley cloth is matte, thick and softly draped, with restrained weave; the steel counter and machinery retain controlled highlights; bare rollers and small hardware have a brighter response; dark wheels read as rubber; the office panes soften detail and reflect light as glazing. Plaster grain resolves in DETAIL_CHECKIN. Small warm counter accents supply the limited wood response.

Wear is authored and localized: counter rail scratches, evidence-storage contact marks and route-paint wear. There is no blanket photographic grunge, universal edge damage or scene-wide plastic shine. Wide views keep surface noise quiet, consistent with the approved spawn craft.

The lowest-margin areas are the uniform finish of large blue steel faces, the relatively plain chair pads and the coarse trolley checker. In full-cycle-02-uv/HERO_TROLLEY the checks appear to carry over the drape and rounded end without an obvious abrupt tear in this angle, but broad nearly uniform cells on the hanging face make small distortion and density consistency hard to judge. The beauty weave has no conspicuous streaking. This is a limitation of rendered evidence, not a native UV certificate.

## Category 6 — 94/100

Warm institutional off-white/plaster, navy/charcoal, orange staff doors and signs, yellow route paint and drab cloth connect to the approved spawn palette. The large blue inspection surfaces are brighter than the surrounding room but serve a coherent functional mass. PLAYER_REVERSE and PLAYER_PINCH show that the same family becomes quieter away from the hero light pool; the neutral diagnostics also temper the apparent brightness.

The cool equipment and restrained warm accents support the bleak dock mood without neon cyan, colour confetti or a glossy sci-fi palette. Blue coverage remains the nearest colour margin; keep secondary services subdued if future detail is added.
