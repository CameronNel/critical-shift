## Blocking technical defect

The fresh native report identifies **G1-01, critical**: two pressed bays on leaf 1 of the cart gate have exposed faces exactly coplanar with the parent panel and penetrate about 5.000114 mm into it. The 40 mm parent panel front is at Y=6.980000019 m; both bay fronts are at the same Y and their rears are at Y=6.985000134 m. The coincident surfaces total 0.443720505 m². The resulting black fields appear in beauty views and persist in the supplemental UV checker. The technical report associates the finding with the native geometry and its measured interfaces; it is a critical construction defect, not an art-direction interpretation.

Repair both bays from the measured front face of their own panel, seat captive fixings against the corrected bay fronts, preserve inherited matrices, then remeasure and rerender the affected interfaces. Required review views include beauty, neutral and UV diagnostics.

The native critic also found that six inherited P2, D2 and D1 support witnesses hit their target floor without proving actual contact with the assembly within 5 mm. This is a registration deficiency; the report says the actual frame jambs reach the floor and does not conclude that the doors float. The inherited G1 pedestal also intrudes about 60 mm into the nominal R1 ±0.6 m straight reservation, matching the selected-original baseline; neither an unobstructed nominal 1.2 m route nor an opened-door/cart runtime route is certified.
