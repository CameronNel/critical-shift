# Full cycle 01 — independent materials / anti-plastic review

Frozen source: `f02`, SHA-256 `dc608cae0a42303e614f2db3dc0cd9d50e4366dc738ac57c35957c6df34e0331` (source identity supplied by primary author). Review is based on actual opened 1067 × 600 images, without source-code inspection, previous verdicts or implementation credit.

**Verdict: FAIL. Category 4: 81/100. Category 6: 94/100.** Each category must be strictly greater than 93. The palette clears its assigned bar; tactile material and UV discipline do not. No dominant generic-gloss/plastic or palette visual veto was observed. The fragmented cover checker is a material-validation blocker. This review does not certify technical cleanliness or the other six categories.

The navy, warm cream, rust-orange doors/signs and restrained hazard yellow belong to the approved spawn family. Cool blue equipment and darker values make the dock appropriately less welcoming. Status red/green and screen green stay small and functional. The palette should be retained through the repairs; increased saturation or extra coloured labels would weaken it.

Material craft is uneven. Bare counter hardware, silver trim and the trolley pole catch tighter highlights than the broad painted bodies. Paper reads matte and separate from its tray. Glass has a recognisable hazy, reflective response rather than sharing the wall response. The wall has visible plaster-like microrelief in the very close foreground of `HERO_SCANNER`. There is no blanket photographic grunge or scratch noise. These are real strengths, but they do not establish tactile identity across the whole room at ordinary camera distance.

The trolley cover is the clearest failure. In the corrected neutral view it reads as a sequence of stiff, smooth humps with a nearly planar skirt; the straps do not create convincing cloth compression or hanging folds. In the corrected evidence checker view, that same cover breaks into angular triangular fragments with abrupt orientation and scale changes. The storage door broad faces have orderly checker cells, so this is a local defect rather than a reason to reject all mapping. A plain colour in the beauty view conceals the cover's mapping problem; it does not satisfy UV discipline.

In `C07_OFFICE_INTERIOR` and its neutral version, the chair pads, CRT housing and tabletop are predominantly smooth, broad-shaded surfaces. Their identities depend heavily on shape and colour. The seat/back pads need a clearer soft upholstery or vinyl response against the rigid molded plastic and painted support. The cargo tunnel, utility boxes and evidence doors also remain very even in colour and finish, with little visible causal contact wear. Matte roughness prevents shiny toys, but it does not by itself produce the craft demonstrated by the spawn reference.

The floor and yellow route paint are exceptionally pristine across `C01_ENTRY`, `C04_SCANNER_APPROACH`, `PLAYER_REVERSE` and `PLAYER_PINCH`. There is no convincing wheel route, repeated threshold contact or selectively worn marking visible at 600p. Broad wall planes likewise show little authored patching or staining from the normal views. Adding more close-range micro-bump would not solve this; the required information is broad, sparse and tied to use.

## Highest-impact repairs

1. **Repair the cover mapping.** Establish continuous textile coordinates over the drape, with intentional seams and comparable checker scale across the top and hanging sides. Remove the triangular fragmentation visible in `full-cycle-01-uv-valid/HERO_EVIDENCE.png`. Recheck a lit checker from both `HERO_EVIDENCE` and `HERO_TROLLEY`; an orderly cabinet front alone cannot approve the cover.
2. **Make the cover and chair pads read as soft materials.** Replace the cover's regular hard humps with broad gravity-driven drape, overlapping thickness/hem and compression at the straps. Give chair pads a readable soft edge, compressed contact and an upholstery/vinyl response distinct from the CRT shell, armrests and desk. Verify `C08_CONCEALED_SUPPORT_H1`, `HERO_TROLLEY`, neutral `HERO_TROLLEY` and neutral `C07_OFFICE_INTERIOR`. Preserve simplification; visible weave is unnecessary at these distances.
3. **Author localized use on the working route.** Add restrained wheel/contact bands at the gate-to-conveyor route, scuffs at the scanner plate/arrival threshold, and selectively interrupted line paint where that contact explains it. Add sparse touch wear at working handles, evidence locks and cargo contact edges. Verify `C01_ENTRY`, `C04_SCANNER_APPROACH`, `PLAYER_REVERSE`, `PLAYER_PINCH`, `HERO_CARGO` and `HERO_EVIDENCE`. Avoid uniform edge wear and blanket dirt.
4. **Strengthen family separation on broad surfaces.** Retain the useful bare-metal glints and plaster microrelief, but add controlled low-frequency variation and a small number of purposeful repairs to painted equipment and architectural surfaces. Keep molded plastic, paint, rubber, upholstery and mineral flooring distinguishable in neutral light through response and construction. Verify neutral `C07_OFFICE_INTERIOR`, neutral `HERO_TROLLEY`, `HERO_UTILITIES`, `DETAIL_CHECKIN` and `C05_CONVEYOR_LEAD_TUNNEL`. Preserve the successful navy/cream/rust/yellow grouping.
