# Full cycle 04 — materials and colour critique

**Verdict: FAIL category 4.** Material identity / UV discipline: **92/100**. Approved spawn colour coherence: **95/100**. The strict category requirement is >93. No dominant material/colour visual veto was found. This specialist does not certify the overall owner 99-point gate.

The dock clearly separates glass, metal rollers and conduits, paper, textured plaster and many matte paint surfaces. The close check-in work view is the strongest local material treatment. Neutral lighting exposes ambiguity between the grey chair pads, the smooth trolley cover and hard molded/rubber surfaces. Colour is coherent with the four approved spawn references: orange, muted blue/navy, charcoal and warm light neutrals recur with yellow restricted to hazards/routes. The broad blue equipment masses are brighter than the reference wall blues but remain bounded. There is no colour confetti.

## Bounded repair targets

1. **Chair pads and broad trolley-cover fields are insufficiently distinct from hard molded or rubber surfaces under neutral light.** Adjust only these local surface families: strengthen matte cloth/pad diffuse response and broad fold-related tonal/roughness variation; retain smooth low-frequency treatment and existing folds. Preserve trolley, straps and chair poses. Evidence: `full-cycle-04-neutral/C07_OFFICE_INTERIOR.png`, `full-cycle-04-neutral/HERO_TROLLEY.png`, `full-cycle-04/HERO_TROLLEY.png`. Rerender these two same-camera neutral views and their beauty counterparts; the cover/pads should read as softer than wheel rubber, metal handle and plastic CRT casing without added texture noise.

2. **Blue machinery and grey cart gate remain too uniform at the areas that workers and cargo touch.** Add restrained broad paint roughness variation and a few authored rubs at cabinet handles, cargo handoff edges and gate contact points. Keep intact large colour blocks and avoid universal exposed-edge wear. Evidence: `full-cycle-04/HERO_CARGO.png`, `full-cycle-04/C06_CART_GATE_G1.png`, `full-cycle-04-neutral/HERO_UTILITIES.png`. In existing hero/gate views, paint should remain matte to semi-matte and read as used manufactured steel, with wear concentrated at actual contact areas.

3. **Traffic deterioration is mostly carried by mottled yellow markings and one scanner-floor patch; the broad floor substrate still looks freshly uniform.** Add a small number of broad, directional substrate scuffs through scanner/cart travel and threshold approach, without changing route geometry, floor datum, lane paint extents or brightness hierarchy. Evidence: `full-cycle-04/HERO_SCANNER.png`, `full-cycle-04/PLAYER_REVERSE.png`, `full-cycle-04/PLAYER_PINCH.png`. The same player-height views should show a used route through substrate response without photographic dirt, busy scratches or reduced hazard readability.

4. **The near-black rectangular gate insert loses soft material response and reads as a void beside the grey sheet metal.** Lift only the insert diffuse value slightly and provide a broad matte rubber highlight/roughness cue while retaining its charcoal grouping and yellow hazard strip. Evidence: `full-cycle-04/C06_CART_GATE_G1.png`, `full-cycle-04/C04_SCANNER_APPROACH.png`. Insert remains darker and softer than sheet metal, but its surface is visible in both fixed close views.
