1. Connect custody with one short, large identifier repeated on the current cargo label, a check-in document and the held cabinet door. Add a physical tamper/release strip across an existing latch or lid seam. Preserve all footprints.
2. Make one active repair legible at the existing service cluster: a mismatched replacement cover, a restrained maintenance tape attachment, and one tool or spare component on the existing hand cart. Keep it one purposeful cluster with quiet space around it.
3. Give the current clipboard a short readable condition and a partly completed transaction: a clear crossed field or red HOLD stamp, a shifted sheet and the chained pen left across it. A hand-corrected shift slip or used glove in the counter/desk cluster would make the trace specific to a worker.
4. Give concealment a small physical history: a localized caster scrub or wheel arc ending behind the screen, and a partly stowed transfer docket tied to the same custody identifier. Keep the load covered and retain the screen and bay footprint.
