# Compliance dock overhaul rubric

Score eight equal categories independently /100, mean totals 100. Every category must be strictly greater than 93; the mean cannot hide a failing category. Current map threshold is preserved. Historical input scores are not overhaul scores.

1. Scale, layout and route readability.
2. Grounded stylized shape language and object-specific construction.
3. Inspection/check-in hero hierarchy and functional composition.
4. Tactile material identity and UV/texture discipline.
5. Localized lighting, depth and bleak atmosphere without hiding geometry.
6. Approved spawn colour coherence and restrained accents.
7. Purposeful worker traces and coercive institutional storytelling.
8. Technical cleanliness, support contact, reproducibility and dependencies.

Hard gates: no visual veto or critical defect; every fixed view; all objective checks; every support-dependent assembly registered; minimum four complete full review cycles; final two materially stable; cold-open/render comparison; editable native source and portable dependencies. Slice reviews evaluate local requirements only and cannot certify the full room.

Source planning targets (task estimates, not established engine budgets): at most 450,000 evaluated authoring triangles, at most 1,150 material submeshes, at most 36 local material families. Record actual baseline/final counters. Runtime draw calls, FPS and collision/navigation remain unmeasured until engine integration.
