# Waste Storage overhaul brief

Owner request, 2026-10-03: rework Waste Storage like the refinery overhaul: Valorant environmental principles, sharp construction, dark, gloomy, run down and pessimistic. Spawn is the finish-quality reference. Two independent skeptical GPT-6 Luna reviewers inspect the actual pixels. The owner requested a 99-point pass; this package uses the stricter working target of 99/100 in every category before final visual acceptance.

This owner mood replaces the older maintained-facility mood for this additive candidate. Keep believable industrial construction, material separation, useful negative space and gameplay readability. Wear must explain contact, leaks, moisture, broken equipment and long-term neglect; avoid uniform noise or cheerful accents.

Local contract: 12 x 18 x 4.8 metre clear interior, receiving origin (0,0,0), +Y into hall. Preserve every original interface, original camera transform/lens, functional equipment location, host hook and door/opening reservation. Central freight route X[-1.8,1.8], Y[4.5,14.5], height >=2.4m remains clear. Preserve receiving and rear turn areas, cell approaches, booth and personnel access. Original module and frozen accepted file are inputs, never outputs.

Hero hierarchy: shielded hot-waste casks, containment cells and controlled extraction. Supporting zones: receiving/inventory booth, sealed residue, dry contaminated equipment, quarantine, rear maintenance/dispatch. Keep the process and segregation understandable before reading labels.

Operational scope: this is receiving, inspection and segregated storage, not a continuous production line. The sealed vessels contain waste; the capture hood/header removes room air through the filter plenum and fan to the original extraction interface. It does not pipe vessel contents between casks. The opened SC01 cover protects its gauge, not the vessel closure. The bench presents an uninstalled replacement seal beside its split, rejected counterpart and a removal pick; a seated seal would require a mating component outside that task state. These explanations establish what the authored equipment does; reviewers must still judge whether the actual images communicate it.

Lighting: world strength zero; no sun, external light, broad unseen fill, HDRI or light portal. Every active light belongs to a visible modeled in-room fixture or actual instrument lens with documented support and power. Failed fixtures have zero light energy and zero lens emission. Dark intervals must retain enough local contrast to read route edges and important controls.

Human detail: a few authored clusters of worn PPE, spent filters, tools, repair supplies, paperwork and personal effects. Objects must be physically supported. Clutter may never obstruct contract routes. No greenery, decorative glowing lines, gratuitous warning signs or random debris.

Style: specific primary and secondary forms, folded sheet metal, restrained sharp bevels, robust cast/steel hardware and grounded adult proportions. Distinguish dirty concrete, faded paint, oxidized bare metal, rubber, cloth, paper and glass. Avoid toy plastics, uniformly rounded boxes, photoreal scan noise and featureless darkness.

Validate all protected anchors, every added support-dependent prop, fixture pairs and clearances. Cold rebuild from the original module in a separate process; compare fingerprints and all required fixed-view pixels. Use portable path metadata and preserve compatible render records during targeted rerenders.

Delivery: an editable additive module_overhaul_R1.blend, replay scripts, original-hash records, checkpoints, current fixed-camera images, independent reports, technical/contact evidence and cold proof.
