# Waste Storage review rubric

Two independent skeptical GPT-6 Luna critics rate the same actual fixed-camera images. Scores are normalized 0–100 per category; each critic must award >=99 in every category and find no critical veto. Report observed defects and camera locations; do not reward object counts, intention or implementation effort. Do not inflate scores to satisfy a target.

| Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Layout, scale and route readability | 20 |
| Art direction, architecture and silhouettes | 20 |
| Waste-process hero equipment and functional clarity | 15 |
| Materials and anti-plastic quality | 15 |
| Fixture-only lighting and gloomy atmosphere | 10 |
| Purposeful dressing and human storytelling | 10 |
| Technical cleanliness, contacts and reproducibility | 10 |

Visual vetoes: generic primitive or uniformly beveled-box construction, plastic response across material families, flat/flooded lighting, cheerful/showroom mood, unreadable darkness, excessive text/clutter, floating props, blocked routes, unmodeled illumination, broken dependencies or hidden camera coverage. A pass must cover every specified fixed view. Technical scoring is bounded by the supplied measured evidence, not inferred runtime behavior.

Refs: Spawn actual polish renders VALIDATE_Hero_A.png and VALIDATE_Spawn.png; refinery R24 actual entry/process/material images illustrate the requested darker mood. Use them as finish/mood references, not blueprints or instructions to copy.

## Quality anchors

Assess completion against the owner brief, the preserved Waste contract and the actual Spawn/refinery references. A 99 is reference-level execution with no material visible defect in that category, rather than proof of unrequested runtime behavior or hypothetical additions. Remain skeptical and name observed defects; do not inflate a score because a build consumed time.

| Category | What a passing execution demonstrates |
|---|---|
| Layout | Receiving, freight spine, turns, cell approaches, personnel and booth access remain clear, plausibly scaled and legible in the required views. The four segregated cells and protected camera/equipment poses are contract requirements; judge their visual differentiation in art direction. |
| Art direction | Specific cast/pressed/folded construction, sharp controlled edges, distinct waste functions, a coherent hierarchy and restrained negative space at the reference finish quality. No generic slab/box substitutes for primary equipment. |
| Process | The staged incoming load is retained, segregated contents/state are visually understandable, cask seals/lifting/controls read, and extraction/maintenance construction explains its function. No label alone substitutes for an invisible modeled state. |
| Materials | Concrete, paint, oxidized steel, rubber, cloth, paper, glass and wood show distinct response and scale. Wear follows use, seams and leaks; broad speckle, soft detached blotches, plastic response and flat damage cutouts fail. |
| Lighting | Modeled supported fixtures are the only sources, targeted pools preserve equipment/route readability, and dark intervals plus exhausted material/value choices support the gloomy brief. |
| Dressing | A few supported human/task clusters communicate interrupted use and overdue maintenance through their forms and condition, without route clutter or excess text. |
| Technical | Supplied authoring evidence verifies protected poses, registered contacts, practical lens seats/apertures, sampled routes, mesh winding, dependencies and independent cold state/pixels. Explicit measurement limits remain; runtime integration is outside this package. |
