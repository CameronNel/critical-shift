# Independent critic B — R25 focused diagnostic

**Scope:** C01 Entry, C02 Casks, C08 Extraction paired against R24, plus new supplemental D03 ExtractionRun. No category scores or full-cycle verdict. The supplemental is new context, not a replacement for any fixed view.

## Observed gains

- **C08 Extraction:** The round filter drop is now visibly inclined. Its axis leads diagonally from the upper header area down toward the fan/filter assembly, breaking the previous vertical post-like read and providing a stronger sense of duct routing. The short wall return and terminal joint are still largely obscured by the foreground body/camera crop, so this view alone does not show an unambiguous complete lower connection.
- **C02 Casks:** The cask faces have sharper, more localized lower paint loss than R24's diffuse mottling. The loss is concentrated at lower skirt/body transitions and reads more like coating failure than blanket speckle. Cask silhouettes, broad smooth body panels and the header-mouth hierarchy are otherwise nearly unchanged.
- **C01 Entry:** The entry view is essentially unchanged from R24. The screened dry-cell fronts are still the only noticeable break in the right-side trough sequence; broad left-side shield and quarantine surfaces remain plain from this camera.
- **D03 ExtractionRun:** This new wider context view does useful work: it places the shield casks/rectangular capture mouths at left and the angled round filter drop, extractor housing and fan at right in one coherent service bay. It clarifies that the two extraction branches are distinct but belong to a larger system, and it makes the inclined drop's route much easier to follow than C08. It also exposes the limited visual bridge between them: the header spans across the high background, but the broad plain wall gap between left and right groups leaves their shared system logic understated. Equipment remains dark and partly merges into the wall; the image gives useful spatial context, not full process legibility.

## Remaining concrete defects

1. **C01 architectural repetition persists.** The screened dry-cell face adds a valuable layered exception, but the entry still reads as rows of low troughs under a dark ceiling. Selectively vary shield and quarantine face construction within fixed footprints and keep the dry screens as one member of a deliberate family rather than the only detailed bay.
2. **C02 material and focal hierarchy remain weak.** Sharper lower loss helps, yet both casks retain expansive smooth panels and near-identical silhouettes. Their distinctive states/roles are still primarily carried by labels/gauges. The shallow rectangular capture mouths remain brighter and more eye-catching than the vessel-to-header construction.
3. **C08 lower drop termination remains hidden.** The incline is clearer, but the visible tube vanishes behind/at the edge of the extractor body before a viewer can read its lower flange/support. Improve the already modeled transition's exposure or silhouette from this angle without asking this crop to show the separate shield branch.
4. **D03's system context still relies on a continuous header and implication.** Its breadth clarifies the physical separation, but the header and branch junctions are visually low-contrast relative to the casks and wall. Shape/value hierarchy could better distinguish the rectangular shield hood branch from the inclined round filter branch while preserving each independent connection.
5. **Finish hierarchy remains behind references.** The Refinery close views show stronger formed profiles, use-led highlights and a more deliberate hero-to-background falloff. R25's darker palette is appropriate, but its hard surfaces still look smooth and evenly shaded; the newly sharper wall/cask wear is a positive, bounded step rather than a finished material pass.

The separate branches are correctly judged in separate fixed views; C08 need not include the rectangular Y11.5 mouth and no single fixture is expected to illuminate both separated junctions. Validation PASS (505,364 triangles, per task) is not visual acceptance, and no full-cycle/technical conclusion is drawn here.
