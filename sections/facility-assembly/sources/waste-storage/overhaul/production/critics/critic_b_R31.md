The process requirement is a readable connection in at least one fixed player view, not every camera showing the entire system graph. D02 separately clarifies the shield branch. This is why process remains below reference-level clarity, not because all views fail to show a complete graph.
- **Concrete material-family deficit:** My earlier “smooth surfaces” wording was too general. In close D01, both uninstalled rubber seal rings read as nearly the same dark charcoal value and hard-edged finish as adjacent dark painted/steel service hardware; the ring's raised concentric profile helps, but its rubber softness/roughness is not strongly distinguished. At the room scale, the painted cask bodies and dark structural collars also remain close in broad diffuse response (C02/W10). Give rubber a visibly softer, less specular edge response and a distinct compression/contact value at its resting edge, while retaining the existing seal shape and uninstalled bench state. This is a more concrete target than changing the inherently regular pressed lid ribs or the contract-required repeated cask form.
