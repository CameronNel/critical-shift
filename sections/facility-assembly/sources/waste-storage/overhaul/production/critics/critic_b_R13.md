# Independent full-cycle review — R13

**Verdict: FAIL.** R13 adds a visible shield-bay capture hood, makes the quarantine pleats legible through their window, and improves the seal-service ring and monitor/gauge housing profiles. Those are real pixel improvements over R08. The room still falls short of the established reference finish: the hood reads more like a long slotted soffit than a specific capture assembly; the cart restraint is not clearly secured; and broad views retain a highly repeated four-cell corridor composition. Final acceptance remains 99/100 in every category with no critical veto, and R13 does not approach that bar.

## Review basis

I inspected and paired all 21 R13 fixed views against R08. R13's manifest is complete and its source SHA-256 (`5b204c32945fe856c5bc4857afa2b35967f9667f09894b4e97a32e6cd0429aad`) matches `validation_R13.json`. The rendered images use the fixed 960×540 camera set and world strength 0. I used the current rubric quality anchors and reopened Spawn's `VALIDATE_Hero_A` and `VALIDATE_Spawn` plus refinery R24 `CAM_ENTRY`, `CAM_HERO_DETAIL`, and `CAM_WORK_NOOK`. The refinery references establish dark but information-rich machinery and material contrast; they are not a request to copy their palette or brighten the requested mood.

## Highest-impact pixel fixes

1. **Make the capture hood a distinct, mechanically supported intake.** In C02/C01 it is still a broad horizontal grille box. Give the visible intake a shaped flare/lip, folded returns/channel frame and a legible header-to-duct transition; use a restrained actual-fixture pool to reveal its depth. Ensure the cask-bay silhouette remains readable in C02 and the hood changes hierarchy in C01/C03/W07 without entering the clear route.
2. **Show the cart restraint as a complete load path.** C05 has only a narrow web line while the tensioning end is hidden. Route two visible bands across the overpack crown to cart anchors, with outward-facing ratchets/tensioners and clear contact at the saddle, so the image reads as a secured hazardous load rather than a cylinder placed on a tray.
3. **Give the repair station a convincing rubber/service state.** D01's stepped ring has improved shape but still looks rigid; show matte compressible rubber seated in a groove with a visibly compressed section or matching mating face, and make one operation clearly in progress. Keep additions supported and local to the bench.
4. **Integrate the extractor task light and inspection hardware.** C08 now reveals more interior detail, but the white front patch and projecting shelf feel abrupt. Make the lamp mount/aim legible and control its pool so the interior reads without looking self-luminous or washing out the adjacent dark surfaces.
5. **Increase material and story variation at room scale.** W05/W06/W07 still read as repeated bays around an unusually clean route. Preserve the four-cell plan and reservations, but differentiate large equipment states and show use-driven wear at seams, leak sources, hand/contact zones and floor turns. Avoid returning to broad speckling or uniformly repeated damage.
