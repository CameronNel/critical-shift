# Critic A — R25 focused diagnostic

This is a four-image diagnostic only; it assigns no full-cycle scores or acceptance credit. I opened R24 and R25 `D01_SealRepair`, `C06_Dry`, `W09_DrySouth` and `C05_Transfer` individually.

- **D01_SealRepair:** The failed seal now has an unmistakable lifted end, and the pick is positioned against that lifted section. This finally reads as removal-in-progress rather than a pick laid beside a split ring. The oil/contact mark is less perfectly circular, but still appears as a broad smooth dark halo centered under the seal; break its circumference and vary the edge so it reads as a smeared, transferred trace. The replacement ring remains a clean, complete circle laid out separately, so the workbench is still unusually orderly.
- **C06_Dry:** The lid ribs retain their R24 formed-panel benefit, and edge damage is substantially less blotchy. Corrosion now appears confined more closely to lid/perimeter contacts, which improves the steel read. The lid centers remain broad and uniformly pale; the ribs are still evenly spaced and the image does not yet show folded returns or wear at actual retaining-bar shoes. Keep the restrained corrosion and make the rib-to-rim construction physically continuous.
- **W09_DrySouth:** This angle confirms the clearer, contact-focused wear; the previous long rusty stripe across the near edge is reduced. Pressed ribs remain visible at range. The stains still bunch into several discrete, dark blobs under the bar, rather than following specific shoe/fastener pressure points, and the wide panel center remains visually empty. Relocate and taper each wear mark to the shoe/latch interface, preserving the large clean fields.
- **C05_Transfer:** The dark neutral webbing is less rust-colored and more plausibly fabric against the brown deck. I still cannot positively read a ratchet or its strap path in this frame: the load blocks the near-side hardware, and a dark vertical element by the canister could be a latch, but its open fork, pivot ears and retainer are not resolved at camera scale. The supported fix is to expose one near-facing ratchet/strap feed at the pallet edge, not enlarge or multiply hardware.

R25's most convincing gain is the D01 removal action; its clearest material gain is the quieter, localized lid wear. C05 remains the weakest of these targeted images because the intended restraint assembly is still occluded/low-contrast. These four images do not establish whole-room art direction or full-cycle readiness.
