# Independent critic B — R27b focused diagnostic

**Scope:** R26/R27b C01 Entry, C03 Reverse and W05 ReceivingExterior; R24/R27b W03 Dispatch; actual Spawn and Refinery R24 references. This is not a full-cycle review and carries no category scores.

## Coaming pixels

R27b introduces a warm brown, broad portal surround in the distant openings. It is plainly visible in C01 and C03 around the dispatch aperture and in W05 at the rear opening; the dedicated W03 comparison makes it strongest. The surround gives the black aperture a more definite outline and adds some material contrast. However, at these distances it reads as a large flat horizontal bar and two broad vertical bars, with only a thin seam line at the head and a few small lower plates. The bevel/shape is still block-like rather than a convincing open fabricated coaming. In W03 it consumes a large portion of the frame and overwhelms the otherwise restrained portal. This is a partial improvement in aperture definition but not yet a finished industrial threshold.

C01's gold-brown frame around the far dispatch opening is small and somewhat incongruous against the cooler wall and existing dark door track; the portal remains a black cutout in an otherwise plain far wall. C03 shows the surround more clearly but retains the same flat bar silhouette. W05 confirms the receiving approach remains unobstructed, with the new frame visible at the back; the portal remains low contrast in its lower half and does little to organize the nearer entry composition.

## Other observed context

- In C01/C03/W05, storage and the path remain legible. The screens/guard details from prior revisions persist, but the frame additions do not resolve the repeated low trough faces or the broad planar walls.
- W03's header sign/track, narrow glowing practical on the right and black void remain. The new warm frame supplies stronger contrast, but it looks like a continuous broad fascia rather than a channel section with returns. It also makes the portal surround one of the brightest/warmest visual masses without a corresponding threshold/detail hierarchy.
- Refinery R24's actual entry and hero images use layered edge profiles, connected brackets and local highlights to make large steel parts read as assemblies. The Waste frame is visibly simpler in construction and finish. Spawn's polish examples support controlled edge definition and clear focal framing, while the Waste palette should remain dark and desaturated.

## Highest-priority follow-up

1. Replace the broad flat frame read with an actual open jamb/head coaming: visible channel returns/rear skins, separable inner/outer lips, bolted corner ties and discrete support feet. Keep the opening dimensions and black beyond-space unchanged. The silhouette should show a fabricated section even at C01/C03/W05 distances; use the W03 crop to verify the head is not just a thick bar.
2. Cool and roughen the steel finish so it belongs to the surrounding structure. The current warm brown block has weak separation between broad faces and seams; a cooler oxidized steel with restrained edge loss and use-darkened threshold would be more coherent with the gloomy room.
3. Give the lower jamb/threshold a clear continuation and contact-wear cue visible in C01/W05. At present W03's upper head dominates while the portal foot detail is too small to balance it.

The black portal interior is expected for this isolated module; the issue is the authored surround's construction and value hierarchy, not missing neighboring geometry. R28 design intent is not credited in this review.
