# Critic A — R23 focused diagnostic

Scope: individually reviewed R23 `C05_Transfer`, `D01_SealRepair`, and `C06_Dry` against their R20 pixels and the actual Spawn hero / refinery R24 hero-detail, material, and work-nook references. This is early flaw detection only; it is not a full-cycle review or category acceptance.

- **C05 Transfer:** The strapped load and outward-facing tensioner blocks are visible, and the canister reads as secured better than the earlier bare cradle. The strongest remaining weakness is that the warm brown vertical straps still read almost like narrow rust-colored bands on the canister rather than clearly woven webbing; the square blocks beside the lower canister body do not quite read as ratchets from this camera. Smallest useful repair: give the near-facing strap a visible woven edge/texture and put one unmistakable ratchet lever or buckle at the forward plate edge, with the strap visibly feeding through it. Keep the load and route unchanged. The refinery hero detail separates web/metal/gasket/paint through contrasting edge response and visible fastener silhouettes; R23 is moving in that direction, though the transfer cylinder itself remains plain at this scale.
- **D01 SealRepair:** The interrupted, bent rejected seal with its pick is now distinct from the grooved replacement seal. The glove draped toward the bench edge adds a more human, less staged silhouette. These are meaningful story improvements, and the replacement remains visibly uninstalled. The parts still sit in a clean, evenly spaced display on a pristine tabletop; the oily wipes/handling do not read strongly at this framing. Smallest useful repair: add one clear, restrained contact trace where the rejected seal was pulled or wiped, and overlap the pick with the split seal so the removal action reads as recently performed. Keep the parts uninstalled. Compared with refinery WORK_NOOK, this is still a row of isolated props rather than a task cluster tied to shift evidence; avoid solving that with extra tiny paperwork.
- **C06 Dry:** Edge corrosion is narrower than R20 and the lid faces are cleaner; the rails and corner shoes remain useful scale cues. The two broad pale rectangular tops still dominate as nearly featureless slabs, and narrowing the wear leaves no strong material break at the fixed view's scale. Smallest useful repair: strengthen the formed perimeter return/downstand and give the visible latch/rail contact points distinct, mid-scale wear rather than spreading more stain over the faces. Keep corrosion tight to actual seams. The Spawn hero and refinery detail refs show a strong primary form with readable joints and material transitions; C06 is still mostly large flat planes.

Across these three images, the biggest pixel return is to make functional hardware silhouettes read at 960×540: a recognizable cart buckle, a seal removal contact trace, and a formed lid edge. Fine procedural texture by itself will not close the remaining reference-finish gap.
