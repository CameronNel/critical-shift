# Independent critic B — R24 focused diagnostic

**Scope:** C01 Entry, C02 Casks and C08 Extraction only, paired against R23. This is not a full-cycle review and carries no category scores or acceptance verdict. I also reopened the actual Spawn polish renders and Refinery R24 entry, hero-detail and work-nook renders as finish/mood references.

## Visible gains

- **C01 Entry:** The two right-side dry-cell faces now show dark diagonal wire screens inside framed openings, breaking the previously continuous smooth trough faces. The rear cell also reads as screened rather than a solid slab. This is the strongest R24 improvement in these three crops: it creates a more layered industrial rhythm without changing the aisle, cell footprint or portal. The lower catch curb remains visible. The openings are still small and dark at entry distance, so the mesh does not become a new noisy focal point.
- **C02 Casks:** Main change is the practical pool's aim/intensity, not new equipment. The casks and upper hood retain the same composition; local shell/rim values lift enough to reveal some face curvature and hardware. The adjacent wall remains dark and the hood is readable. Improvement is modest at this close crop; no distracting direct-light hotspot appears.
- **C08 Extraction:** The physical wall fixture is now aimed toward the extraction assembly, revealing more of the upper round drop and nearby filter shoulders while preserving a dark interval on the left wall. This directs attention toward the relevant hardware. The lower assembly remains partly concealed by the filter body and framing, and the outlet/filter relationship is not fully legible from this fixed side view; judge the connected path across the other views as well.

The tighter pools move the scene toward the intended gloomy industrial mood while retaining readability. The brighter reference images are useful for their deliberate focal hierarchy and crafted edge highlights, not their brighter palette. R24’s screens introduce a more specific folded/mesh construction language than the general smooth tub surfaces; the three available pixels suggest a worthwhile architectural improvement, but coverage of the other required views is pending.

## Remaining visible defects in these views

1. **C01:** The entry still resolves as a repeated line of waist-high containers; only the nearest right dry fronts break the rhythm. The cells at left and quarantine retain broad solid planes. Extend the same level of architectural differentiation selectively to the other functional bays while preserving the fixed four-cell plan; avoid turning every wall into mesh.
2. **C02:** The paired vessels remain close in silhouette and color, with large smooth body panels and a long shallow header grille. Their rims, lugs and rails have better light but do not yet carry the layered material contrast of the refinery machinery reference. Give each vessel finish/state a clearer visual difference and make the grille/header transition feel formed, not simply a bright rectangular insert.
3. **C08:** The aimed light gives a useful pool, but the central vertical drop still disappears behind the filter/foreground silhouette and the lower junction is obscured. Improve the visible support/junction within this crop where possible, while respecting that this round branch is physically separate from the rectangular shield hood branch in C02/D02.

The Spawn and refinery examples remain more resolved through stronger authored focal hierarchy, edge definition and material response. R24 improves readability without losing the requested dark mood, but current pixels are only a partial diagnostic set; no full-room conclusion should be drawn from these three views.
