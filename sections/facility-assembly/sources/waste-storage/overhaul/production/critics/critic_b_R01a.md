# Independent full-room visual review — R01a

**Verdict: FAIL.** The full camera set shows a recognizable storage process and a deliberately dark industrial palette, but much of the space reads as clean, repetitive gray-green bays under uneven light. Several required work areas lose useful detail in near-black shadow. The central route is mostly clear in these projections, yet its repeated yellow boundaries divide the space into a very regular corridor-and-bay pattern. Run-down character and human presence remain weak. The scenes are far below the required 99/100 in every category, with an unreadable-darkness veto in several views.

## Highest-impact repairs

1. **Restore functional readability in the dark cameras.** Raise local fixture-supported contrast on the transfer cart (C05), dry-store containers (C06/W09), quarantine interior (C07), and door frames/thresholds (W01/W02/W03) so their state and edges can be read. Preserve dark intervals elsewhere; do not lift the whole room evenly.
2. **Break the corridor-and-bay repetition.** Rework large-scale architectural rhythm, storage silhouettes and gap widths in the route-facing views. The matching square bay fronts and parallel yellow stripes recur in C01/C03/C04/W05/W07 and make the whole plan feel like a repeated kit. Keep the clear freight spine, but give each waste function a stronger shape and value identity.
3. **Differentiate and age the major materials.** Add broad, selective wear that explains handling, damp and repair to the casks, steel, wall bases, cart and storage lids. Distinguish bare oxidized metal, painted shell, rubber, concrete and paper at gameplay distance. Avoid applying blanket dirt to the repeated assets.
4. **Clarify process flow without relying on labels.** Make the handoff from receiving (W02/W05), inventory (C09/W08), segregation (C02/C06/C07/W10), transfer (C05) and extraction (C08) clear through spatial hierarchy, equipment silhouettes and state cues. C02/W10 casks are readable, but the near-black cart and bins break the process story.
5. **Add restrained, localized evidence of people and neglect.** Expand the strongest human signals from C10/D01 into a few distinct work zones: worn task tools, tagged failed seals, repair materials, shift evidence or a used handling area. Current large views read maintained and nearly showroom-clean despite their dim lighting.
6. **Audit all light-emitting sources, especially inventory.** Validation covers 20 LIGHT/lens pairs and world strength zero, but C09/W08 also show a small bright task tube over the inventory monitor. Confirm whether it is purely a material-emission lens or contributes a light source; document its fixture, support and power path so it cannot become an untracked additional illuminator.
