# Critic A — R24 focused diagnostic

This is a four-view diagnostic, not a full-cycle review or category score. I inspected R24 `C05_Transfer`, `D01_SealRepair`, `C06_Dry` and `W09_DrySouth` individually against their R23 pixels, then reopened the actual Spawn polish and refinery R24 hero-detail, material and work-nook references. R24 validation is reported PASS at 504,906 triangles, 280 new plus 57 inherited contacts; those counts do not determine the visual findings below.

- **C05_Transfer:** The newly framed dark mesh screens behind the cart and the dry bins materially improve depth and make this bay feel built from separate framed enclosures rather than continuous blank panels. The cart/load itself has barely changed in the camera: the canister dominates, while the sewn web and U-fork ratchet remain difficult to resolve against the brown load deck. The near-right hardware is a small dark mass without a clearly visible pivot/retainer silhouette; strap routing into it is hidden by the load/deck. The R24 target hardware is therefore not yet delivering an unmistakable tie-down read, even in the close transfer view. Keep the restraint and expose one near-facing fork/lever and the web feed across the canister, with a small value separation from the deck; do not add another prop.
- **D01_SealRepair:** The oil/contact trace is now visible beneath and around the rejected split seal, grounding it better than the clean ring in R23. It is a plausible improvement, though the broad diffuse halo is close to a perfect ring-shaped stamp and could read as a graphic decal instead of oil. The pick still lies beside the split rather than levering it, and the intact replacement remains a neat complete circle. Break up the stain with an irregular contact edge and have the pick tip visibly engage the cut; the Spawn/refinery references make their hero object/action legible without relying on labels.
- **C06_Dry:** The integral pressed stiffeners change the blank lid planes into recognizable formed steel panels with a useful highlight/shadow ridge. The framed screen and continuous-looking catch curb add credible enclosure depth behind the bins. Remaining issues: the stiffeners are broad and evenly spaced enough to feel like applied strips, not pressed returns, and the large lid centers remain clean, pale fields. Preserve the old rim, but make each stiffener transition into a crisp folded downstand/return with localized contact wear at the retaining bars. Avoid more damage speckle.
- **W09_DrySouth:** The new long ridges are much more legible at this oblique angle than in C06, so they materially improve lid construction. The adjacent lid still reads as a plain pale panel, and the dark corrosion remains a near-continuous rusty band along its front/side rim; the stiffener shadows have overly uniform spacing. Tie the ridges into formed perimeter returns and confine corrosion to the actual bar shoes, latch points and seam breaks. The R24 refinery materials reference separates shell, machined edge and oxidized contact points more cleanly.

The strongest R24 gain is credible structural layering from the framed screens and a better formed-lid read. The strongest remaining defects are the obscured transfer restraint in C05, the non-actioning pick and ring-like stain in D01, and the still uniform lid/wear treatment in C06/W09. These four images do not establish whole-room quality, full fixed-camera coverage or R24 acceptance.
