# R49 targeted shell-finish diagnostic

I opened actual R45, R48 and R49 C02_Casks and W04_CellService images. In R49, the seven small exposures register at normal camera size as discrete contact/edge rubs, mostly around protruding hardware, carrier edges and lower handling/nameplate zones. C02 shows scattered subordinate marks on SC01 and SC02; W04 shows a couple of small shell chips rather than a broad field. Their slightly warm undercoat is visible against the gray-green coating, but the restrained size and placement read plausibly as exposed coating from contact and handling.

The R46 cloudy/camouflage read is absent. I see no crisp repeated decal/stamp, decorative pattern or new process cue. The R49 images improve on R46 and address R48's very smooth broad read in these crops. I found no remaining pixel defect here that should block full continuation. This is a targeted diagnostic, not a full-cycle score or acceptance decision.
