# Independent critic B — R26 focused diagnostic

**Scope:** C01 Entry, C03 Reverse, C07 Quarantine and W05 ReceivingExterior against their R24 counterparts. This is a four-view diagnostic, not a full-cycle review or acceptance score. Supplied R26 validation is PASS; no score is inferred from it.

## Pixel gains and limits

- **C01 Entry:** The shield-side equipment gains a raised rectangular guard frame across the front of the bay. The cask/guard silhouette now has more depth than R24's mostly low, continuous top edge, but from this long view it reads as a thin horizontal rail crossing equipment rather than a clearly supported safety/maintenance structure. The dry screens remain the only highly legible folded cell faces. Most of the room is unchanged.
- **C03 Reverse:** The residue bay gains stepped/cast shoulder forms around the vessels, and parts of the service/guard frame are visible over the cells. These create some height variation but are low contrast and partly lost behind the foreground tubs. The aisle is still clear. New additions are modest at room scale.
- **C07 Quarantine:** Folded corner returns/formed details are visible at both upper side edges of the quarantine bay. They break the featureless box outline slightly, but the folds appear as small isolated wedge shapes against broad walls and do not yet explain a larger frame or hinge/load path. Lid continues to obscure the wall heading; open state and viewport remain readable.
- **W05 ReceivingExterior:** New bay structures read as dark vertical and horizontal framing around left and right storage faces. This adds depth but remains visually subordinate to the broad rectangular portal surround and repeated tubs. Receiving opening is still a plain framed black void as expected for an isolated module; its authored surround has little formed edge language or wear.

## Highest-impact remaining visible defects

1. **Portal architecture is the strongest room-scale miss in these views.** In C01 the far dispatch opening is a black rectangle under a thick, plain header; in C03 the same opening has a similarly simple frame; W05 foreground receiving portal repeats a broad squared surround. Keep black beyond the contract opening, but give the authored jambs/heads real formed construction—layered coaming, channel returns, corner gussets/fasteners, worn threshold and door-track hardware—so these portals feel built into a working freight facility rather than cutouts in flat walls.
2. **The new guard frames need readable supports and purpose.** C01 and C03 show thin rails that cross cell faces with weak visible bracketing. Give the guard structure clear welded uprights, foot plates and attachment points, with a consistent practical function (impact guard or access protection) and preserve route clearance. At current distance the frame reads as a line, not a sturdy fabricated assembly.
3. **Quarantine folds need integration into the bay construction.** C07's wedge-like returns stand apart from the otherwise plain wall and broad box; add continuous folded returns/channel seams and visible fasteners so these look like formed corner stiffeners tied into the cell rim. Keep the raised lid and viewport legible.
4. **R26's additions do not yet shift the surface story.** C03 and W05 retain large smooth, evenly valued partition panels and clean floor; the cast shoulders have low contrast. Localized projected loss at cask handling zones and seams, together with narrow edge wear and concrete damp/runoff near actual contact points, would give the new geometry scale without introducing clutter.
5. **Room-wide hierarchy remains diffuse.** C01/C03/W05 still give similar weight to ceiling bars, plain walls and rows of containers; there is no strong entry-to-process focal progression. Existing modeled fixtures can target the receiving/inspection work areas and let the inactive spans recede, retaining world-zero and route readability.

These observations support the reported modest gain rather than a broad transformation. No new process line or extra illumination is needed; the next pass should invest in formed portal coamings and coherent supports for already-present bay frames, with localized surface wear that explains use.
