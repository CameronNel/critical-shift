## Remaining visible defects

1. **Broad primary surfaces are still too uniform for the reference finish.** In C02_Casks and W04_CellService, the cask bodies show their collars, lugs, fasteners and instruments clearly, but most of each body resolves as a large low-variation grey-green plane. C06_Dry and C07_Quarantine likewise show broad cell panels with little visible value/roughness separation from adjacent painted steel. The result is that repeated bays and vessels share more of one generic painted-metal read than the individually authored shapes in refinery R24 CAM_PROCESS/CAM_HERO_DETAIL. R39’s softer wall wear helps; the remaining shortfall is on the main equipment surfaces themselves.
2. **Practical diffuser faces remain a repeated bright pattern.** In C01_Entry, C03_Reverse and C04_Route, small pale ceiling lenses repeat as regular horizontal bars and catch the eye before some of the storage silhouettes. The ceiling around them remains near-black and the floor/walls are not broadly washed out. This is a lens-face luminance/repetition issue, not excessive light spill; localized wall pools and route contrast remain intact.

I did not retain the R37 hard-cutout wall finding against R39. Reopened R37/R38/R39 C09_Inventory and W03_Dispatch pixels show the R38/R39 wall treatment has changed the dark, hard-edged patches into faint, graded variation. C09’s former lower-left clusters are substantially softened, and W03’s small lower-west-wall marks are subdued. I do not see a consequential residual wall defect in R39.
