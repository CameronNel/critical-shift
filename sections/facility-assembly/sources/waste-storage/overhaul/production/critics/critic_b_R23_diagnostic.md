# Independent R23 focused diagnostic — three views only

This is a targeted pixel diagnostic, not a full-cycle review or acceptance score. I compared R23 against R20 in C08_Extraction, C02_Casks and C01_Entry, and reopened the actual Spawn finish renders and refinery R24 entry, hero-detail and work-nook references. R23's validation JSON reports PASS and world strength 0; that evidence does not substitute for the image read.

## What improved

- **C02_Casks:** The blue-gray continuous intake/header and muted ochre flared mouths separate the capture assembly from the vessel coatings more clearly than R20's mostly uniform pale grille. The cask faces appear quieter, with less broad corrosion speckle. The forged collars and vessel silhouettes remain legible.
- **C08_Extraction:** The round vertical drop is a more specific form than the prior slab-like branch and establishes an air path above the existing filter machinery. The wall pool helps retain the equipment in the dark, and the service area remains readable.
- **C01_Entry:** The capture element is visible at the shield bay and the overall aisle remains dark yet readable. The low-key mood is consistent with the brief.

## Remaining pixel defects

- **C08:** The new round drop exits the top of the camera frame and its lower connection is obscured by the tall filter/service assembly. In the pixels, the hollow drop still reads as a plain dark tube behind the machine; its flange and actual inlet transition are not legible. The large neighboring ducts and vertical supports continue to crowd the filter face.
- **C02:** The flared ochre mouths and dark slats are clearer than R20, but the long capture unit still reads partly as a wide horizontal grille. The round branch and the two mouths do not yet resolve as one continuous intake-to-header assembly at this angle. The pale/ochre contrast makes the mouth edges pop, though those straight parallel lines continue to dominate over the cask service detail.
- **C01:** The capture assembly reduces to a small pale horizontal object above the left shield bay. It contributes little to the room-scale focal hierarchy and does not explain the extraction path from this distance. The four-cell corridor remains visually repetitive, with a long smooth central floor and mostly uniform ceiling rhythm.
- **C02/C08:** The new coating differentiation is an improvement, but large vessel and duct surfaces still have broad low-frequency fields. The 2–12 mm edge corrosion is subtle at these crops, and no large view here makes the surfaces feel notably exhausted or recently serviced.

## Reference comparison and smallest high-impact action

Spawn's finish references use clear shape hierarchy, sharply readable forms and distinct material response. The refinery references maintain a gloomy value range while using a specific hero machine, visible connection logic and local practical pools to explain the equipment. In these three R23 views the capture system has more variation than R20, but the new drop-to-header junction is still visually ambiguous and the wide entry barely registers the hood.

**Smallest high-impact action:** aim the existing shield-bay practical so its pool catches the lower drop flange and the inside edge of one intake mouth together. This should visually bind the round drop, header and intake in C02/C08 without adding another light source or changing the gloom. Confirm the same junction gains a readable highlight from C01; avoid increasing general fill.
