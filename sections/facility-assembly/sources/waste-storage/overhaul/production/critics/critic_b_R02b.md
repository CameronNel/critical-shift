# Independent full-room visual review — R02b

**Verdict: FAIL.** The restored genuine bay practicals materially improve readability over R01a: the transfer cart, dry-store lids and authored portal jambs now separate from their surroundings. The main correction introduces a larger visual regression: broad, high-contrast orange-white corrosion covers entire cask shells, collars, dry-store covers and quarantine faces. At these camera distances it reads as repeated speckled camouflage/noise, not wear concentrated at real rims, joints and handling points. The room remains too regular and too clean in its architecture and dressing to feel convincingly neglected. The scene does not approach the required 99/100 in any category.

## Highest-impact corrections

1. **Replace blanket corrosion with contact- and failure-based wear.** Remove most of the mottled pattern from flat shell panels and cover centers. Concentrate restrained oxidation at flange joints, fasteners, feet, leaking seams and touched handles, with different age and severity per vessel. The R02b treatment is a major materials regression across C02/C06/C07/W04/W09/W10.
2. **Make cask and cover construction carry the hero read.** Give covers formed edges, returned metal, stepped sealing faces and a clear locking/interface sequence; preserve readable silhouette. C02/W10 show functional hardware but the wide lids and mottled collars still read as repeated circular plates.
3. **Make the segregation/transfer process visible through objects.** Load or prepare the C05 trolley for a clear task, and place a legible spent filter/identified item inside the quarantine lid clearance in C07. Use changes in equipment state to distinguish dry, quarantine, residue and shielded storage without adding more signs.
4. **Break the repeated bay and lane rhythm.** Keep the required central route open, but vary bay-face construction, service access and large negative-space groupings so C01/C03/C04/W05/W07 do not read as repeated rectangular stalls with parallel stripes.
5. **Give the broader environment evidence of neglect.** The lighting repair solved important readability gaps, but the room still feels recently cleaned apart from the blanket speckling. Add a few authored, localized traces of water, repair, handling and prolonged use tied to actual surfaces and work zones. Preserve the clear practical-light hierarchy and dim intervals.
