# Independent full-room visual review — R04c

**Verdict: FAIL.** R04c makes two useful changes visible: the R02b corrosion blanket is much reduced, and the transfer cart now carries a seated overpack. True bay practicals maintain visibility, and the portal frames/approaches read as authored geometry. The overall room still lacks the polish demanded by the 99/100 bar. Most concerning, the quarantine filter is barely visible above the rim, and the new partition skins read inconsistently as oxidized steel versus brown horizontal boards. Repeated bay forms, broad smooth surfaces and sparse human evidence remain.

## Evidence inspected

I opened every individual image in the complete R04c manifest (21/21), comparing each fixed camera with the corresponding R03 view. Camera positions and lenses remain fixed. The render manifest hash matches `validation_R04c.json` for the checkpoint (`4336f7a0…42a69dad`). I reviewed the actual pixels only; later R05 plans receive no credit here. Portal content beyond the isolated-module openings is excluded; this review scores the visible jambs, bulkheads, walls, thresholds and approaches.

## Highest-impact pixel defects

1. **Make quarantine contents visible as a filter, not just a cap.** In C07 the top rim of a pale object peeks above the chest, but nearly all of it is hidden by the front wall. Reposition and scale the payload so the visible portion unmistakably reads as a spent filter while remaining inside the closure clearance.
2. **Make the new partition construction read consistently as steel.** In C01/C05 the blue panels look like sheet metal, but C03/C04/W07 show brown horizontal courses that read as stacked wood boards. Preserve the channel/skin silhouette while giving the entire family consistent folded edges, steel value/color and restrained joint corrosion.
3. **Give shielded cask caps a manufactured profile.** C02/W10 still read as circular disks on top of repeated cylindrical bodies. Add a visible crown/step and closure interface that explains how the cap seals and how lifting pads remain seated; do not rely on a label or bolt count to carry the silhouette.
4. **Finish the wear pass at broad cover scale.** R04c is a real improvement over R02b, but C06/W09 still show speckles across cover centers and large faces. Reserve rust for rims, joints, fasteners and feet, with variation between assets. Let the dry-store covers read as metal first.
5. **Keep widening the story and composition beyond the main aisle.** The incoming overpack helps C05/W05. Quarantine and dry storage still look like rows of similar containers, while the approach remains split by two uninterrupted yellow lines. Use different equipment states and localized human traces while keeping route reservations clear.

Full fixed-view coverage is present; cold proof and final acceptance are not.

**R04c full-cycle review: FAIL; retain the lighting and loaded cart, then fix quarantine legibility, partition material consistency, cask cap construction and remaining broad-face wear before another complete comparison.**
