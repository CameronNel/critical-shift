New exposed-loss patches appear on SC01 at the upper-right shoulder below the collar and low on the front-left body. Their placement also feels decorative rather than tied to handling, collar rub or a specific impact. Keep the broad fade, but reduce the dark outline and show a layered edge/primer-metal transition concentrated under the collar contact and at plausible handling/scuff zones. Since this view is close enough to see the shoes, place the losses at a shoe edge, collar overlap or contact hardware and soften the transition. The casks otherwise remain visually repeated, but that is not the targeted change in this diagnostic.

The targeted cask coating change does not improve quarantine function or give this image new story information.

This note assesses only these pixels and does not grant whole-room credit.
