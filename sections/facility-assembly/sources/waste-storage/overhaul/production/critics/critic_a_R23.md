## Highest-impact remaining pixel repairs

1. **Clarify extraction as one continuous, camera-readable assembly.** The R23 riser in C08 is a better vertical cue, while D02 shows the flared intake mouths, but the fixed player views still do not connect intake → header/plenum → fan. In the existing room envelope, make the header/plenum turn and fan interface visible together in C01/C02/C08 or add a deliberately framed view of that existing route; keep the seated fixtures and access lane clear.
2. **Give the four required cells distinct mid-scale silhouettes and surface construction.** C01/C03/C04/W07 repeat the same bay partition and bin profile. Add only a few structural differences that survive wide frames—folded returns, reinforced corners, service doors or distinct rim hardware tied to each cell's function—rather than more micro-labels or props. Preserve the fixed cell plan and cask poses.
3. **Finish cask and bin material response at close and middle range.** C02/C06/W04/W09/W10 still show pale broad panels and soft rust masks. Model crisp lid downstands/returns, then keep sharper, narrower oxidation at actual latches, gasket seams, shoes and contact edges; replace lower-face mottles with a limited directional leak/contact trail where supported. Avoid broad decals or extra speckle.
4. **Make the held transfer load and repair action read as actual work.** In C05 give one near-facing ratchet a legible lever, anchor and visible strap path over the load. In D01 overlap the pick with the split seal and place the rejected/replacement distinction in a physically plausible service sequence; one localized oily glove/bench contact trace will do more than additional loose props. Keep the rest of the bench sparse.
5. **Create a restrained task-light/value hierarchy at inspection points.** C07/C08 pleats and C09's held-state information remain low contrast. Use only the already supported task/inspection fixtures to put a controlled pool on the filter window, gauge or held inventory state, allowing surrounding bays to fall darker while keeping route readability.
