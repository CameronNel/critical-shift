# Independent full-cycle review — R20

**Verdict: FAIL.** R20 makes a coherent step forward in process communication: the capture header/hood is visibly more articulated, the cart now has visible side tensioners, the quarantine pleats read through the window, and the inventory/repair details tell a clearer maintenance story. The main room still does not reach reference-level execution. The broad composition stays repetitive and tidy, several hero surfaces retain broad mottling or blank faces, and the new process details are small or awkwardly placed in several fixed views. The final bar remains 99/100 in every category.

## Review basis

I opened every one of the 21 R20 fixed-camera renders and paired each with its R13 counterpart. I also inspected supplemental `details/R20/D02_CaptureService.png`; it supports close reading of the hood but does not replace a required camera view. The complete render manifest source SHA-256 `ecc4ae92fdcefbeaf7028ddd3661b7e31d56c0836f10d29e6c7546108d97f57e` matches `validation_R20.json`.

I reopened Spawn `VALIDATE_Hero_A.png` and `VALIDATE_Spawn.png` and refinery R24 `CAM_ENTRY.png`, `CAM_HERO_DETAIL.png`, and `CAM_WORK_NOOK.png`. The requested gloomy mood differs from Spawn's brighter palette, while its finish reference still highlights composed silhouettes, layered construction, deliberate material separation and a legible human-use cluster. The refinery views show dark values with a specific hero vessel, connected pipework and controls, and purposeful work details. R20 is more functionally specific than R13 but remains less composed and differentiated at room scale.

1. **Carry the capture function into the wide views.** The close-up D02 is stronger than the fixed-room read. Give the header-to-intake transition a distinct silhouette and material/value break at C01/C02/C03/C04 and W05/W07, while keeping the clear ceiling and route reservations. The smallest high-impact change is to strengthen the visible transition between the wall branch, central header and two flared mouths, rather than adding more slats or props.

3. **Move the shift note off the inventory display.** C09/W08 show “NO RELIEF” but it masks part of INVENTORY. Clip it to an adjacent bezel/board at eye level so both the shift message and the terminal's purpose stay legible.
4. **Make wear follow material and use rather than broad pattern.** Narrow vessel corrosion to rims, fasteners, jacket seams and touched fittings; replace W10's flat spall cutout with chipped thickness/exposed substrate and a plausible local source; keep lid wear variable across covers. Preserve the better restrained R20 coverage.
5. **Make the repair cluster feel actively worked.** D01 now communicates the right state; show one grounded action cue such as a visibly disengaged old seal with the pick contacting its split and a compressed section/groove on the mating hardware. Keep the replacement seal, rejected counterpart and gloves supported and uncluttered.
