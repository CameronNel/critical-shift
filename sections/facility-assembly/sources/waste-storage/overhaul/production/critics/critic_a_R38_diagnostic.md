| C02_Casks | R37's opposed dark fittings read as one continuous bridge across the gap. In R38, both exposed metal spindle ends and the dark negative space between them are more visible. This finding describes appearance only; it does not claim the modeled components are joined. I made no full-scene or technical acceptance judgment.
