## Highest-impact remaining pixel repairs

1. **Expose the tie-down assembly at the near side of C05.** Reposition the fork/ratchet pivot and its web feed so one real lever, anchor and strap path are unmistakable above the deck edge. The new steel screen is a good secondary improvement; it does not compensate for the intended load restraint disappearing behind the load.
2. **Close the formed-lid construction and wear pattern in C06/W09.** Carry the pressed ridges into crisp returns/downstands and interrupt the continuous rust stripe at real contact boundaries. Keep pale centers differentiated with subtle use variation rather than noise.
3. **Make the extraction route one readable silhouette in fixed views.** D02 shows the intake construction, but C08 still loses the rise above frame without revealing its destination. In the existing envelope, ensure at least one player view contains the rise, header/plenum turn and fan/interface as one connected, readable assembly.
4. **Make D01 show a specific action.** Let the pick tip engage and deform the split seal; replace the nearly perfect ring-shaped oil halo with a small irregular grounded wipe/contact trace. Keep the replacement seal and the rest of the bench sparse.
5. **Differentiate bay architecture through large forms, not additional props.** C01/C03/C04/W07 repeat the same partitions. Retain the contract-required four cells and repeated casks, but use a few cell-specific folded frames, service doors, screen extents or reinforced corners that remain visible at 960×540 and reinforce each use.
