Verdict: HOLD style-slice expansion. This is a credible restrained industrial shell, but it has not yet demonstrated the complete canonical style-validation slice. This is not whole-section acceptance. The intentionally absent connector apron/ground is excluded from defects and scoring; do not add ground to fix this review.

## Visible strengths

The ribbed entrance, canopy brackets, practical lights, downpipe with clips and modest electrical cabinet provide plausible construction. Off-white, charcoal and muted orange form disciplined colour groups. Entry is immediately identifiable and its localized light pools have visible falloff and bracket shadows. Quiet wall areas are valuable. No dominant glossy-plastic, sci-fi, photoreal-noise or toy-like veto is visible.

## Highest-impact blockers

1. The required human-use cluster is absent. The front contains a reader and sign, and the side contains a cabinet, but no legible 3–5 human storytelling props or primary work cluster proves the slice requirement. Add one compact, wall-supported maintenance/compliance cluster with purposeful handling wear; preserve the quiet wall and entrance clearance. Do not scatter filler across the building.
2. Localized use is not convincingly visible. Wall texture is more apparent than authored handling/weathering at the reader, threshold, door edges or downpipe discharge. The overall read remains newly assembled and procedural. Introduce restrained, broad, location-specific variation, not global noise.
3. REVERSE exposes an unresolved plain projecting cuboid and pale vertical infill above it. Its function and junction with the facade cannot be understood from the pixels. If this is protected existing connector geometry, retain it and document its role; judge a resolved additive trim/junction treatment around it without changing the original dimensions. Do not propagate this anonymous box treatment as an exterior design motif.
4. The images are useful shell views, but OBLIQUE and REVERSE are elevated overview cameras. No demonstrably gameplay-height entrance/reader/material view is provided. A full-slice expansion gate needs that evidence; physical scale and usable reader height cannot be established from these images alone.
