# A13 roof services

Built supported roof-service assemblies on ten sections: ventilated painted-steel casings, weather hoods, rain louvers, mounting rails/anchors, local isolators, sealed roof conduits and inspection covers. Mine and below-grade condenser geometry are preserved. No facility utility-loop or machinery-rating claims are added.

Five roof samples per assembly establish a level supporting patch. BUILD.json records the sampled source surfaces, heights and positions. The addition contains 340 editable objects, batched into one viewport mesh. Original geometry is available through the existing fast/original display toggle. This is a construction/detail increment, not final art acceptance or a measured FPS improvement.

Files: blender/facility_walkthrough_A13_roof_services.blend and facility_master_A13_roof_services.blend. Launcher prefers A13; Shift+F and gravity-off walking remain. Sources remain unchanged. Replay build_roof_services.py from A12. Fresh Cycles previews in connections/roof-services/renders use authored new geometry and cached room context. Route regression repeats the A12 floor, headroom and crossing scan; runtime collision remains a separate Unity task.
