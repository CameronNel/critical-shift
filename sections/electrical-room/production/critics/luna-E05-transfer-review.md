# Luna concept review — E05 transfer and priority

**Reviewer:** Luna, independent electrical-room concept reviewer  
**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Asset:** `sections/electrical-room/art/concepts/E05-transfer.png`  
**Scope:** depicted transfer/priority equipment guidance only; no geometry, footprint, interface, camera, rating or saved-scene approval

## Disposition

**E05: APPROVED COMPONENT GUIDANCE WITH A STATUS-COLOR CORRECTION.** E05 is a strong interaction-focused concept for the manual transfer and priority station. The paired normal/reserve handles, mechanical interlock, priority selector, reserve gauge, physical crank, inspection access, cable entries and grounded base communicate a real manual recovery action in a compact authored assembly. The broad cream/charcoal/oxide-orange treatment fits the strict target and contains no teal.

The green `SOURCE AVAILABLE` lamp is not adopted as authored palette. It is a green status accent rather than teal, but the room palette correction is to render that state amber. Keep the red fault state distinct. All text in E05 is concept guidance only; no ratings or generated labels become specification.

## Accepted guidance

- Carry forward the paired normal/reserve handles and physical interlock as the dominant station silhouette.
- Preserve a large tactile priority selector and a separate manual-crank action so the station reads as equipment rather than a decorative UI panel.
- Use the analogue reserve gauge and compact status grouping, changing the green source-available cue to amber in authored materials.
- Retain cream/charcoal/oxide-orange with amber/red functional accents and no teal.
- Treat the rear casing, inspection access, feet and cable entries as construction cues and prove their actual support and approach in the complete room.

## Rejected authority and history

E05 does not supersede the original electrical-room brief, architecture contract, `interface.json`, S05 baseline or the fixed measured room. It does not define TD dimensions, priority simulation, electrical ratings, runtime strings, state authority or exact cable connections. The green lamp is explicitly corrected to amber; no concept text is adopted automatically.

## Review result

**E05 transfer result: APPROVED COMPONENT GUIDANCE WITH AMBER STATUS CORRECTION.** The transfer hierarchy, mechanical interlock, priority selector, reserve readback, manual crank, service construction, material separation, no-teal palette and interaction silhouette meet the 90 guidance threshold. Room placement, lighting, text authority and technical claims remain rejected or unproven.
