## Highest-impact observed defects

1. **The rendering language remains too pale and uniform.** In S01, the huge bright left wall, light floor, white bus and luminous cabinet fronts produce a showroom/product-mockup impression. In S02, instrument surrounds, cabinet paint, hardware and trolley still do not separate into convincing tactile material families. This falls substantially below the reference images' differentiated painted metal, steel, concrete and functional plastic/rubber.
2. **The cabinet cluster does not yet demonstrate sufficient object-specific construction.** S02 reveals secondary detail, but the presentation is dominated by simple rectangular face plates and attached disks/blocks. The very large dark vertical seams, projecting fastener-like nubs, and clean uniform surface treatment reinforce assembled primitives. Electrical gear can properly be rectilinear; the defect is the weak specificity and finish of the visible manufactured parts, not rectilinearity itself.
3. **Lighting obscures the intended finish rather than proving it.** S01's upper utility elements and cabinet instrument panels are conspicuously washed out, and the scene has little controlled contrast between the working focal area and secondary architecture. Strong local hardware shadows alone do not establish the required environmental lighting hierarchy.
4. **The required slice is not fully evidenced.** A doorway and frame are visible only at the extreme left; a complete functioning door is not demonstrated. No clearly visible practical fixture is available to relate to its pool of illumination. The cart cluster does not visibly establish the required three to five human storytelling props with clear material identity.
5. **Power-sequence readability is presently label dependent.** The two cabinets read as a matched pair with almost equal visual emphasis. The titles communicate sequence only on closer inspection; the visible utility connection reads as a bright generic overhead assembly. The expected process story is not yet visually traceable with confidence.

## Gate findings

- **Anti-plastic / procedural-cleanliness veto: triggered by the dominant pale satin presentation.** This is not a claim that everything is highly glossy; the failure is the common smooth synthetic appearance across materially different assets.
- **Anti-blockout gate: failed.** There is useful secondary cabinet detail, but the bus transitions, small props and overall cabinet presentation are not yet at the approved references' simplified-but-specific standard.
- **Gameplay-height evidence: present**, according to the recorded camera heights and the visible perspective.
- **Full slice coverage: incomplete**, for the door, practical fixture and clearly readable human-work props noted above.
- **Technical and cold-start gate: unassessed**, not waived and not represented as a failure discovered by geometry inspection.

The existing negative space, restrained accent count, plausible general equipment scale, visible hinges/latches and recognizable operating mat are useful foundations. They do not offset the visual vetoes or authorize expansion. The next comparison must use the same two cameras and framing, with the same render settings, and remain a slice review.
