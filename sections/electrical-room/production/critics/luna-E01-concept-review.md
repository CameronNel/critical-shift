**Reviewer:** Luna, independent electrical-room concept reviewer

**Scope:** concept relevance and visual guidance only; no geometry, interface, ratings, footprint or saved-scene approval

## Disposition

**E01 overall: REJECTED as a strict final style target; retain limited layout and equipment guidance.**

E01 is a coherent wide view of an electrical distribution room. It gives useful high-level cues for a left-side switchgear run, a central protected transformer, a right-side battery/transfer grouping, a rear workbench and a central keep-clear route. It reads as a complete room more effectively than the current S05 slice. The image is rendered as a photographic industrial visualization, however, with dense conduit/fastener detail, rich concrete/floor texture, realistic reflections and a broad neutral-orange treatment. That finish conflicts with the strict Valorant-style semi-realistic direction and the user’s requirement that the final room contain no teal.

The visible `11 KV / 400 V DRY TYPE` transformer text and any other electrical ratings in this concept are unrequested image content. They are rejected as specification. E01 has no authority over voltages, capacities, equipment dimensions, portal positions, interfaces or runtime behavior.
