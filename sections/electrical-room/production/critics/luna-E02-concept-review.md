**Reviewer:** Luna, independent electrical-room concept reviewer

## Disposition

**E02 overall: PARTIAL GUIDANCE / FINAL STYLE TARGET REJECTED.**

E02 is a stronger complementary composition than E01 for the strict room direction. The central negative space, graphite overhead busways, orange/charcoal switchgear, guarded transformer, reserve-battery edge and transfer panel make the distribution story easier to parse. The palette contains no visible teal and the forms are broader and more graphic than E01.

The image still leans toward a polished architectural visualization: skylight/daylight shafts, rich floor variation, soft photographic reflections, dense repeated hardware and cinematic contrast. The fixed room remains the authoritative **11.0 × 16.4 × 4.8 m main envelope with its reserved routes and equipment bounds**; E02’s apparent height, skylight, camera perspective and spaciousness cannot alter it. Generated slogans and image labels are visual noise for specification purposes. No text or ratings from E02 are adopted.

## Accepted guidance

- Keep the broad spatial read: a clear central working aisle, a strong switchgear wall, a visually protected transformer mass, and a distinct transfer/reserve side.
- Carry forward the graphite/oxide-orange/hazard-yellow/neutral-concrete grouping and absolute no-teal rule.
- Use the busway’s visible casing, bends and supports as a prompt for believable utility construction, while preserving the exact architecture centreline and headroom contract.
- Use floor drainage and sparse yellow safety edging as restrained route cues where they fit the saved layout.

## Rejected content and history

E02 does not supersede the electrical architecture, `interface.json`, the original electrical-room brief, the S05 baseline or the strict art direction. The image’s skylight, apparent room scale, exact camera, labels, slogans and any inferred equipment ratings are rejected as authority. The image suggests a complete-room composition, but no unseen TX/TD/RB/WB details or integration hooks may be credited from that suggestion. E01 remains rejected as a photographic final style target; E02 improves the graphic direction while remaining below the strict style threshold.

## Review result

**E02 concept result: PARTIAL / CONSTRAINED GUIDANCE.** Approve only the no-teal palette, switchgear silhouette, transformer guard composition, supported busway language, central route composition and sparse safety edging as visual guidance. Reject the photographic finish, skylight/exterior-light dependency, cropped reserve/repair evidence, generated slogans and all concept text as specification. No geometry or non-critic file was changed.
