## Disposition

**E08 overall: PARTIAL GUIDANCE / LAYOUT REJECTED.** E08 has a useful graphic overall read: a strong switchgear wall, a distinct transformer mass, a broad central floor and supported overhead bus. Its cream/charcoal/oxide-orange/yellow treatment contains no teal and is closer to the strict target than E01. The image places both visible doors on the far wall (`TURBINE` and `WASTE`), which conflicts directly with the authoritative electrical-room arrangement: D01 entry on the south boundary and D02 exit on the opposite north boundary. That layout is rejected and must not guide construction.

The exact room remains **11.0 × 16.4 × 4.8 m** with the reserved routes and equipment envelopes. E08’s apparent room width, door positions, camera perspective, light shafts and equipment spacing cannot change those values. Concept labels are visual cues only; no generated slogans, ratings or interface names are adopted.
