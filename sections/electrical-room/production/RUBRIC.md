# Independent review rubric

Each category is scored independently out of 100. Acceptance requires every relevant category >=90, zero critical defects, all ten cameras and objective checks passing, at least four complete review cycles, stable final two cycles, and fresh-process reproduction.

| Category | Weight | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Scale / circulation | 20 | Metric architecture, entry, route, reverse, pinch views and clearance checks |
| Shape / art direction | 20 | Specific manufactured forms, approved-reference comparison |
| Hierarchy | 15 | Traceable incoming/switchgear/distribution/essential supply, controlled density |
| Materials | 15 | Painted steel, concrete, rubber, bare metal, glass, fabric/paper response |
| Lighting | 10 | Practical falloff, focal guidance, readable secondary areas |
| Color | 5 | Restrained functional color fields and accent discipline |
| Environmental storytelling | 5 | Human work evidence and recovery affordances |
| Technical correctness | 10 | Editable source, supports, routes, dependencies, deterministic cold build/reopen |
