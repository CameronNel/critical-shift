# Electrical Room art authority

The current project target is grounded stylized semi-realism. The builder and independent Astra Ultra reviewer inspected the approved reactor generated references `reference-a02-hall.png` and `reference-b01-controls.png` from the active reactor worktree. Their material and lighting principles apply here; their geometry and layout were not imported or copied.

The original room uses a dark petrol switchgear bank, warm precast concrete, graphite rolled steel, rubbed metal contact points, nearly black insulating rubber, ochre standby equipment and subdued copper behind transformer guarding. Practical fluorescent work pools establish legibility; local repair lighting provides a warmer secondary focus. Wear is authored at grips, access corners and handling surfaces. Broad roughness/color variation is restrained; no photographic noise or universal glossy shader.

Construction hierarchy: fabricated sheet-metal shells and recesses; separate metering, breaker and lower ventilation compartments; bus casings, splice collars and threaded hangers; cast meter bezels; mechanical isolation handles; service cart wheels, folded trays and test leads. The shape vocabulary is specific to the device.

No new AI-generated reference was adopted. No third-party geometry, textures, fonts, HDRI, downloads or paid assets are required. Blender's bundled Bfont is used for sparse machine and navigation labels.
