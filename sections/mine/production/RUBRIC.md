# Gullet Mine — Visual / Production Rubric

**Current score:** unscored. A score without actual Blender pixels would be theatre.

Score each category 0–10 after reviewing all fixed cameras. Record evidence and the worst visible defect for every category below 9.

| Category | Weight | Acceptance target |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Art-direction match | 15 | Grounded stylized semi-realism; no primitive web-demo or glossy sci-fi read |
| Architecture / construction logic | 12 | Bay, portal, gate, supports and services look plausibly built and maintained |
| Geological form | 12 | Continuous purposeful mine excavation; no sphere/voxel/cartoon-rock read |
| Mine gameplay readability | 12 | Preparation, route, work faces, wet area and collapse progression read without walls of text |
| Primary / secondary forms | 10 | Important assets remain identifiable without tertiary decals |
| Materials | 8 | Rock, ground, concrete, painted metal, bare metal, rubber, timber and water separate clearly |
| Lighting / atmosphere | 8 | Constrained practical mine lighting with contact and readable hierarchy |
| Cart / haulage | 6 | Rails and carts feel functional, grounded and navigable |
| Progressive sector states | 6 | State changes are spatially meaningful and do not visibly overlap |
| Detail density / storytelling | 5 | Authored clusters and quiet space; not clutter spam |
| Technical cleanliness | 6 | No obvious floating objects, seams, clipping, missing materials or camera-hidden failures |

## Acceptance

- Weighted total must be at least 90/100 for final art acceptance.
- No category may score below 8/10.
- Art-direction match, construction logic, geology and gameplay readability must each score at least 9/10.
- A regression in a fixed camera blocks acceptance even when the aggregate score rises.
- Scores require cited render filenames / iteration evidence in `TASK_STATE.md` or a review log.
