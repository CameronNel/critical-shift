Review settings: Cycles HIP,32 samples, denoising enabled,1440×900, seed17, AgX Medium High Contrast. Every GPU invocation uses the shared facility GPU gate. Rendering does not save or mutate the delivered scene.

Exterior views demonstrate the owned opening geometry and approach visibility. They do not establish neighboring floor support, connected corridors or whole-facility playability. Dimensions and clearances come from saved-scene measurements; pixels establish appearance and readability. Neither substitutes for the other.
