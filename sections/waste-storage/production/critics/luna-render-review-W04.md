Visible scene mean: **75.1/100 — REJECT**. No visible category reaches 90. This is not an approval of the geometry PASS claims.

## Improvements versus W03

- C01/C03/C04 have a slightly clearer route read from the centre floor line, and more cask/handling silhouettes are visible from the overall cameras.
- C02 and W04 show more complete cask hardware and a more legible suspended chain/hoist assembly than the previous partial reads, although the orange vertical element still blocks the left cask and the hook is not shown carrying a load.
- C06 dry containers now show more visible lid rails, handles, fasteners, and a readable `DR02` marker instead of almost featureless box faces.
- C07 quarantine’s open lid and container supports read more clearly than the earlier occluded blockout.
- Floor wear patches and additional practical wall fixtures add limited use evidence while retaining the no-teal palette.

## Regressions / unresolved visible problems

- The scene still presents an over-clean, narrow material vocabulary. Most large surfaces are pale cream or smooth graphite, and the W10 reference’s stronger planar value structure and tactile separation are not reached.
- C01/C03/C04 remain architectural blockouts at overall scale: repeated cell frames and large blank wall panels dominate over functional equipment and human-use evidence.
- The large orange vertical utility remains visually disconnected in C02/C03/C04. No coherent utility or extraction route is visible from the affected views.
- C05 shows an empty cart platform and an unclear service/personnel approach. It does not visually resolve the reported route problem.
- C08 extraction is still a simple box, orange fan/duct, and two pale doors. Intake/exhaust continuity, sensor hardware, service access, and maintenance storytelling remain below the W05 reference.
- C09 inventory is an isolated terminal and analogue gauge against a plain wall; the W06 workbench cluster, scanner, papers, seal kit, and container context are not present in this render.
- C10 seal bench has a useful board and tools but remains a generic table/box with sparse surrounding context.
- W01 personnel, W02 receiving return, and W03 dispatch each show an almost entirely blank dark closed door. They are not useful visual proof of egress/receiving/dispatch operation, threshold depth, parked leaves, or service-network continuation.
- W04 cell service is dominated by a cask and divider. It shows component detail but not 1.4 m cell access, 0.9 m maintenance depth, or a clear hand-chain working approach.

## Technical status kept separate

The builder reports all current root geometry/dimensions/equipment/main-cart/turn/player-route checks passing, while internal component/support auditing is incomplete. Three prior service-route failures were addressed in source but the W04 pixels do not prove those fixes. The render set therefore remains visual-only evidence; no technical or integration PASS is inferred.

## Gate decision

**REJECT W04.** It is a measurable improvement over W03 in cask/container component detail and route-line readability, with no palette regression. The main structural visual deficit remains: generic blockout architecture and material response, flat lighting, incomplete utility/hoist storytelling, sparse human-use props, and unusable portal evidence. Re-review after the W05 structural/material pass is required.
