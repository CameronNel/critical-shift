## Directly observed defects

### C01 Entry

- The centre lane is legible and appears unobstructed, but the room reads as a sequence of pale partition walls rather than four distinct storage functions.
- Cell headers are large, unsupported-looking orange signs with low information value at this distance; they read as generic labels rather than integrated compliance/inventory affordances.
- Wall, floor, partitions, and fixtures share a narrow cream/charcoal response. The broad surfaces are unusually clean and lack the tactile construction variation shown by W07.
- Ceiling beams and repeated uprights create a regular kit rhythm. There is little secondary construction around thresholds, seals, guards, or service access.
- Lighting is even and diffuse. Practical fixtures provide little localized task hierarchy or contact depth.
- Human-use evidence is almost absent. The entry view does not communicate carts, inspection, monitoring, handling, or maintenance activity beyond silhouettes at the sides.

### C02 Casks

- The paired casks have recognizable cylindrical masses and lifting points, but ribs and collars read as repeated smooth strips with limited manufacturing specificity compared with W03/W04.
- A large orange vertical element at the right edge is visually disconnected from the shown cask/room utility context and competes with the casks.
- The suspended hoist/ring is visibly unfinished or disconnected: the hanging element does not establish a complete, mechanically legible chain/handling relationship in this crop.
- The casks, platforms, and enclosure share a nearly uniform satin/cream response. The result is cleaner and flatter than the matte-but-tactile W04/W07 target.
- The camera is cramped around the cell, so access depth and the route relationship cannot be read from this evidence.
- The small gauges and IDs are too weak to supply meaningful storytelling at this framing; the casks remain largely uncontextualized storage props.

### C08 Extraction

- The filtration skid is dominated by simple rectangular and cylindrical masses. The close view does not show the authored twin-filter service construction or connected airflow path evident in the W05 reference.
- The orange fan/duct treatment reads as a broad colour block. Its relationship to intake, exhaust, motor, and supported ducting is ambiguous from the pixels.
- The space around the skid is blank and pale, with no convincing maintenance depth, access hardware, guards, service tools, or selective wear.
- The single gauge is too small and low-contrast to establish operational state. The machine has weak focal hierarchy beyond its orange housing.
- Light is flat with a weak contact shadow; there is no strong practical pool or dark secondary plane to separate the assembly from the wall.

### C09 Inventory

- The terminal is a generic cream box on a plain table. It lacks the folded desk construction, analog dose meter, handheld scanner/cradle, paperwork, seal kit, tools, articulated task lamp, cable/raceway, and dry-container staging shown in W06.
- The monitor text is legible enough to suggest inventory, but the surrounding scene provides no physical process context or contamination-monitoring story.
- Material response is nearly uniform across table, monitor shell, wall, and floor; there is no convincing distinction between painted metal, glass/screen, paper, rubber, and desk surface.
- Composition leaves large empty areas with a weak focal hierarchy. Lighting does not create a practical task zone.
- The camera crop cannot prove the booth aperture, desk access, or personnel route.

## Additional gate notes

- No teal/cyan/blue/green accent is visible in these four renders, so the explicit colour veto is satisfied.
- The renders are cleaner and less glossy than W01, but they remain too generic and under-authored to qualify as the W07 graphic matte target or the W04–W06 component standard.
- Builder-identified problems are confirmed by pixels: generic/overly clean response, insufficient manufacturing detail, unsupported cell signs, disconnected utility routing, unfinished hoist chain, and sparse human-use props.
- The four cameras do not establish the final ten-camera baseline. C02 and C09 approach validity is already flagged for correction; do not compare them as a stable final baseline until the builder freezes corrected cameras.
- The Electrical/Waste seam, portal dimensions, cart clearances, support contacts, named hooks, source reproducibility, and cold-start behavior remain unverified by this image set.
