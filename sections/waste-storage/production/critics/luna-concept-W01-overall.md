The concept is useful as a warm industrial composition and material/palette reference, but it does not qualify for autonomous approval because Materials (84), Scale/Circulation (87), Shape/Art Direction (88), Storytelling (89), and technical evidence (45) are below 90.

## Directly observed defects

- The floor surface shows strong broad reflections and reads glossy in the foreground and centre lane.
- Front-right dry containers and left-side casks appear too large relative to nearby doors, booth glazing, guards, and the overhead crane system; exact geometry remains unverified.
- `SWL 32T` is a visible crane rating but is explicitly unsupported and must not be adopted as a gameplay/engineering fact.
- The storage presentation relies on repeated tall casks and repeated rectangular containers, producing a somewhat catalogue-like rhythm rather than distinct authored silhouettes for each waste function.
- The lighting is clean and readable, but the bright rear portal and foreground floor highlight compete with the central working zone.
- The scene has little visible human-scale maintenance evidence beyond the monitoring booth; operational storytelling is mostly labels and equipment.
- The render is highly detailed and photoreal in surface response. It risks the art-direction veto for modern AAA drift even though its construction logic and warm colour blocking are strong.

## Scope decision

Use only as a rejected/conditional visual reference for broad composition, warm concrete/graphite/ochre grouping, overhead handling, cell labelling, and maintained-facility intent. Do not treat it as an exact floorplan, dimensional authority, crane rating, portal proof, or final material target. Re-review any subsequent concept or render at fixed-camera pixel level and score every relevant category independently; approval remains withheld until all relevant categories visibly reach 90 or higher and technical evidence is supplied separately.
