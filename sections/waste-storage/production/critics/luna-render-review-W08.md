## Improvements visible versus W05/W06

- The former continuous low crossbar obstruction is no longer the dominant cell-front read. Cell openings, bay graphics, orange lower wall bands, and WS labels now separate the room's functions more effectively.
- W05–W07 provide real open-aperture evidence from the receiving, personnel, and dispatch sides without fabricating neighboring geometry.
- W01 visibly shows the personnel leaf swung open, and W08 shows the booth door leaf and operator station. These are useful local construction reads even though neighbor scenes remain unassembled.
- C07's explicit `QH01` label and quarantine treatment resolve the previous visual ambiguity between ordinary dry storage and hold inventory.
- The W05 filtration, cart, cask, inventory, and seal-service detail survives into W08, and the W10-compatible matte warm palette remains intact.

## Current visible failures and evidence limits

- C09 is an unusable almost-black render. This is a camera/render failure, not a stylistic choice, and prevents inventory coverage and a complete camera baseline.
- C01/C03/C04/W05/W07 wide views still rely on repeated pale bay walls and dark rails for most of their structure. The new graphics help, but the scene remains less specific and less visually layered than W10.
- C02/W04 still show a bare hanging hoist hook and a dominant orange vertical element whose relationship to a complete service route is not legible.
- C06/C07/W04 show the equipment, but their tight framing cannot demonstrate the 1.4m storage access or 0.9m maintenance depth. The expanded audit's W08 clearance failures are therefore consistent with the pixels; W09 source fixes are not yet proven by this set.
- C08 shows a more believable filter skid but not its entire intake/exhaust path, capped boundary relationship, or maintenance working envelope.
- W01–W03 are dark because neighboring sections are unassembled. They should remain complementary local aperture evidence only; they cannot prove downstream continuity.
- The wall and cell-panel variation is now less visibly blotchy than the rejected W06 direction, but it still reads as a repeated procedural texture across many surfaces rather than controlled broad matte construction wear.

## Top three changes before another visual gate

1. **Machinery and service legibility:** resolve the hoist's visible parked/end state and make the extraction/service routing read as one complete operating assembly in both close-up and wide views. C02, W04, and C08 currently show capable components without a fully legible room-level connection.
2. **Material response:** keep the matte warm palette, but replace the repeated noisy wall/cell texture impression with deliberate broad variation and stronger response separation between concrete, painted metal, rubber, glass, paper, and worn contact surfaces. C01, C03, C06, C08, and C10 are the clearest evidence.
3. **Reference and camera finish:** restore a valid C09 inventory render and carry W10's graphic bay hierarchy into the remaining wide/approach views so the room reads as a specific Valorant facility rather than a sequence of pale partitions. Preserve the no-teal palette and the already useful functional labels; do not use the unassembled void views as substitute neighbor geometry.

## Gate decision

**REJECT W08.** W08 is a clear visual improvement over W05, with real open-aperture evidence, better bay graphics, explicit quarantine identity, and stronger local equipment reads. It remains below 90 because C09 is unusable, wide architecture/materials are still generic, machinery routing and hoist state are incomplete, close-ups do not communicate required access, and the unassembled neighbor views cannot establish continuation. Re-review W09 pixels after its clearance fixes and a valid full camera baseline.
