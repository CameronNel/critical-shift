## View-specific defects

### Main overall and route views

- **C01 Entry:** The centre lane is clear, but both sides read as pale generic bins/partitions. Large orange cell headers are unsupported-looking and provide little functional information. The repeated ceiling grid, clean walls, and evenly spaced dividers create a kit-like rhythm. Cask, cart, monitoring, and inspection activity are barely legible.
- **C03 Reverse:** The return view repeats the same generic cell rhythm. A prominent orange vertical utility element at the right is not visibly connected to a coherent service path. The dark rear door and “ELECTRICAL / RECEIVING” label make the portal relationship ambiguous.
- **C04 Route:** The route remains readable, but the left extraction/jib area and right open container are visually shallow. The raised lid/panel at right and foreground divider interrupt the service context. The overhead hoist chain does not establish a complete working relationship with a load.

### Functional close-ups

- **C02 Casks:** Repeated cask ribs and collars are smooth and mechanically generic compared with W09. The suspended chain/hook and the large orange vertical element feel unfinished or disconnected. Gauges/IDs are visible but small and do not communicate exposure/inventory state.
- **C05 Transfer:** The cart deck and lift saddle are readable, but the camera shows an empty handling platform with no secured waste load. The staged cart sits close to a cell wall and the frame does not establish a clean personnel/service approach. The builder-reported parked-cart route failure is therefore not contradicted by this image.
- **C06 Dry:** The dry container has a simple lid/handle read but is largely blocked by the dark divider. There is insufficient visible differentiation between dry contaminated tools/filters and the other cells.
- **C07 Quarantine:** The open-lid quarantine container is heavily occluded by the divider and has little surrounding evidence of quarantine control, inspection, or inventory hold.
- **C08 Extraction:** Filtration reads as a broad orange fan housing plus a box with two pale doors. The intake/exhaust path is not visually continuous, the utility routing appears disconnected, and the wall/service zone is empty. It lacks the physically coherent, detail-rich W05 skid language.
- **C09 Inventory:** The inventory terminal and dose gauge now read, but the scene remains sparse: no desk/workbench context, scanner cradle, seal kit, paperwork cluster, or container staging at the W06 standard. Screen and gauge are low contrast against the beige scene.
- **C10 Workbench:** The seal-service board and tools provide useful purpose, yet the bench is a simple table with a generic box and nearly empty wall. It does not carry the tactile construction, wear, and human-use density shown by W06.

### Portal and cell-service evidence

- **W01 Personnel:** Nearly the entire frame is a blank dark door leaf. It does not show the required outward egress context, nearby route, human-scale approach, or medical/reanimation intent; it is an invalid visual proof of that portal.
- **W02 ReceivingReturn:** A closed dark sectional door fills the frame. The threshold, three-metre receiving clear width, parked overhead leaf, cart approach, and opposite room relationship are not visible.
- **W03 Dispatch:** Same failure as W02 from the dispatch end: a closed door panel with no readable service-network continuation, cart turn, or clearance context. The pair cannot prove two usable opposing portals.
- **W04 CellService:** The cask fills the frame and shows some clamps/gauge/contact wear, but the divider blocks most access context. It does not prove 1.4 m cell access, 0.9 m maintenance depth, or the jib hand-chain approach.

## Known non-pixel blockers retained

The builder reports three service-route validation failures: parked cart/personnel clearance, residue-row access spacing, and jib hand-chain placement at the service approach. Fixes were authored but not yet built in the W03 source. These are technical/geometry blockers in addition to the visible defects above; they are not converted into an invented numeric technical score here.

## Gate decision

**REJECT W03.** The set establishes a useful layout blockout and a warm no-teal palette, but every visible category is below 90. The highest-impact visible problems are generic under-authored architecture, weak material separation, flat lighting, incomplete/ambiguous utility and hoist construction, insufficient inventory/workbench storytelling, heavy occlusion in side-cell views, and unproven opposing portal reads. Re-review is required after the current W04 material/lighting/silhouette pass and after the three route fixes are actually built and rendered.
