# Luna review — W11 inventory booth component concept

## Scope

This is a direct pixel review of `art/concepts/W11.png` as complementary inventory-booth/component guidance. It is not evidence for the room plan, portal transforms, door states, or engine integration. The authored plan and `architecture/REQUIREMENTS.md` remain authoritative for placement and orientation.

## Visible read

The desk workflow is clear and useful: terminal, analog dose gauge, hand scanner, receipt log, checklist, seal-tag tray, task bins, keyboard, and chair all communicate an active inventory station at human scale. The booth glazing gives a strong operational sightline into the storage apron, and the broad matte warm concrete, cream, oxide, and charcoal treatment fits the approved Valorant direction. Lighting is practical and shaped, with enough value separation to keep the desk readable.

The image does **not** show the requested south receiving opening through the glazing. It visibly shows a labeled `DISPATCH` opening and `QUARANTINE HOLD` area through glass. Therefore its implied south receiving orientation is rejected. That mismatch cannot authorize a camera, portal, cell, or layout change.

Minor visible issues remain in the reference itself: repeated faction-style logos are unsupported by the prompt's “no logos” constraint, and the potted plant reads as decorative clutter in an otherwise purposeful control desk. The signage and desk paperwork are useful visual evidence, while the exact labels and orientation must not be copied as plan authority.
