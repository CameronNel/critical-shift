## Measured evidence reviewed separately

- `production/validation/W20/technical.json` reports `pass: true` for material assignments, finite geometry, equipment inventory, footprint, fixed cameras, registered roots/supports, player routes, cart routes/envelope, portal openings, connection markers, saved source bytes, and external dependencies.
- `motion_process.json` reports `pass: true` for the personnel leaf, monitor booth leaf, vent process joints, and intake/exhaust separation. This is static pose/AABB and joint evidence; it is not physics or CFD proof.
- `contact_candidates.json` reports revision W20 with one connected component and no unanchored components at the stated tolerance. This supports anchoring/readiness but is not exact mesh-contact proof.
- `detail.json` reports `pass: false` solely at `maintenance_boxes`. The `VF01_filters` maintenance face is obstructed by `Vent isolator branch` (reported face approximately `[-5.65,14.46,.025]` to `[-3.1,15.36,1.9]`); the other listed maintenance boxes have no obstruction objects.
- The full W20 source replay exposed a shared helper name being overwritten by a shader-link loop. That issue is fixed and W21 replay is running, so no W20 reproducibility claim is made here. The W21 utility-curve envelope change is pending and receives no credit.

The measured route, opening, support, and motion passes justify the strong local layout score. They cannot override the failed filter maintenance box or replace pixel inspection of loose utility lines and material response.

## Remaining visible issues

1. The extraction utility curves/feeds in C08 and the booth/inventory feed in C09/W08 still look under-supported or terminate ambiguously in the rendered images. Their authored continuity and maintenance envelope need to agree both in the pixels and in the measured checks.
2. The W20 `VF01_filters` maintenance obstruction is a concrete integration blocker. W21's pending utility-envelope work is relevant, but no pass is credited until the saved artifact and detail audit agree.
3. The broad room views retain repeated pale bay surfaces and a generally smooth response. W20 is materially closer to W10, but the anti-plastic separation is the only visual category below 90.

## Gate decision

**REJECT W20 for final approval.** The rendered direction is strong and most visual categories clear 90, with especially good machinery, local aperture, label, and lighting evidence. The rejection is caused by the failed `VF01_filters` maintenance box, visibly unresolved utility routing in C08/C09/W08, and the still-pending source replay reproducibility. W01–W03 dark neighbor views are recorded as unbound-interface evidence limits, not as Waste-module defects. Re-review after W21's measured maintenance/utility correction and a successful full source replay.
