# Luna bounded evidence-gap audit — W05 validation and architecture

## Scope

This is a read-only audit of `architecture/REQUIREMENTS.md`, `architecture/interface.json`, the current `blender/validate_scene.py`, and the W05 source path. It identifies evidence gaps that can affect integration readiness. It does not alter geometry or prescribe coordinate recipes. Visual art scoring remains separate from technical validation; a clean validator result cannot turn an under-90 render set into approval.

## What the current audit establishes

The script checks non-empty material slots, finite evaluated bounds, a floor footprint, an equipment-type count dictionary, fourteen camera objects at player-height Z, registered root support rays, conservative player AABB sweeps, a straight cart envelope, square turn envelopes, three exterior portal ray samples, connection-marker existence and exact marker centers, authoring-source byte hashes, and library/image dependency state. Its own report correctly limits the result to evaluated geometry and root anchors rather than engineering certification or whole-map integration.

Those checks are useful, but they do not yet establish several requirements stated in the architecture brief.

## Consequential gaps

| ID | Coverage gap | Why it matters for this room |
|---|---|---|
| G01 | **Equipment taxonomy contradicts the authored count.** `REQUIREMENTS.md` calls for 2 dry containers plus 1 quarantine container. `full_hall.py` gives `DR01`, `DR02`, and `QH01` the same `equipment_type='dry_container'`, while `validate_scene.py` expects 3 dry containers. | A passing count can conceal a missing quarantine class. The validator needs semantic evidence for exactly one quarantine object, its state/interaction metadata, hiding volume behavior, and the `INSPECT_QUARANTINE` target, separately from ordinary dry-container count. |
| G02 | **The internal monitor-booth opening is absent from aperture checks.** `portal_openings` samples receiving, dispatch, and personnel only. | The glazed booth leaf, its 1.1×2.3m opening, parked 90° state, and operator-side approach can still be blocked or misoriented while all three exterior portal checks pass. |
| G03 | **Door and gate motion envelopes are not tested.** There is no evaluated sweep for the outward personnel leaf, the parked overhead receiving/dispatch leaves, the monitor-booth leaf, or the retracting cell gates. | Static rays do not prove clearance in the authored open/parked states. Door thresholds and cell access can conflict with the cart lane, carry path, or maintenance approach even when a centerline ray is clear. |
| G04 | **Clearance evidence uses a 0.6m player AABB for every branch.** The brief also requires 1.0m walking clearance, 1.8m two-person carry clearance, 1.4m storage access, and at least 0.9m maintenance depth. | The current route check cannot prove a worker carrying a long item, a pair of workers, or a maintainer can reach the filter, casks, container faces, bench, inventory station, and jib. The cart check covers one straight envelope and square turn bounds only; it does not test the rectangular cart/load through branch approaches or a true turn. |
| G05 | **Utility markers are existence/position checks, not continuity checks.** The marker loop compares `IF_*` object locations with `interface.json`; it does not inspect marker normals, sleeve dimensions, wall penetration, or an endpoint graph. | The authored extract network may look connected while stopping short of `WS_EXTRACT`, crossing a wall, facing the wrong way, or failing to leave a usable capped boundary. The same evidence gap applies to the `WS_POWER` and `WS_DATA` sockets and the low-voltage/no-false-Electrical-U02 semantic boundary. |
| G06 | **Portal dimensions are hardcoded in the validator rather than cross-checked to the contract.** | A source/interface drift can pass the ray samples if the hardcoded values remain unchanged. Receiving, dispatch, and personnel clear dimensions need to be derived or compared against the current contract, including the inherited .32m wall depth and owned 2mm lip decision. |
| G07 | **Sign and label fit is not validated.** FONT/CURVE objects are included in bounds, but there is no check that portal identifiers, cell labels, warning plates, or monitor text fit their backing surfaces, remain readable from the fixed cameras, and do not float or penetrate wall/gate geometry. | W05 specifically depends on readable cell and service signage. A text object can have a material and finite bounds while being clipped, unsupported, reversed, or too small to serve navigation and prevention requirements. |
| G08 | **Support checks stop at registered assembly roots.** Internal fixture, conduit, pipe, chain, desk, sign, paper, and hardware children are not individually checked for contact, attachment, or accidental float. | Root contact can pass while a visible service line, filter fitting, hoist chain, or desk prop is disconnected. This is especially consequential for the physical utility-routing and maintenance-detail corrections in W05. |
| G09 | **Geometry validity is narrower than the visual/structural requirement.** There is no explicit non-manifold, inverted-normal, self-intersection, duplicate-overlap, hidden-object, hinge-pivot, or wall-penetration audit. | AABB/BVH bounds and aperture rays can miss a bad local seam, coplanar z-fight, self-intersection, or a prop that visually floats or clips into a guard. Those defects need either a dedicated evaluated-geometry check or direct render evidence. |
| G10 | **Camera validation checks count and Z only.** It does not check lens/framing, clipping, target validity, portal/cell/equipment coverage, or rendered stability after reopening the saved artifact. | Fourteen camera objects do not prove that the W05 approach corrections actually show the receiving, personnel, dispatch, booth, cells, filtration, inventory, and service access. Prior W03/W04 evidence included blank or occluded approaches despite the camera-count check. |
| G11 | **Runtime contract hooks are not audited.** Source creates stable-looking audio, incident, interaction, inspection, and navigation empties, but the validator does not check their presence, unique IDs, target ownership, or consistency with object metadata. | The Blender artifact can be visually complete while engine handoff lacks a stable inventory, quarantine, ventilation, breach, audio, or portal hook. The brief explicitly limits Blender to delivering physical source plus hooks; that handoff needs evidence. |
| G12 | **Dependency check omits fonts.** It reports font paths but only enforces packed/generated image/library conditions. | A required label font can remain external and fail on a cold reopen even when the dependency check passes. Text assets should be packed, generated, or otherwise explicitly accounted for. |
| G13 | **The fresh headless validator opens the saved artifact, but a full cold repeat/fingerprint is still pending.** Source hashes prove selected script bytes, and the marker test reads the local JSON; the current report does not rerun every camera after a clean process, compare render/scene fingerprints, or compare the full scene contract and hook topology. | Early integration readiness needs repeat evidence that the saved artifact remains stable after a clean load and that rendered output, interface IDs, and hook topology still match after dependency/context loss. |

## Priority for the next evidence pass

The highest-impact gaps are G01 (quarantine semantics), G02–G04 (internal opening, dynamic door/gate states, and required human/cart/maintenance envelopes), G05–G06 (actual utility continuity and contract-derived openings), and G10–G11 (camera coverage and handoff hooks). G07–G09 and G12–G13 then close the visible construction and repeatable cold-start/fingerprint gaps. These are evidence requirements; they do not change the authored dimensions, orientation, or equipment plan.

## Luna status

The current validation script is **not sufficient by itself for integration-readiness approval**. This audit records missing proof only; it does not claim that each corresponding scene element is absent. W05 visual review and the formal full-camera baseline remain separate gates.
