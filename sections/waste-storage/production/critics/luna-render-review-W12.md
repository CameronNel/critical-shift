## Improvements visible versus W08

- C09 is restored from the prior almost-black failure and now provides usable inventory evidence.
- C08 is wider and shows a more complete filtration assembly, including separated intake/exhaust treatment, cassette rims, pressure lines, fan, and isolator.
- W12's warm wall grazing creates clearer practical light pools and value separation across the overall and close-up views.
- Metric texture treatment reduces the scale drift and blotchy repetition seen in earlier passes; surfaces now read more consistently matte.
- C01/C03/C04 and W05–W07 retain the corrected open cell-front and actual aperture presentation without the W05 continuous low-crossbar obstruction.
- W07 quarantine identity is clear and no unsupported nuclear rating appears. The empty cart remains a valid operational presentation.

## Remaining visible failures and limits

- **C01 framing remains incomplete:** it does not yet give the full booth/apron relationship, so W11 inventory guidance is only partly represented. W13's wider lens is pending and receives no credit here.
- **C02/W04 contact specificity is incomplete:** cask trunnion/weld, suspension, and gauge bracket joins remain visually simplified. W13's pending contact pass is not credited.
- **C08 contact specificity is incomplete:** the filter adapter/cassette connections are clearer than W08 but still read as simplified joins at close range. Full endpoint continuity and service clearance are not visible.
- **C09 is usable but under-dressed relative to W11:** monitor, gauge, log, keyboard, and box are present, while the handheld scanner/seal workflow is not clearly visible in this camera.
- **C10 remains a generic isolated bench vignette:** useful tools are present, but the bench has limited surrounding context and weak construction specificity.
- **W02/W03 remain dark unbound-interface views:** this is not treated as a false closed-leaf claim or an owned-Waste defect. It only limits evidence of downstream scene continuity, which is outside this local module's authored scope.
- **Wide architecture remains repetitive:** oxide bands and labels help, yet C01/C03/C04/W05/W07 still present long runs of similar pale panels and generic beams rather than the full W10 graphic hierarchy.
- **Clearance status stays separate:** W09's expanded 1m walking, 1.8m carry, 0.9m service, and dense-aperture checks are measured evidence. These PNGs do not prove those numbers, especially in C05–C08 and W04.

## Gate decision

**REJECT W12.** W12 is the strongest complete set so far and resolves the W08 black inventory failure, improves filtration continuity/read, adds warmer wall grazing, and preserves the approved no-teal matte direction. It remains below the 90 gate because C01 coverage is incomplete, close-up contact detail remains weak, C09/C10 are still sparse against W11, and the local wide architecture is repetitive. W02/W03's unbound-neighbor limitation is recorded without assigning it as an owned-Waste defect. Re-review after W13 contact corrections and wider C01 framing.
