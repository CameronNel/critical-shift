## Improvements visible versus W04

- C02 and W04 show more deliberate cask collars, gauges, shoes, fasteners, seal controls, and hoist-chain detail.
- C05 is a more specific and believable transfer cart. Its empty platform is operationally valid; the remaining issue is the obstructed/unclear approach around it.
- C08 is materially improved from the earlier broad filtration block: paired filter doors, latches, gauge, fan, belt, and cabinet details read clearly.
- C09 and C10 now have purposeful inventory and repair props rather than isolated featureless boxes.
- Floor wear is broad and matte enough to avoid the earlier glossy-floor direction, and the palette remains free of teal/cyan/blue/green.

## Regressions and unresolved visible problems

- The lowered dark crossbars/rails visibly span the fronts of all four cell approaches. They are most obvious in C02, C05, C06, C07, and W04, and remain apparent in the overall views. This is a physical-looking obstruction, not merely camera occlusion, and is already slated for removal in W06.
- C01/C03/C04 still read as repeated pale partitions and blank wall panels at room scale. The W10 reference's graphic bay structure and stronger material separation are not yet present.
- C02/C03/C04 retain a visually dominant orange vertical element whose utility relationship is not legible as a continuous service route.
- The hoist chain is visible but ends in an unresolved bare hook. This is a completion/state problem; the absence of a cart load is not being treated as a defect.
- C06/C07 cell close-ups are dominated by the crossbar, so they do not prove readable access, quarantine separation, or a useful maintenance approach.
- C08 is a good component improvement but remains too tightly framed to show the complete extraction path or service clearance.
- C09/C10 provide useful prop guidance but still feel like isolated stations against plain walls, with weak booth, room, and operator circulation context.
- W01 personnel, W02 receiving, and W03 dispatch remain blank closed portals. They do not visually demonstrate outward egress, open/parked overhead shutters, threshold depth, or service-network continuation.

## Technical status kept separate

This image review does not score geometry dimensions, route tests, utility endpoint continuity, support contacts, stable IDs, or engine hooks. The lowered-crossbar defect is visible and independently disqualifying; pending W06 removal must be re-rendered in the formal baseline. W05 remains historical evidence and receives no approval.

## Gate decision

**REJECT W05.** W05 is a real component-detail improvement over W04, especially for the cart, filters, inventory, bench, and casks. It remains below the 90 gate because the crossbars visibly obstruct all four cell approaches, portal evidence is unusable for operation, overall architecture and materials remain generic, and utility/hoist/maintenance storytelling is incomplete. Re-review W06 pixels after the structural correction and camera baseline.
