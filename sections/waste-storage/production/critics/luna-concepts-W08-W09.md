# Luna pixel review — W08 and W09

Direct pixel review of the supplied concept sheets. These are visual/construction assessments only. Generated labels and dimensions are not authoritative: the Waste Storage plans and interface control placement, portal sizes, envelope, cell access, and modeled container dimensions. No integration or runtime approval is issued from either sheet.

## W08 observations

- The level-floor correction is visible: the room now reads as a broad, dry, matte industrial hall with low concrete cells, clear central lane, monitoring booth, floor-level extraction equipment, hand truck, hose/tool dressing, casks, overpacks, and dry containers.
- The broad planar wall treatment, dark structural framing, restrained oxide-orange bands, warm practical pools, and limited surface noise are materially closer to the W07 graphic Valorant target than the W02 preview renders. No teal/cyan/blue/green accent is visible.
- The left (south/receiving) view presents a blank rear wall branded “WASTE STORAGE” rather than a clearly visible opposite dispatch aperture. The right view presents a central door labelled “DISPATCH”, but the pair does not make the two required opposing portals unambiguous. Annotation text cannot substitute for a visible portal. This is a room-design contradiction for a two-ended receiving/dispatch hall and is the reason for rejection.
- The low cells and broad centre route read well, but the repeated barrels/casks and box containers still lean generic at this distance. The monitoring booth and hand truck help, while human-use/inspection evidence remains light.
- The floor is matte and much less reflective than earlier plates. Concrete and painted steel separate reasonably, though some cell walls and floor surfaces still share a similar low-contrast response.
- The composition is clean and readable, but the two-panel presentation and large annotation band make it a design sheet rather than evidence for a fixed in-game camera. No cart sweep, door leaf, personnel egress, or maintenance depth can be proven.
- The “4 × low concrete cells (1.1 m high)” and cask diameter annotations are treated as generated claims only. Modeled dimensions remain governed by the current architecture requirements and inherited interface.

W08 visual direction is conditionally useful; W08 as the complete room concept is rejected until both opposing portals are actually present and readable in the room design. No coordinate or replacement-geometry recipe is prescribed here.

## W09 observations

- The main tall cask and shorter residue overpack have clear differentiated silhouettes and a convincing hierarchy. The sheet shows stepped bolted lids, forged lifting eyes, side trunnions, over-centre toggle clamps, a flush sample port, rib/guard panels, skid/fork channels, and contact bases.
- The close-up panels make the closure and handling logic readable. The portable casks are independent; the prior undesired pipe joining them is absent.
- Material response is tactile and controlled: matte cream painted shell, dark structural guards/seals, restrained oxide-orange hardware, and exposed contact metal. It aligns well with the approved W04/W07 direction, though the close-up still has more realistic surface detail than a final gameplay render needs.
- Generated dimension labels (`H 2.3 m Ø 1.4 m`, `H 1.3 m Ø 1.2 m`) are not adopted as authority. The planned Waste Storage container dimensions and interface requirements control implementation.
- The upper-right exterior vignette and slogan are useful mood/context only. They do not establish an exterior Waste Storage opening or room boundary and must not override the interior room contract.
- The sheet contains no teal/cyan accent and no unsupported lifting rating. The cask IDs and feature callouts support storytelling, but the board cannot prove actual pivots, support anchors, storage-cell access, or cart handling.

W09 passes as a scoped visual/construction reference for casks and residue overpacks. It does not pass as dimensional, portal, runtime, or whole-room evidence.

## Integration status

Both sheets leave measured portal alignment, the Electrical-to-Waste seam, utility sockets, cart/turn envelopes, door sweeps, support contacts, named hooks, reproducibility, and cold-start behavior unverified. W08’s visible portal inconsistency is a design rejection; W09’s generated dimensions and vignette are simply non-authoritative scope exclusions.
