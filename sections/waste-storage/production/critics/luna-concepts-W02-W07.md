Measured integration, portals, clearance, support anchors, source reproducibility, runtime hooks, and cold-start behavior are **unverified**, rather than scored as image failures. ## Plate observations

### W02 reverse

The reverse camera has a readable central route toward the receiving door, a glazed monitoring booth, ventilation equipment, cell fencing, casks, and side service zones. The warm concrete/graphite/oxide palette is compliant and contains no teal/cyan/blue/green accent. The image still reads close to a polished realistic render: repeated crate/cage masses and a somewhat reflective floor lower strict shape/material scores. Equipment at both foreground edges visually narrows the route even though the centre remains open. No person, cart envelope, or door-sweep evidence is visible. Use as a rejected reverse-composition reference only.

### W03 casks

The casks have strong authored primary/secondary forms: stepped lids, trunnions, clamps, fluted ribs, skid bases, and a dose-sample port. Material separation and warm light are clear. The large horizontal pipe visibly joins the two otherwise portable casks. Under the current storage-cell design this reads as a fixed process line, weakening segregation and making the portable role ambiguous. This is a design contradiction independent of the otherwise high component scores. The plate also remains more photoreal and texture-forward than the graphic matte target. Reject.

### W04 cart/jib

The cart reads as a believable manually pushed transfer unit: handle, folded deck, crossrails, caster forks, rubber wheels, brake pedals, lift saddle, and restraint hardware are visually distinct. The floor jib has a coherent column/boom/hand-chain/hook assembly, and the hook is visibly carried by its chain. Scale relationships are plausible for the stated cart envelope. The close-up uses a realistic PBR response and cinematic framing, so this is approved only as a component design reference whose authored forms and warm palette can inform the room. It does not prove the room route, handling clearances, or runtime interaction.

### W05 filtration

This is a strong functional machinery plate. Twin gasketed filter doors, clamps, hopper-like lower sections, centrifugal volute, separate motor/coupling, bolted base, intake/exhaust duct, isolation crank, differential gauge, and power isolator are all visible. The airflow path reads continuously from intake through filtration and exhaust. Front service access is legible. The restrained cream/charcoal/oxide palette and practical side lighting work well; the image remains slightly more rendered/realistic than the strict graphic target but does not show the glossy-floor problem of W01. Approved as a scoped filtration component reference, not as an exact placement or connection proof.

### W06 inventory/dry container

The strongest human-use storytelling plate in this set. The workstation visibly supports inventory and exposure work through a CRT-like terminal, physical keyboard, analogue dose-rate meter with separate needle, handheld scanner and coiled lead, logbook, stamped tags, seal kit, tools, articulated task lamp, chair, and cable/raceway details. The dry-filter container has a distinct closed state and an open empty state, with clamps, gasketed lid, lifting pockets, and internal volume. Desk, chair, rubber mat, and container proportions read adult-scale. The prop density is purposeful rather than random, though the high surface realism should be moderated in final room renders. Approved as a scoped component/storytelling reference only.

### W07 corrected overall

This is the clearest overall visual target: broad matte planar surfaces, crisp value blocking, restrained oxide-orange bands, quiet concrete, dark structural steel, readable cell labels, and a clearly open centre lane. It satisfies the no-teal override and is materially closer to the required Valorant-influenced stylized semi-realism than W01/W02. The monitoring booth, cell partitioning, cask/overpack grouping, quarantine hold, rear filtration area, and purposeful hand truck/tool station communicate the room at a glance. The dispatch opening is depicted as an exterior scene and the filtration skid sits on a raised balcony; both are explicitly invented for this plate and are excluded from Waste Storage adoption. Those exclusions are scope decisions, not a request for a coordinate redesign. Pass the visual direction; reject the plate as a complete room design until those contradictions are absent and the actual room evidence exists.

## Integration status

All six concepts leave exact interface and runtime integration unverified. The actual room must still prove the planned 12×18×4.8 m envelope, receiving/personnel/dispatch portals, electrical seam contract, centre cart route and turn circles, storage access/maintenance depths, utility sockets, support contacts, named cameras, source reproducibility, and fresh-process checks. These concepts cannot supply that proof.
