# Original visual development and rejection history

All concept images were generated with ChatGPT image generation for this assignment. They are visual guidance, not geometry or dimension authority. All Blender meshes, curves, materials, text and lighting were authored originally; no downloaded or neighboring scene geometry was imported. Primitive authoring helpers were adapted from this builder's Electrical section source.

Directly inspected style references included the approved reactor hall/control imagery, approved mine Valorant imagery, and the completed Electrical direction. The user's strict Valorant and **no teal** instruction overrides their other palettes. The retained direction uses warm concrete, ivory/oxide painted steel, charcoal, rubber, metal and purposeful practical lights.
