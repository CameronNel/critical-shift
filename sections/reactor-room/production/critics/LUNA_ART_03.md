# Luna art critique 03 — corrected camera baseline

Independent visual review of `art-03`, completed after inspecting all ten renders once. Comparison set: `reference-a02-hall.png`, `reference-b01-controls.png`, Art 01 and Art 02. Camera purpose was judged from `production/CAMERAS.md`: entry, control, stair and high views are evaluated for their stated subjects; the bank detail view is not required to include the pool; the enclosed stair view is not required to prove architectural adjacency.

Art 03 is a better reviewable batch than Art 02. The entry lane is less obstructed, the hero frame retains the vertical bank relationship, the control-room camera is in the intended observation zone, and the high view carries more of the room. The control glazing has an obvious optical defect, however, and the underlying material/shape problems remain. The pass is **FAIL**.

## Camera evidence

### 01 HERO

The portrait frame is materially better than Art 02 for the main subject. Both banks, their upper housings, carriages and long columns are visible together with the recessed pool and south gate. The cyan pool now owns the lower frame. The upper support structure is close to the top boundary, and the pool still has a uniform cyan wall-band look instead of strong lower-source depth. The control glazing at right is visible but carries the same optical haze seen in 06.

### 02 WEST_ENTRY

The corrected lane removes the large Art 02 foreground obstruction. The room reads as an entry, with the pool ring, perimeter equipment, control glazing and overhead bank relationship visible. The low perspective truncates parts of the upper banks at the top edge, but the subject remains understandable. Surface response is cleaner and darker than Art 02 in places, yet equipment silhouettes remain generic and lightly worn.

### 03 SOUTH_GATE

The emergency gate, acknowledgement button and separate red control remain clearly visible. The pool is bright and readable behind the rail, with better local shadows than earlier passes. The wall/floor/material split is still soft, and the shaft remains a uniform cyan volume. The gate assembly is a strong functional read in this camera.

### 04 TURBINE_AISLE

The corrected aisle is open, with the turbine-side equipment and route readable. Practical falloff is more evident than Art 02. The right turbine remains cropped as a detail edge, which is acceptable for an aisle subject but reduces complete machinery evidence. The equipment still shares repeated teal/white materials and the floor arrows remain flat graphic markings.

### 05 REVERSE_NORTH

The pool ring and paired columns read cleanly, with a stronger room depth gradient than Art 02. The reverse perimeter remains a row of small, similar teal machines. Pipework and wall panels are clearer than the first pass but still too clean and uniform for the reference’s maintained industrial feel.

### 06 CONTROL_ROOM

The camera is no longer blocked by the large foreground console, so the intended observation position is visible. The glazing itself is visibly defective: a broad milky/opaque band and inverted-looking reflections smear across the middle and lower field, while the room beyond is distorted and partially washed out. This is a direct image failure in glazing readability and environmental credibility. The upper field shows the reactor and perimeter, but the optical defect prevents a clean control-room read.

### 07 COMPACT_STAIR

The enclosed stair is readable as its own subject. Treads, dark stringer, yellow edge lines and handrails are clearer than Art 02, and the exposure is less muddy. The space still has very little material variation and no visible context beyond the enclosure. Adjacency is not scored from this view per the camera brief.

### 08 MATERIAL_SLICE

The shift-check sheet is now fully legible and gives the close-up a small human-use anchor. The white pipe route, yellow wheels, teal vessel and ribbed turbine are clear, but they still look like smooth simplified surface families. The wall panels have broad value shifts but no convincing localized service wear. The close-up is cleaner than Art 02 without becoming materially tactile.

### 09 BANK_MECHANISMS

This is a valid dedicated bank detail view and is judged on the bank assembly only. The fixed upper bodies, lower carriages and two drive columns are clear. The stronger contrast helps the silver trim and dark underside read. The repeated cylinder-and-panel language remains obvious, with large blank fronts, identical bands and small labels doing most of the work. It is a clear two-bank image but not yet a high-specificity industrial mechanism image.

### 10 EAST_HIGH

The wider 4:3 frame is a real improvement. It shows the pool, south gate area, paired banks, perimeter equipment and circulation relationship in one room-level image. The banks approach the top edge but are substantially more complete than Art 02, and the pool is no longer cut off at the bottom. The room remains pale and sparse at distance; the control glazing region still carries optical haze and the perimeter machines collapse into repeated teal blocks.

- View 06 is in the intended observation zone rather than behind the console, but the control glass has a severe milky/inverted optical defect.
- View 10 now retains the full room relationship with less top/bottom cropping.
- View 01 is a stronger hero composition with both bank assemblies, the pool and the emergency gate held together.
- View 07 is more readable as an enclosed stair subject; adjacency remains deferred as specified.
- The room’s practical light falloff and structural darks are stronger than Art 02.
- The cyan pool reads more clearly, but its shaft remains evenly illuminated rather than strongly depth-layered.
- Materials remain too smooth and uniform, especially white pipework, bank shells, consoles and pale flooring.
- Bank close-up construction remains repetitive and label-dependent.
- Human-use evidence is still sparse beyond the shift-check sheet and functional labels.
- Reproduction is unassessed because `cold_start` is false on this run; no technical credit is awarded from assurances.

## Decision

**FAIL — art-03 is a stronger review batch but not approval-ready.** The camera corrections materially improve the evidence, and the room now has a credible cyan pool/two-bank hierarchy from the applicable room views. The control glazing visibly fails, materials remain too smooth and generic, the pool lacks convincing lower-source depth, environmental use is sparse, and the technical reproduction gate remains unassessed. The next review must use fresh ten-view evidence after the glazing defect is resolved and must include the final cold-start check before technical approval can be considered.
