Independent visual review of `art-05`, completed after inspecting all ten renders once. Comparison set: `reference-a02-hall.png`, `reference-b01-controls.png`, Art 04, and the previous corrected-camera batch.

Art 05 is a clear recovery from the Art 04 wear regression. The broad speckled side-face pattern is gone. Remaining wear is sparse and mostly reads as small edge chips around panels and lower trim. The pool/two-bank hierarchy and room framing remain strong. The pass is still **FAIL** because the scene remains below the visual 90 gate, the control glazing still carries visible reflection haze/ghosting, the banks retain generic construction, and final technical reproduction is pending.

## Camera evidence

### 01 HERO

The best room-level composition remains intact: two complete bank assemblies, long columns, recessed cyan pool, rail, south gate and east glazing all read together. The Art 04 side-face speckles are gone. Small edge wear is visible around carriage panel seams and lower trim without dominating. The pool is bright and deep enough to read as a hazard, but its cyan remains broadly even through the shaft and the east glass carries visible reflections.

### 02 WEST_ENTRY

The cleared entry lane remains a strong improvement. The room reads as a large industrial hall with the pool ring and perimeter stations visible. The bank assemblies are partly compressed toward the upper frame but remain identifiable. Materials are calmer than Art 04, with no broad-face speckling; the architectural shell remains smooth and lightly differentiated.

### 03 SOUTH_GATE

The emergency gate, acknowledgement control, bypass/SCRAM control and pool rail remain legible. The yellow and red accents carry useful functional contrast. The pool’s cyan reflection is readable behind the gate. The surrounding consoles and breaker bank now have quiet surfaces, although the scene still lacks small service evidence beyond labels and gauges.

### 04 TURBINE_AISLE

The aisle remains open, and the turbine-side equipment is readable as a working area. The cropped turbine edge is acceptable for this dedicated aisle view. The broad speckle regression is gone; only subtle edge wear remains on visible equipment. The white pipe and pale wall/floor still read as smooth manufactured surfaces without much tactile variation, and the floor route arrow remains a flat graphic cue.

### 05 REVERSE_NORTH

The pool, paired columns, rails and rear utility route remain clear. The perimeter equipment no longer has distracting full-face damage. White pipes and teal tanks remain visually simple, and the cyan shaft has the same evenly distributed light as the other room views. This is a stable reverse read, but not yet a highly tactile one.

### 06 CONTROL_ROOM

The lower horizontal opaque floor spandrel/sill is modeled structure and is not counted as a glass defect. The glass above it is more transparent than Art 04, but it still shows real optical haze and multiple ghosted reflections: duplicated pool rails, equipment and bright fixtures overlap the view through the pane. The control-side room is visible, yet the reflection layer weakens clarity and makes the observation glazing feel visually dirty or doubled. This remains a material/readability defect even after excluding the solid sill.

### 07 COMPACT_STAIR

The enclosed stair is readable as the dedicated subject. Treads, edge lines, rails and wall light are clear, with no Art 04 speckle regression. The space remains visually plain and isolated, but adjacency is correctly deferred to the separate diagnostic evidence.

### 08 MATERIAL_SLICE

This is the strongest material improvement over Art 04. The broad side-face damage is gone. The pressure gauge face has more useful detail, pipe flanges and handwheels read as separate components, the shift-check notice provides human-use evidence, and the sparse edge wear is believable at this scale. The white pipes still look very smooth, the wall and floor are low-information, and the teal turbine body remains close to a single stylized material family.

### 09 BANK_MECHANISMS

The dedicated bank detail is clean and easy to judge. Both banks, fixed housings, lower carriages and columns read clearly. The full-face speckle regression is gone; a few small edge chips and trim marks remain. The underlying construction is still repetitive and generic: smooth cylindrical bodies, identical rings, broad blank panels and labels provide most of the identity. The image is materially calmer than Art 04 but not yet a high-specificity mechanism study.

### 10 EAST_HIGH

The wide high view retains the complete room relationship: pool, rail, gate area, paired banks, doors, carts and perimeter equipment all remain visible. The pool and bank hierarchy is strong, and the old speckle noise is absent. At distance, most equipment still compresses into repeated teal blocks; the pool remains a uniform cyan focal field and the east glazing still carries visible reflection haze.

## Observable changes from Art 04

- Broad full-face speckles are gone from the hero banks, consoles, breakers and perimeter equipment.
- Remaining wear is sparse and concentrated around visible edges, seams and lower trim, which is much closer to the reference maintenance language.
- View 08 has stronger material evidence through pipe flanges, handwheels, gauge detail and the shift-check notice.
- The pool/two-bank hierarchy and room-level camera coverage remain stable.
- View 06 no longer has the Art 04 opaque-looking glass wash across the whole image, but actual ghosted reflections and haze remain above the modeled opaque sill/spandrel.
- White pipes, pale wall panels and flooring remain smooth and less tactile than A02/B01.
- Bank silhouettes remain clear but construction remains repeated and generic in view 09.
- The room still has sparse human-use evidence beyond labels, gauges, carts and the paper notice.
- Technical reproduction remains unassessed because final cold-start evidence is pending.

## Decision

**FAIL — art-05 is the first materially credible recovery from the Art 04 regression, but it is not approval-ready.** The sparse edge wear is visually acceptable and the ten-view composition is stronger. The scene still misses 90 in shape specificity, reactor depth hierarchy, materials, lighting depth, detail discipline and environmental credibility. View 06 has real glass reflection haze after excluding the solid lower spandrel, and the final cold-start technical gate remains open.
