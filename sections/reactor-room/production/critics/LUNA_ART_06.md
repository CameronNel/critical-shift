## Camera and diagnostic evidence

### 01 HERO

The pool, paired banks, long columns, rail and south gate still compose coherently. The edge-wear treatment remains restrained and does not repeat Art 04’s broad speckle. The banks remain cleanly identifiable but show the same generic service-face construction as 09. The cyan pool is bright and readable, though the shaft remains an evenly cyan-lit volume. The east glazing still carries doubled reflections.

### 02 WEST_ENTRY

The entry lane remains clear. The route, pool rim and perimeter stations read at a useful scale. The bank silhouettes are compressed toward the ceiling but remain understandable. Broad shell surfaces remain pale and smooth, and the scene still depends on teal colour and labels more than material differences for station separation.

### 03 SOUTH_GATE

The emergency acknowledgement, separate red control and yellow gate remain legible. The pool is bright behind the rail and the floor route is open. The surrounding consoles and breaker bank remain visually simple. Contact shadows and local wear are present only weakly, so the interaction foreground has less tactile weight than B01.

### 04 TURBINE_AISLE

The turbine aisle reads open, with the fuel rack, waste tank, pipe route and breaker bank visible. The practical wall light creates a useful local pool. The equipment still shares smooth teal/white responses, and the floor arrow remains a flat graphic marker. No broad speckle regression is visible.

### 05 REVERSE_NORTH

The rear pool ring, rails, columns and utility route remain clear. The large wall plates and pale shell are legible but visually quiet to the point of feeling unfinished. The rear stations remain small and interchangeable. The pool has readable depth but still lacks strong lower-source variation.

### 06 CONTROL_ROOM

The room is visible through the observation glazing, but the pane still produces multiple ghosted reflections of rails, pipes, lights and equipment over the view. The lower horizontal opaque floor spandrel/sill is modeled structure and is not counted against the glass. The actual reflection/haze above it remains a material/readability defect: the operator-side view is legible only as a doubled overview.

### 07 COMPACT_STAIR

The dedicated stair view is readable and evenly exposed. Treads, edge lines and rails are clear. The enclosure remains plain and provides no adjacency evidence, which is deferred correctly to diagnostics. There is no Art 04-style wear noise here.

### 08 MATERIAL_SLICE

The material slice remains one of the strongest close views. Flanged white pipes, yellow wheels, dark base, teal vessel, pressure gauge, console and shift-check notice are individually readable. The visible surfaces are still smooth and broadly uniform; the scene has controlled colour grouping but limited tactile breakup.

### 09 BANK_MECHANISMS

This dedicated bank view is judged on the bank subject only. Both fixed housings, lower carriages and drive columns are clear, with restrained edge wear and good value separation. The service surfaces remain generic: repeated cylindrical bodies, identical trim, broad blank doors and small labels dominate. This is a readable assembly study, not yet a high-specificity industrial mechanism.

### 10 EAST_HIGH

The high view gives a useful room-level relationship between pool, paired banks, doors, carts, gate area and perimeter stations. The pool and two-bank hierarchy remain clear. At distance, stations still collapse into repeated teal blocks and the east glazing remains visibly reflective/hazy. The wide composition is strong enough for room readability but not enough to clear the material and environmental rows.

### PLAN_DIAGNOSTIC

The roof-off plan confirms the octagonal shell, centred pool, surrounding circulation ring, south emergency position and east control-side connection. The plan is useful spatial evidence and shows no obvious route break at this scale. It is a flat diagnostic image, so it supplies no proof of material response, human-scale perception or tactile finish.

### CONTROL_INTERIOR_DIAGNOSTIC

The diagnostic reveals chairs, a desk/console, a mug and wall controls, providing direct operator-use evidence. A large near-black vertical occluder covers roughly half the frame from top to bottom. The remaining space is mostly blank wall and partial furniture. The operator room is therefore only partly inspectable, and the image cannot support a high environmental-credibility score by itself.

**FAIL — art-06 improves evidence and provides a credible first reopen check, but the visual gate is still not met.** The plan diagnostic supports the room layout, the pool and two banks read clearly, and the edge wear remains restrained. Generic bank service surfaces, weak tactile separation, uniform pool depth lighting, glass reflection haze and the heavily occluded control-interior diagnostic keep the visual categories below 90. Technical reproduction is provisionally scored from the fresh reopen evidence but remains pending final full validation.
