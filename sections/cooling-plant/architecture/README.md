# Cooling plant architecture — CP-A02

This package fixes the section-local dimensions and aligns them to the S03 design corrections. As-built geometry acceptance remains pending. It is an original schematic for a fictional game environment. Dimensions without a canonical value are implementation decisions, not engineering calculations or completed runtime tests. The JSON contract is authoritative for numeric envelopes; the SVG floorplan and PNG are generated from it.

The floorplan uses metres, entry threshold `(0, 0, 0)`, `+Y` into the section and `+Z` up. Clear hall dimensions are 11 × 13 × 5.8 m. Walls are 0.3 m thick, extending outside the clear room; the section stays entirely at `Y >= 0`. There is no raised maintenance platform. The 5 × 5 m main portal is flush with the floor and has a fixed-open overhead service door, so no door leaf swings across the cart route.

The west side carries two distinct horizontal pump skids. Each is 3.05 × 2.10 m; their shaft axes run in X with motor ends toward the main aisle. The front operator lane is 1.4 m deep; the shared lane between pumps is 1.3 m. The permanent centre lane is 2.2 m wide from the entry to Y12.6, reserved for carts and rescues with 2.4 m clear headroom. Ordinary machinery, guards, pipes and dressing must stay outside these volumes.

The east hero is a longitudinal shell-and-tube exchanger. Its permanent envelope starts at X2.3, providing a 1.2 m equipment reach strip outside the centre-lane edge at X1.1. This strip supports workers reaching the machine; it is not designated as a public cart route. The rear dome reaches Y9.29, inside the reserved body envelope ending at Y9.3. A 2.6 × 3.5 m work box behind it, X2.0..4.6 and Y9.3..12.8, remains empty to Z3.0 for cover removal and tube withdrawal. The 3.85 m barrel contains a nominal 3.35 m removable tube cartridge, with 3.5 m extraction travel: an explicit game-scale implementation decision. The dedicated hoist mechanism working zone sits above this box, Z3.1..5.35, below the roof girders. Fixed hangers may extend above it to the ceiling; unrelated services and props must not obstruct mechanism travel.

The west rear alcove is bounded by X−5.5..−1.6 and Y10..13. Its 1.2 × 2.2 m doorway has a 1.22 m leaf at hinge (−4.74, 10.20), shown open 90° parallel to +Y. The conservative swept bounds are X−4.78..−3.5, Y10.16..11.45. Their overlap with the non-route west jamb/hinge edge is explicitly declared; it does not exempt the actual clear portal or operator approach from geometry checks. The operator lane still stops at Y10. The nominal 2.8 × 0.7 × 0.9 m bench target is X−5.05..−2.25, Y12.3..13, with top Z0.936 and tools bounded to Z1.24; its S03 depth/upstand trim must be checked before as-built acceptance. A 0.9 m standing strip fronts the bench. The conservative door sweep overlaps the front 0.05 m of this strip at the west edge, and the open leaf ends at Y11.42; the final local standing-space check remains pending. The cart envelope, including its handle, is X−2.885..−1.9023, Y10.630..11.270, Z0..1.05. The alcove is a personnel repair space, not a stretcher turning destination.

The reserve restart terminal is mounted on the east wall near Y≈2, at X4.85..5.5 and Z0.77..1.7, with a 1.45 m operating strip at X3.4..4.85. It supports a local portable battery socket and a separately capped mine-water hose coupling. These are authored interaction locations for later engine integration. They do not create a second shared reserve-power pool. Incoming primary return uses the west front port (−5.03, 0, 3.76), bore 0.32 m; outgoing supply uses the east front port (4.99, 0, 3.76), bore 0.30 m. Both sit outside the portal width. The overhead hot crossfeed at Y2.5 has underside Z4.08, preserving the 2.4 m cart-route headroom; it does not cross the portal depth Y0..0.6. No service may cross the 5 m entry opening below Z5.0. Secondary water uses two east-wall ports. Utility risers are a tagged service network, separate from machine body envelopes, and must still respect clearances. External endpoints and the drainage outlet remain proposed. Local floor drains are flush.

## Reactor P02 coordination

The read-only P02 drawing source defines the southeast hall facet from `(10.8, −6)` to `(6, −10.8)`. Its centred 5 m cooling portal has endpoints `(10.167767, −6.632233)` and `(6.632233, −10.167767)`, with midpoint `(8.4, −8.4)`. A rotation of −135° around Z and translation `(8.4, −8.4, 0)` maps this section's entry endpoints exactly onto that opening; local +Y then points southeast in reactor coordinates. **This transform is proposed only and has not been applied to either section.**

Using the entire 11.6 × 13.3 m outer shell, its highest reactor Y is −4.298781. P02's control-room gross rectangle is X10.8..14.96, Y−3.4..3.4, so the rectangles have at least 0.898781 m of separation in Y. The east stair gross bounds X12.46..18.71, Y3.2..6.4 have at least 7.498781 m separation. These are conservative plan-separation checks, not a review of the eventual assembled geometry or external routes. The reactor's own southeast exterior wall is 0.36 m thick; a front shoulder seam may overlap it when assembled. The connector owner must resolve the join without treating this proposal as permission to alter the reactor.

P02 station 06 is the reactor's existing local turbine/throttle service station. The separate turbine-room brief establishes a new conversion room but supplies no verified world transform. Its facility-wide placement and connection to electrical distribution are therefore unverified here. No turbine-room geometry, wall, endpoint or connection has been assumed. The cooling plant is a pump/exchanger maintenance space and does not duplicate that conversion hall.

## Deliverables and regeneration

- `../interface.json`: dimensions, portals, machine envelopes, keep-clear and swing volumes, proposed sockets, runtime handoff.
- `floorplan.svg`: original vector floorplan with dimension chains, maintenance reserves and portal section.
- `floorplan.png`: inspected raster preview of the same SVG, when generated.
- `build_plan.py`: standard-library SVG authoring and numeric layout validation.
- `validation.json`: actual contract checks, clearly separate from Blender geometry and runtime validation.

Run `python sections/cooling-plant/architecture/build_plan.py` from any working directory. Optional rasterization uses the bundled Node `sharp` library; the vector is the original artifact and requires no external asset, font download or copied geometry.
