# Asset provenance

Original geometry and procedural shaders from `blender/build_scene.py` and `kit.py`, starting factory empty in Blender 5.2. No imported legacy scene geometry, third-party textures, purchased assets or generated mesh services. Blender's built-in font is used for editable lettering. No external scene dependencies are required.

The approved reactor reference images were viewed read-only for art calibration and are not embedded as scene assets.
