## Observed strengths

- The image communicates a maintained industrial cooling service hall with two large west-side pump assemblies, a substantial east-side shell-and-tube exchanger, a broad central working lane, a rear workshop window/bench area and a dedicated overhead hoist.
- Pump casings, motors, coupling guards, flanges, gauges, valves, saddles, exchanger cover, crane trolley, drains and concrete plinths provide unusually useful construction cues. The image gives a builder a credible language for assembled and maintained machinery.
- The cream institutional wall fields, teal painted equipment, charcoal structure, stainless/bare-metal accents, limited safety yellow and warm practical lighting align well with the approved reactor reference principles. The sparse directional labels and emergency-water/reserve-power cues support functional reading.
- The rear workshop view and localized floor wear add human maintenance context without relying on a wall of signage.

## Defects and limitations

- The central route reads clearly, but the image does not prove the 2.2m permanent cart/rescue lane, the 5m x 5m reactor opening, or the full 5.8m clear height. The camera is an interior beauty view with the entry edge absent, so entry/return orientation and rescue traversal remain unproven.
- The exchanger’s rear tube-withdrawal reserve is not visible or unambiguously readable. The apparent rear service side is visually busy with equipment and pipework; the concept cannot establish the required 3.5m extraction work volume.
- Pump front/coupling access and the exchanger side reach strip are suggested by floor markings, but their continuous clearance cannot be verified from one image. The center route could look generous while local service pinch points remain hidden behind the pump and exchanger masses.
- The image is close to the project’s upper realism boundary: floor reflections, dense small pipework and high-frequency concrete/paint variation push toward a photoreal industrial/AAA look. The final room should preserve the broad value grouping, simplified silhouettes and restrained surface frequency of the approved reactor plates.
- The concept is denser and more uniformly occupied than the reference’s strongest negative-space moments. Preserve quiet wall and ceiling areas so machinery hierarchy remains legible from gameplay height.
- The visible pipe labels are useful, but all final text, supply/return identities, control cues and emergency connections must remain sparse, legible and causally tied to gameplay. Labels cannot substitute for visible routing.

## Required use

Use R01 as a construction/material/lighting direction reference only. Root must verify the measured plan and interface against the completed room and render all ten fixed cameras. Layout remains unapproved until the entry opening, central lane, pump service zones, exchanger side access, 3.5m rear withdrawal reserve, hoist working zone and restart/mine-water access are visible and technically validated in the authored scene.
