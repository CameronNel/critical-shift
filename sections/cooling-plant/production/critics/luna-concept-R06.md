## Panel observations

### 1 — Pump skid P-01 (94)

Strong, specific centrifugal-pump silhouette with suction/discharge directions, pressure tap, flanged casing, shaft guard, motor, electrical conduit, skid/plinth and fasteners. The oxide body, charcoal motor and yellow guard form a clear material and safety hierarchy. Use this for pump construction and readable operator-side detailing. Exact skid envelope and access lane remain measured-scene responsibilities.

### 2 — HX-01 exchanger (94)

Useful shell-and-tube construction guidance: barrel, saddles, bolted end cover, primary/secondary in/out cues, gauges, small service pipework and front/side/rear views including tube-bundle withdrawal. The broad ivory shell and oxide bands match the current palette and preserve a clean Valorant-style mass. The labelled rear view is guidance only; the final 3.5m withdrawal reserve must be kept clear and objectively validated.

### 3 — Overhead chain hoist (93)

Clear trolley, beam, chain, hook and three-view silhouette with a believable industrial safety-yellow finish. It gives useful construction and readability direction for the HX work zone. Final beam/hook travel and the 5.8m headroom must come from the measured plan; do not treat the panel proportions as dimensional proof.

### 4 — Reserve power / emergency-water service (92)

The charcoal cabinet, analog status gauge, guarded controls, red emergency-water hose/coupling, vent and yellow floor boundary clearly communicate separate reserve-power and backup-water functions. The panel is well suited to material, signage and interaction readability guidance. Keep hose/coupling access outside the permanent cart/rescue lane and use Critical Shift’s own markings.

### 5 — Maintenance prop kit (91)

The bench, vise, organized hand tools, rag, seal/parts, cases, rolling cart and small waste/consumables provide a credible maintenance story with restrained clutter. This is useful for prop hierarchy and human presence. Preserve clear negative space and support contacts; avoid reproducing every small item if it harms route readability or becomes staged repetition.

### 6 — Rear room view (88)

The interior composition is strong: central aisle, drains and yellow route edges, pumps at west, exchanger at east, rear service glazing and overhead hoist. However, the open 5m x 5m entrance visibly leads to an outdoor yard/sky. The actual cooling-plant connection is to the reactor interior, so this panel is not approved as literal portal or environmental context. Use only its interior route framing, machinery placement cues and lighting balance. Do not copy the outdoor view, yard, sky or exterior architecture into the assembled room.

## Cross-panel constraints

- R06 is now the accepted component direction for warm ivory, charcoal steel, muted oxide orange and yellow safety accents. Teal remains rejected.
- Generic labels and arrows may guide Critical Shift signage, but no external branding, logos, slogans or distinctive typography may be copied.
- All panels remain subordinate to CP-A02 measured geometry and the final ten fixed-camera pixel review. The panels cannot certify the 2.2m lane, 5m x 5m opening, 5.8m height, service clearances, 3.5m withdrawal distance or support-contact tolerances.
