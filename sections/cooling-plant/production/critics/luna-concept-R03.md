## Pixel evidence

- R03 has the intended clean, broad, readable Valorant-influenced shape language: large pump and exchanger masses, simple framed walls, controlled teal/cream/charcoal/yellow grouping, clear floor route and restrained focal contrast. It is materially cleaner and less photoreal than R01.
- Painted teal equipment, off-white exchanger shell, charcoal framing, grey concrete, yellow safety lines/guards and red emergency hose separate clearly by value and response. The surfaces remain somewhat smooth, but they read as distinct families rather than one universal glossy shader.
- Warm local fixtures and brighter rear workshop/door zones establish a usable key hierarchy. The center route remains readable while the machinery receives the visual emphasis; no dominant cyan glow or flat uniform wash is present.
- The image provides useful construction cues: two west-side pump skids with guards and gauges, east shell-and-tube exchanger on saddles, overhead pipe supports, drains, hoist, rear workshop and a marked pull-out bay. The central lane and rear service logic are easy to infer.
- Layout is plausible at a glance: pumps left, exchanger right, workshop/alcove behind, central travel aisle and a dedicated rear pull-out bay. Exact 2.2m lane width, 5m x 5m entry, 5.8m headroom and 3.5m extraction distance remain unverified from this interior view.

## Limitations / implementation constraints

- All visible Kingdom logos, slogans, typography and branded marks are invented source-image branding and must not be copied into Critical Shift. Retain only the generic visual principles: broad signs, sparse functional wording, geometric arrows and restrained department identifiers.
- The concept does not show the reactor-facing entry threshold, so entry opening, return orientation and rescue/cart traversal need explicit fixed-camera proof in the final scene.
- The apparent pull-out bay label is helpful, but the final Blender room must leave the actual rear exchanger work volume clear of permanent pipes, props, bins, columns and parked carts and must pass the measured 3.5m reserve check.
- The concrete floor still carries moderate mottling and reflection. Keep the final implementation’s surface frequency restrained and tactile, with localized wear and contact shadows rather than photographic grunge or uniform wet gloss.
- The image suggests supply/return routing but does not prove every declared local socket or remote connection. Do not infer installed external endpoints from this concept.

## Use decision

Use R03 as the accepted style target for the complete-room build: strong simplified machinery silhouettes, broad colour fields, matte-to-semi-matte tactile materials, localized practical lighting, functional yellow/red accents, clear central flow and a readable rear maintenance/pull-out composition. Preserve the measured CP-A02 contract and objective validation as the authority for dimensions and interfaces.
