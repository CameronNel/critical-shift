## Highest-impact observed defects

1. **Synthetic material family dominates the slice.** In C07 and C10, teal machinery carries clean broad highlights, uniformly rounded edges and almost uniform surface finish. C08 reinforces the issue across unrelated materials. The reference images retain readable material identity and sparse, contextual wear even on quiet surfaces. The current anti-plastic and authored-use standard is not met.
2. **Workshop object construction is below the required art standard.** In C08, the vice is ambiguous block assembly, the cloth reads as a regular wavy sheet, and the pale wrench lacks strong metal identity. These are foreground review subjects, so their simplified finish is highly exposed. They do not yet look like the specific, tactile working objects in the approved references.
3. **Pristine, evenly arranged dressing weakens the human story.** C08 has several appropriate object types, but their spacing, orientation and lack of visible wear suggest staging. C07's trolley also feels newly placed for display. The slice does not yet communicate a specific maintained-but-used workplace.
4. **Light shapes are insufficiently selective.** C07's bright pipework and light walls compress depth; C08's wall and table consume much of the value range. Contact shadows exist, but the overall effect remains closer to a clean product render than an atmospheric playable industrial interior.
5. **The circulation camera does not prove its named concern.** In C07, the pump occupies most of the foreground and the trolley overlays the door approach. The visible image cannot establish the available worker/cart passage or door operation. This is an evidence limitation, not a claim that measured geometry fails.

## What is already working

- The pump/motor is identifiable from the main image without reading text.
- The palette is restrained and the guard uses a purposeful accent.
- Large wall areas are quiet; signage is sparse.
- Local occlusion, some bare-metal highlights and the workshop practical provide a viable foundation.

These strengths do not offset the scored deficiencies. Additional prop quantity or labels would not prove the missing quality.
