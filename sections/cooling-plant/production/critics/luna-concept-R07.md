## Pixel evidence

- R07 fixes the rejected R06 reverse-context problem: the route leads into an enclosed industrial connector with interior wall, ceiling and handrail continuity rather than sky, yard or exterior architecture.
- The warm ivory / charcoal / oxide orange / yellow palette is consistent and contains no teal. Pumps on the right, the exchanger on the left, a broad central route, linear drains, overhead hoist and a framed internal doorway establish a strong reverse read.
- Large clean machinery silhouettes, broad wall/value fields, sparse labels and localized warm practical fixtures align with the accepted R05/R06 Valorant direction. The central opening creates depth without decorative neon or excessive greebles.
- Flanges, pipe bends, gauges, saddles, plinths, structural framing, drains and hoist hardware provide useful construction cues while keeping the image readable at gameplay distance.

## Limitations and exclusions

- The internal doorway and distant machinery are compositional guidance only. Do not copy reactor-owned geometry, control equipment, portal structure, or any specific downstream room from this image.
- This view cannot prove the 5m x 5m reactor-facing opening, 2.2m cart/rescue lane, 5.8m clear height, turning/door clearances or any remote utility endpoint. Preserve CP-A02 and verify all dimensions objectively.
- The long interior depth may suggest a larger connected facility than the local 11m x 13m room. Use depth and framing as visual guidance while keeping the actual room bounds and connector seam under the measured contract.
- Smooth floor response and clean ivory surfaces are acceptable as concept direction, but the final authored room should retain restrained tactile variation, localized wear and contact shadows without drifting into photoreal grime or wet gloss.

## Use decision

Use R07 to guide the final reverse-view composition: an enclosed interior connection, route continuity, warm practical falloff, oxide/ivory/charcoal machinery hierarchy, yellow safety edges and a calm readable central lane.
