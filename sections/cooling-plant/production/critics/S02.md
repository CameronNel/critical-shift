## Highest-impact remaining defects

1. **Material and human-use specificity remains below the target.** C10 provides close scrutiny of large areas of painted metal but shows a nearly uniform, newly finished machine. C08 still does not make the wrench convincingly metallic or the cloth convincingly textile. The approved references distinguish material families while retaining authored, localized evidence of use.
2. **New darkness reduces useful detail.** The front grille and terminal-enclosure faces have very little readable surface information. The image is less overexposed than S01, but darker is not itself a finished lighting solution. These areas matter to the pump's visual construction.
3. **Foreground supporting props remain generic.** The replacement ring's smooth donut appearance does not communicate a particular machine part. The wrench, lamp and door do not yet sustain close inspection at the standard of the approved reference objects. Stronger motor work does not make those other focal objects finished.
4. **A new unexplained detail appears on the pump flange.** C07 and especially C10 show a tight row of touching hexagonal heads across the top of the otherwise regularly fastened flange. The cluster reads as arbitrary or accidentally crowded hardware and breaks the machine's construction clarity. Pixels do not establish whether this is actual duplicate geometry; that requires technical validation.
5. **The workshop tells a broad category, not a clear working condition.** It now says maintenance bench more effectively, but the isolated placement and pristine finish still weaken the people-work-here requirement. The small parallel scratches are too slight to carry the narrative.
