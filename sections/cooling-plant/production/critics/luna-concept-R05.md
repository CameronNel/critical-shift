## Pixel evidence

- R05 obeys the latest palette correction: warm ivory architectural fields and process pipework, charcoal structural steel and equipment, muted oxide orange pump bodies/pipe bands, and yellow safety guards, crane and route markings. No teal equipment or teal accent is visible.
- The visual language is now unmistakably aligned with the requested Valorant environmental principles: large clean silhouettes, broad value groups, restrained texture frequency, readable edge accents and composed negative space. The machinery has specificity without the repeated glossy sci-fi-kit read of the earlier concepts.
- Materials separate clearly in the image: ivory painted process surfaces, oxide-painted pump casings, dark structural steel, concrete/plinths, yellow safety metal and red emergency hose. The response is somewhat smooth, but the concept remains tactile and controlled rather than a universal plastic shader.
- Practical warm fixtures produce readable pump, workshop and exchanger zones with soft falloff into the lane. Contact shadows and floor drains anchor the equipment; there is no excessive cyan/emissive treatment.
- The composition plausibly communicates two west pump skids, an east shell-and-tube exchanger aligned along the room, a central cart/rescue route, rear west service/workshop space and a labelled rear pull-out bay. It gives useful construction cues through saddles, flanges, guards, gauges, supports, drains and overhead hoist.

## Limitations / implementation constraints

- This interior concept does not show the reactor-facing entry threshold. The 5m x 5m opening, 2.2m permanent cart/rescue lane, 5.8m clear height and entry-to-return traversal must be proven by the measured plan and final fixed cameras.
- The rear label “PULL-OUT BAY 3.5 M CLEAR” is directional guidance only. The authored scene must keep the actual 3.5m exchanger cartridge withdrawal volume clear and pass objective validation; the text itself is not evidence.
- The image suggests service strips but cannot verify pump access, exchanger side reach, hoist travel, door swing, restart-terminal approach or the required support-contact tolerances.
- Keep the final surface treatment inside this broad, clean range. Reduce any temptation to add dense photographic grime, uniform gloss or extra pipe/sign clutter during implementation. Oxide orange should remain a controlled department/material cue with yellow reserved for safety information.
- Generic “COOLING PLANT,” “SERVICE,” and pull-out wording may be adapted to Critical Shift’s own signage system. Do not import external brands, slogans, logos or distinctive typography.

## Use decision

Use R05 as the current accepted visual target: warm ivory architecture and piping, charcoal steel, muted oxide orange machinery, yellow safety accents, readable Valorant-style silhouettes, matte-to-semi-matte tactile response, localized practical lighting, and a calm central route framed by clearly purposeful machinery. The CP-A02 measured contract and full ten-camera evidence remain authoritative for dimensions, access and integration readiness.
