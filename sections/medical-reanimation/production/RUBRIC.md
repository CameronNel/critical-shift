# Independent Medical acceptance rubric

Eight categories each scored from 0 to 100: scale/circulation, shape/art direction,
hierarchy, materials, lighting, color, environmental storytelling, technical correctness.
Every category must independently reach 90, with no visual veto or critical failures.
Overall is the arithmetic mean; the mean cannot compensate for a failing category.

Visual scores require actual inspection of ten fixed camera images. Technical scores
require objective evidence, including geometry, support contact and cold reopen.
Four full review cycles minimum, final two without material regression. A separate
entry/decontamination style slice must pass before full-room production.
