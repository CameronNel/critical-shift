## 1. Scale / circulation — 48

CAM_ENTRY, CAM_REVERSE, and CAM_ROUTE now show an adult-proportioned hall: door height, dado, ceiling fixtures, stool, and recovery slab all imply a standing worker. The central apron is empty enough to walk. The OCRU mouth in CAM_HERO is a large opening, not a peephole. The decon stall in CAM_DECON is enterable. That is the credit.

Circulation as a *process room* still fails. No retrieval cart occupies the southwest parking bay in CAM_ENTRY, CAM_ROUTE, or CAM_REVERSE — the bay is empty floor. A thin utility trolley tucked beside the OCRU in CAM_REVERSE does not read as a body/cart arrival object. The west machine face is a glass display case, not a loading mouth you commit a ragdoll or cart into. CAM_PINCH shows the berth as disconnected slabs with visible gaps, so the loading surface does not read as a continuous adult bed. CAM_DECON is a shower stall, not a suited-adult gross-decon workflow. The hanging white mass on the east wall (CAM_ROUTE, CAM_ENTRY) eats volume without offering a use. The room is walkable. It is not a readable retrieve → decon → load loop.

## 2. Shape / art direction — 23

Severe failure against Valorant-influenced grounded stylized semi-realism. Spec requires the OCRU frame, berth, opening, seals, service coupling, cartridge receiver, restart controls, and maintenance panels to read as different constructed parts. CAM_HERO / CAM_PINCH show a labelled fascia around an empty painted box: light strips, a sandwich of grey slabs, a yellow sponge, two butter-pat cubes, and token side rails. No suit-service coupling, no hose, no seal language, no machine guts. It reads as a retail freezer / morgue drawer, not an industrial recommissioning unit.

Secondary hardware is primitive CSG. CAM_CONSOLE is a yellow shed over a can, a 4×4 cube keypad, three pegs with candy caps, and a silver stick — kindergarten solids, not a guarded restart deck. CAM_DECON is a pipe, a disc, a yellow cube, two cylinders, and a yellow bin. CAM_MAINT is a grey plate with three spheres. CAM_RECOVERY is a slab on stick legs with a ribbed yellow brick at the foot. Cartridge “inserts” in CAM_ROUTE are eight identical bottles on shelves. Doors, plaques, the hanging cabinet, stools, and ceiling ribs are bevelled boxes. Compared with the Valorant plates (constructed housings, gauges, rails, weight) and the med-bay plates (open cyan chamber with a built berth and hookup, chunky console, labelled cartridge rack), this is a generic low-poly kit, not specific industrial art.

## 3. Hierarchy — 36

The cyan OCRU volume is now the landmark. CAM_HERO, CAM_PINCH, CAM_ENTRY, and CAM_ROUTE all let the interior glow hold the west side. Dual diagnostic screens add a smaller cyan beat on the rear wall. That is the only hierarchy that works.

Everything else is same-value grey furniture. CAM_ROUTE presents console, cartridge cabinet, decon opening, power puck, hanging cabinet, and recovery slab as a row of equal quiet boxes. The hanging white cabinet is the largest secondary mass and it is blank, so it competes with the hero for no reason. The floor stencil is a washed rectangle that does not structure the apron. CAM_CONSOLE’s yellow shed is a false accent — it pops because it is candy-coloured, not because restart is the designed interaction. CAM_MAINT is a footnote panel on a blank door leaf. From gameplay distance the process sequence does not step down in visual weight.

## 4. Materials — 25

One satin response covers painted equipment, walls, furniture, floor, “metal” pegs, and “glass.” Spec forbids uniform satin plastic as a dominant read; that is still the dominant read.

CAM_MATERIALS, the materials camera, is a muddy brown close-up of a dark cabinet, a dark pane, a capsule handle, and a white console corner. It does not prove painted metal, concrete, rubber, glass thickness, or fabric as separate families. The recovery “towel” in CAM_RECOVERY is two stacked white boxes; there is no cloth. OCRU pads in CAM_HERO / CAM_PINCH do not read as black rubber. Floor tiles in CAM_REVERSE / CAM_ROUTE are flat value fields with no cart-route wear. Hazard tape has a stripe pattern and the gurney end is ribbed; that is the only tactile break. Handles, kick plates, and parking lanes show none of the local wear the spec asks for. Valorant plates separate concrete, painted steel, rubber, and glass at a glance. These pixels do not.

## 5. Lighting — 30

CAM_ENTRY, CAM_ROUTE, and CAM_REVERSE are washed by even ceiling rectangles. Falloff is weak, contacts are thin, and there is no practical pool structure of the kind in the Valorant plates or the warm directional med-bay plates. Spec asks for warm practical ceiling light; the hall is a cool office grid. Flat even lighting is an automatic-fail condition and it is still the room’s lighting language.

Credit is local. CAM_HERO / CAM_PINCH have a real interior volume: strip lights, corner uplights, and cyan fill. CAM_DECON / CAM_MAINT throw striped shadows from the hazard jambs. Screens emit a little. CAM_MATERIALS is underexposed brown rather than a materials beauty light. None of that rescues the wide shots.

## 6. Color — 40

Cyan is now where the spec puts it: OCRU interior (CAM_HERO, CAM_PINCH, CAM_ENTRY, CAM_ROUTE) and diagnostic screens (CAM_CONSOLE). Entry-door slits in CAM_REVERSE read as cool-white view bars, not stolen cyan. That confinement is the best colour decision in the pack.

The shell is still a cream office. Spec: “not teal machines or cream rooms.” Walls in CAM_ENTRY / CAM_REVERSE / CAM_ROUTE are beige-cream over a grey dado. Yellow is not hazard-only. Hazard stripes on OCRU jambs, door frame, decon opening, and gurney end are correct (CAM_HERO, CAM_REVERSE, CAM_DECON, CAM_RECOVERY). Yellow then leaks onto the restart shed, console pegs, decon pipe cube, OCRU sponge and pad cubes, and the BIO bin as object colour rather than hazard language (CAM_CONSOLE, CAM_DECON, CAM_PINCH). Candy yellow on toys plus cream walls plus a cyan vitrine is not a departmental palette. Graphite/bone equipment exists only as generic grey plastic.

## 7. Environmental storytelling — 32

Required procedure (retrieve → decontaminate → load → suit service → power → cartridge → physical restart → monitored cycle → recovery) is sketched as a lineup of props, not readable as industrial work.

- Arrival: CAM_ENTRY shows the hall and a faint CLEAN SAFE ACCESS ONLY pad. No body cart in the parking bay (CAM_ENTRY, CAM_ROUTE, CAM_REVERSE).
- Decon: a dry stall with a stick shower, two bottles, a BIO cube, and a grate. No wet floor, no hose-down, no contamination control (CAM_DECON).
- Chamber / suit-service: empty cyan box; no port, no hoses, no couplings (CAM_HERO, CAM_PINCH).
- Cartridge insertion: a shelf of unlabeled identical bottles behind an open door, not four labelled rows feeding a receiver (CAM_ROUTE). The yellow shed on the console does not read as insertion.
- Restart: toy housing, cube pad, pegs (CAM_CONSOLE).
- Monitoring: the one function that actually reads — STANDBY / READY FOR RECOMMISSION / AWAITING SUBJECT and a UNIT MAP stick figure with NO SUBJECT (CAM_CONSOLE).
- Power / battery: three lamps and a cabinet labelled RESERVE (CAM_MAINT, CAM_MATERIALS).
- Consumables: a blank hanging slab, not a glazed cabinet of bottles and metal boxes (CAM_ROUTE, CAM_ENTRY, CAM_MAINT). East bench holds a faucet and empty paperwork (CAM_REVERSE).
- Maintenance: CAM_MAINT is a door leaf plus a lamp panel, not an OCRU service hatch or status interior.
- Recovery: a morgue-like slab, boxed linen, boxed pillow (CAM_RECOVERY), against the brief’s “not a hospital or morgue.”

Copy that reads: OCRU / T-01 / ORGANIC CONTINUITY AND RECOMMISSIONING UNIT, CAUTION KEEP CLEAR AUTOMATED SYSTEM, OCC HEALTH, POWER / MAINS / RESERVE / ISOLATE, RINSE SANITIZE RETURN TO WORK, L3-01, BIO, STANDBY checklist. Plaques for HUMAN STABILITY / OPERATIONAL CONTINUITY and FIT PEOPLE / SAFER SITES are present as washed rectangles (CAM_ROUTE, CAM_REVERSE). LIFE REBUILDS VALUE and the triangle facility mark do not read. The comic-bureaucratic tone is a few decals on an unused showroom. AWAITING SUBJECT matches the empty props; it does not make them look like a workplace.

## 8. Technical correctness — 42

CAM_ENTRY images the room. CAM_HERO and CAM_PINCH image an interior. Decals that were mirrored in earlier packets now read forward on OCC HEALTH (CAM_RECOVERY), RESERVE (CAM_MATERIALS), MAINS / RESERVE / ISOLATE (CAM_MAINT), decon copy (CAM_DECON), and the console screens (CAM_CONSOLE). No exploding mesh is obvious. Stool and gurney feet meet the floor. That is the technical credit.

Remaining pixel errors are still structural. The floor stencil in CAM_ENTRY is faint to the point of illegibility. RESTART / ABORT on the console is tiny (CAM_CONSOLE). The supplies cabinet is an opaque blank solid, not glazed storage (CAM_ROUTE, CAM_MAINT). OCRU glazing reads as a thin front plane, not glass with thickness (CAM_HERO). CAM_MATERIALS is a muddy, uninformative plate. The berth is a gapped pallet, not a continuous adult surface (CAM_PINCH). Cream wall shader contradicts the spec in every wide shot. Wear maps are absent. Support-contact and cold-reopen evidence required by the rubric are not in this packet. Builder validation PASS cannot raise this score.

## Top 5 highest-impact defects

1. **Generic low-poly / Three.js language is the room.** Uniform satin plastic, flat fluorescent wash, repeated boxes. Automatic visual FAIL against Valorant-influenced grounded semi-realism and against both approved plate families (CAM_ENTRY, CAM_ROUTE, CAM_REVERSE, CAM_CONSOLE, CAM_DECON).
2. **The hero OCRU is an empty cyan display case, not a recommissioning machine.** No suit-service hookup, no seals or couplings, berth is gapped slabs. Kills shape, loading, and the core procedure (CAM_HERO, CAM_PINCH).
3. **Restart, cartridge insertion, and console controls read as toys.** Yellow shed, cube keypad, candy pegs, unlabeled bottles on a shelf. Physical restart and insertion do not exist as industrial functions (CAM_CONSOLE, CAM_ROUTE).
4. **Body/cart arrival is missing.** The designated parking bay is empty floor in every camera that can see it (CAM_ENTRY, CAM_ROUTE, CAM_REVERSE).
5. **Recovery, consumables, and maintenance fail their functions.** Boxed fake towel on a morgue slab (CAM_RECOVERY); blank hanging cabinet instead of glazed supply storage (CAM_ROUTE, CAM_MAINT); three spheres on a plate and no OCRU service hatch (CAM_MAINT).
