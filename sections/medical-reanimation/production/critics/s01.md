## Highest-impact observed defects

1. **C00, overall architecture and materials:** broad blank surfaces and nearly uniform smooth finish dominate the image. The approved references show visibly built material layers, controlled surface variation and convincing local contact; that level of specificity is absent here.
2. **C00, illumination across the wall and floor:** the work area, open floor and quiet wall occupy a narrow lighting range. The practical is visible at the top, but its influence is not sufficiently distinct to organize the space or establish depth.
3. **C00, left doorway and lower threshold:** the dark door occupies a large part of the frame without readable operating hardware. Its sign is cut by the top border and much of the threshold grate is outside the lower border. The full constructed transition and its operation are not proven by this evidence.
4. **C00, basin and surrounding utility:** the functional cluster is identifiable but visually insubstantial. Small fittings, soft material response and weak contact cues leave it looking more like an early model than installed, maintained equipment.
5. **C00, right-hand paperwork and small props:** the visible paper does not carry a legible procedure; marks fall to its right. Human use is too faint and sparse to satisfy the slice's storytelling requirement. No distinct fabric response is visible.
