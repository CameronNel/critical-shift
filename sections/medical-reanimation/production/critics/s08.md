## Largest remaining defects

1. **C00b, folded linen on the shelf; C00, hanging towel:** fabric identity has improved but is still the largest material weakness. The folded piece's upper layer lifts as a stiff sheet, with a broad planar surface and regular edge. The hanging towel remains smooth and firm. Both read less like handled cloth than the required grounded material treatment.
2. **C00, door contact edges; C00b, backsplash wear:** the new pale marks are highly isolated and bright. Several door marks resemble attached flecks or drips, and the backsplash chip reads as a small pale patch on an otherwise perfectly even surface. The image does not consistently sell worn paint with an underlying material layer.
3. **Both views, broad walls, floor and painted equipment:** material response remains very even. There is enough pixel coverage to judge this; a closer camera will not by itself resolve it. The requirement is restrained authored material variation and localized use, not dense photographic grunge.
4. **C00b, glove on bin:** the closed tips correct the former tubular-piece failure, but the fingers have a regular rounded, inflated appearance and little convincing limp glove behavior. Its miniature toy-like quality remains noticeable beside the improved basin.
5. **C00, pressure instrument; C00b, small utility interfaces:** the gauge reads chiefly as a pale disk in C00 and is outside the diagnostic close-up. Some utility details remain ambiguous at the actual wider view. This is a secondary issue; it should not displace the material and fabric defects as the next major correction target.
