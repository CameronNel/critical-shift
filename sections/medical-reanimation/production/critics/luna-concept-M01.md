**Concept decision: REJECT for adoption.** The concept is a strong composition and machinery guide, but the material score is below the required 90. The dominant concrete and floor treatment is too photoreal and grunge-forward for the requested Valorant-style art direction. Keep the current R09 spatial arrangement as the baseline; do not treat M01 as permission to redirect the room.

## Layout / spec fidelity — 93

M01 preserves the existing R09 entry composition and station relationships. The OCRU occupies the left/west wall; the rear console and narrow cartridge storage sit centrally; the decon opening is rear/right; the glazed supply store and recovery position occupy the right/east wall; and the middle remains a readable working apron. R09 CAM_ENTRY shows the same front-facing relationship, while CAM_REVERSE confirms that the OCRU and east-side work area swap sides as expected when viewed from the rear. This is a faithful visual interpretation of the saved arrangement.

The single concept view does not prove the southwest cart parking, the entry threshold behind the camera, the east bypass under obstruction, or the rear service reach. A cart is not visibly staged, so arrival-route evidence is incomplete. Those are evidence limits, not reasons to move the room. The concept also compresses the rear service/decon relationship enough that the exact route needs to remain validated in the actual scene.

## Machinery / operational readability — 92

The OCRU now reads as a manufactured employee-service machine: deep fascia and jambs, a captive upper access treatment, lower support rails, a supported berth/tray, edge seals, visible side service hardware, and bundled service hoses. The restart console has a coherent worktop, two diagnostic screens, guarded physical controls, drawers, and a stool. The adjacent rack visibly stores sealed consumables. Decon has a framed threshold, shower fixture, floor grate, and waste container. The right-side cabinet, power/status lamps, work surface, and recovery cot support the intended room function.

The image does not make all interactions equally explicit. Cartridge insertion is implied by stored canisters rather than a clear receiver; mains/reserve power logic is represented by lamps but not functionally legible; the OCRU maintenance hatch and the body/cart route are not shown; and the monitored vulnerable cycle remains screen-level suggestion. These are acceptable for a concept plate but prevent a higher score.

## Materials — 88

Material separation is much stronger than R09: painted light equipment, graphite control surfaces, darker rubber/seals, glass cabinet panes, a fabric-like hanging towel, bare-looking fasteners, and concrete/plaster walls are distinguishable. The chamber is neutral white rather than teal/cyan, and the accents are restrained orange/yellow hazard language.

The failure is the surface treatment. The wall and floor carry visible photoreal mottling, stains, and small surface variation across broad areas. Metal edges and the floor reflections also lean toward a polished product render. The requested target calls for low-frequency, authored wear and stylized semi-realistic material response with quiet surfaces; M01 reads closer to photoreal industrial visualization. The concrete drift is the specific concern: it is not yet controlled enough to pass the strict material gate. The next material direction must reduce high-frequency grunge and universal realism while keeping the clear material families.

## Lighting — 93

The concept has warm practical overhead fixtures, a warmer wall-light pool on the rear/right, stronger falloff around the room edges, readable contact shadows under stools and carts/cots, and neutral task light inside the OCRU. The ceiling structure creates depth without flooding every surface equally. The OCRU remains the landmark while the console and decon read as secondary stations.

The chamber task lights are bright and slightly photographic, and the overhead grid still contributes broad ambient exposure across the hall. Those are minor deductions; there is no flat office wash or blue/teal lighting failure in M01.

## Reference fidelity — 91

M01 follows the approved high-level Valorant principles: believable human-scale construction, layered manufactured parts, broad neutral value grouping, purposeful orange accents, clean silhouettes, practical light, and restrained readable detail. It contains no teal/cyan machines or chamber glow, no copied map layout, and no obvious protected logo. The room feels industrial and maintained rather than hospital-luxury or horror-morgue.

The deduction is for the same photoreal concrete/floor drift and slightly over-finished product-visualization quality. The concept is closer to the target than R09, but it is not yet a strict final art-direction reference at 90+ across every relevant material read.

## Required corrections before concept adoption

- Rework the wall and floor surface language toward broad, quiet, authored tonal variation with localized wear; remove the photoreal grunge emphasis.
- Keep the neutral chamber task lighting and orange functional accents; preserve the no-teal/no-cyan rule.
- Preserve the demonstrated station arrangement and OCRU construction language.
- In a follow-up concept or render review, expose the cart/arrival relationship, cartridge receiver, power selection, and maintenance access clearly enough to verify the chapter 5 loop.

No scene score is inferred from the concept, and no final-room acceptance is claimed. M01 is rejected at the concept gate solely because Materials is 88/100; the other scored categories independently clear 90.
