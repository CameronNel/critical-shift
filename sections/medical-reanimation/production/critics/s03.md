## Highest-impact next-pass defects

1. **C00, broad architecture and equipment surfaces:** the uniformly smooth, pristine finish remains the largest distance from the approved references. Additional object count has not made the major material families more tactile or specific.
2. **C00, right-hand hanging textile:** three teal areas visibly cut through its front. Its regular sharp fold rhythm reads closer to a rigid folded sheet than handled cloth. Both the physical intersection and the material/silhouette ambiguity are significant.
3. **C00, center-right working cluster:** the light wall, backsplash, basin edge, dispenser and shelf contents occupy similar values. Their roles are understandable with close attention, but their separation and authored construction do not hold strongly at gameplay size.
4. **C00, basin and doorway contact/use areas:** there is very little visible evidence of repeated handling or cleaning. The slight floor markings and small orange items do not establish maintained industrial use across the primary work surfaces.
5. **C00, technical evidence:** the report's twelve support checks still cannot prove complete support registration, actual circulation, or absence of scene intersections. The towel error demonstrates why a passing contact list cannot be promoted to a general geometry pass.

These are observed defects and evaluation outcomes, not instructions for specific replacement geometry.

## Dark doorway assessment

The opening occupies a large, nearly uniform dark area, so it unquestionably affects this camera's composition and reduces the amount of built surface available for inspection. **An absent adjacent module is not itself a defect in this section, and I am not requiring the builder to author neighboring geometry to pass this slice.**

With the parked leaf now visible, it would be inaccurate to continue describing the entire dark region as an unfinished blank door face. It is an open boundary. The hierarchy score considers its visual competition, but the opening is not an automatic visual veto, and no technical failure is inferred solely from the missing neighboring view.

The substantial style deficiencies remain visible on the built right-hand corner when considered independently of that dark region. Material identity, cloth construction, and the visible intersection prevent acceptance even if the exterior opening is excluded from aesthetic judgment. Retaining the fixed s02 camera remains useful for honest comparison.
