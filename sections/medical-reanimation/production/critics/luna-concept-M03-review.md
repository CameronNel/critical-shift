M03 is materially better than M01: broad wall panels are quieter, the orange/graphite grouping is more graphic, and the room no longer depends on a teal/cyan chamber glow. ## Layout / spec fidelity — 94

M03 retains the station relationships shown in M01 and the R09 entry render: OCRU on the left/west wall, restart console at the rear center-left, cartridge storage immediately to its right, rear/right decon opening, glazed supplies and recovery work position along the right/east wall, and a broad central working apron. R09 CAM_REVERSE confirms the same arrangement from the opposite direction, with the OCRU on the right and the entry portal centered. M03 is therefore a visual refinement of the saved room rather than a layout redirection.

The front view does not show the single entry behind the camera, southwest cart parking, the rear reserve/power arrangement, or the exact east bypass under a dropped body/cart. This is a single concept plate and cannot prove those interfaces. No deduction below the gate is warranted because the depicted layout itself agrees with R09; those items remain actual-scene validation requirements.

## Machinery / operational readability — 93

M03 gives the OCRU a specific manufactured read: deep framed jambs, upper access housing, captive-looking opening treatment, supported adult berth, rails and seals, side service hardware, and bundled service lines. The rear console has two diagnostic screens, a worktop with guarded physical controls, drawers, and a stool. The adjacent rack reads as stocked sealed cartridges. Decon has a framed threshold, shower, grate, and waste container. The right cabinet reads as glazed storage, the vertical lamps read as a power/status panel, and the east worktop/cot positions support recovery and supplies.

The image still implies rather than proves the cartridge receiver, reserve-versus-mains selection, maintenance hatch, body/cart staging, or the full monitored-cycle state. Those are runtime and integration contracts, not reasons to alter the preserved composition. M03 earns a concept-machinery pass because the major chapter 5 steps now have distinct visible machine cues.

## Materials — 91

M03 is a clear improvement over M01. Painted sheet-metal panels, graphite controls, darker rubber/seals, glass storage, fabric on the right worktop, concrete/plaster walls, and bare-looking hardware separate at gameplay-scale image size. Broad surfaces are calmer, edge wear is limited, and the orange accents behave as functional trim/hazard language. There is no universal satin-plastic read.

The remaining risk is the generated image’s photographic rendering: the floor and wall panels retain subtle mottling and soft reflection, and the chamber materials are very clean. This is now restrained enough for constrained guidance, but the Blender pass must keep wear low-frequency and localized and avoid turning the concrete into noisy photoreal grunge. M03 does not authorize copying the exact texture response.

## Lighting hierarchy — 94

Warm practical overheads and wall fixtures create localized pools and visible falloff. The OCRU interior uses neutral white task lighting rather than colored glow. Contact shadows anchor the console, stool, recovery furniture, cabinet, and OCRU supports. The ceiling beam structure gives depth while the rear decon and east side remain secondary. Compared with M01, M03’s darker upper structure and more deliberate orange utility run improve the focal hierarchy.

The OCRU task light is bright and the hall remains generally well exposed, but M03 avoids the flat even fluorescent wash that failed R09. Keep this hierarchy while ensuring actual gameplay views remain readable.

## Color discipline / no-teal compliance — 97

No teal or cyan appears in M03’s machine, chamber, wall, floor, or light treatment. The palette is neutral concrete/bone/graphite with oxide orange and small hazard yellow cues. Orange is clustered on machine trim, utility routing, control accents, and safety edges rather than spread across every prop. This directly corrects the R09 cyan chamber and stays within the user’s explicit no-teal override.

The image includes a generic medical cross on the glazed cabinet. It is not used as a canonical logo or department mark; keep any final signage original and restrained.

## Environmental storytelling — 92

The concept communicates an active employee recovery room through a loaded cartridge rack, diagnostic screens, guarded controls, stool, decon shower/grate/waste bin, stocked glazed cabinet, recovery cot, paperwork, towel, and service hoses. It reads as an operational workstation rather than a luxury clinic or horror morgue. The broad central floor remains available for the retrieve/decon/load sequence.

The cart parking bay and a dropped body are not visible in this camera, and the exact order of interactions is not legible from one still. M03 supplies enough purpose and equipment cues for concept guidance; actual fixed-camera renders must verify the cart, body handling, and chapter 5 sequence.

## Reference fidelity / strict Valorant principles — 92

M03 follows the approved principles: believable human-scale industrial construction, layered specific forms, broad value blocking, controlled materials, practical lighting, readable silhouettes, and deliberate negative space. The orange/graphite structure and quiet panel seams are more graphic and art-directed than M01. It avoids copied map layout, sci-fi holograms, neon strips, and the rejected teal chamber.

The image still carries enough generated architectural-visualization polish that the final scene should simplify texture frequency and preserve authored wear. The score reflects useful principle fidelity, not permission to copy exact props, labels, or camera perspective.

## M01 comparison

M01 scored well on arrangement and warm practical lighting but failed Materials at 88 because its concrete/floor treatment leaned photoreal and grunge-forward. M03 keeps the same layout and machinery story while reducing surface noise, strengthening graphic orange/graphite grouping, and removing all teal/cyan. M03 therefore clears the concept-guidance gate; M01 remains rejected as a final style target.

## Canonical and implementation limits

- Preserve the existing R09 placement and dimensions. M03 is a style and construction guide, not a new floorplan.
- Treat only the canonical name **Organic Continuity and Recommissioning Unit (OCRU)** as authoritative.
- M03’s visible OCRU, console, cartridge rack, decon, power/status, storage, and recovery cues are guidance; exact interaction IDs, host hooks, states, clearances, and failure behavior come from the interface contract and actual scene validation.
- Keep the no-teal/no-cyan rule in all Blender materials and lights.
- Translate concrete and painted metal with broad controlled response and localized wear; do not reproduce photographic grunge or universal reflections.
- Do not adopt generated slogans, medical branding, or exact concept labels beyond the canonical OCRU/RESTART/DECON vocabulary.
