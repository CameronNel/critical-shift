## Use rule

For every new asset:
1. identify its row;
2. inspect the referenced plate(s);
3. model primary and secondary forms in flat grey;
4. verify scale from plate 21 / 24;
5. only then add materials and tertiary detail.

If an asset is missing from this matrix, derive it from the closest functional family rather than defaulting to “box + bevel + panel.”
