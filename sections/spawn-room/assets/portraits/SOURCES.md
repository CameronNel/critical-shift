# Original institutional prints

Both PNGs were generated specifically for Critical Shift with OpenAI's built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-06. The subjects are fictional. No third-party image or game asset was supplied as an input. These are AI-generated project artwork, not claimed public-domain or CC0 downloads. No external attribution license is required by an incorporated third-party asset.

| File | Dimensions | Use / modifications |
|---|---|---|
| human_contribution.png | 1024 × 1536 | One hallway supervisor portrait, mapped to matte paper in an authored narrow metal frame. Packed into the blend. |
| commissioning_crew.png | 1536 × 1024 | One briefing-room crew print. Same paper/frame treatment; packed into the blend. |

Prompt direction for **human_contribution**: original fictional institutional portrait of a 55-year-old Black woman safety supervisor, gray hair, olive work overalls and off-white shirt, holding a hardhat; restrained grounded semi-real editorial painting on aged off-white paper, muted olive/ochre palette, no real person or corporate logo; footer “HUMAN CONTRIBUTION / SERVICE • CARE • CONTINUITY.”

Prompt direction for **commissioning_crew**: original fictional workplace crew portrait of four adults, two men and two women, varied skin tones, ages 30–65, olive work jackets, ochre hardhats, one rolled plan, plain concrete turbine building behind; restrained grounded semi-real editorial painting; footer “COMMISSIONING CREW / SECTOR 04 / FIRST SHIFT.” No real people, branding or game imagery.

All room geometry and procedural material graphs are authored locally in `grounded_kit.py` and `grounded_room.py`. No marketplace models, downloaded textures, HDRIs, fonts, or ripped assets are used. Text uses Blender's bundled Bfont. Reference SVGs are repository art-direction material and are not applied as runtime textures.
