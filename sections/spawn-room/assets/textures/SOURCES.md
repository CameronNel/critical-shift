# CC0 surface sources

Downloaded 2026-09-06 for the user-requested worn, stylized Spawn Room revision. Only CC0/public-domain assets are incorporated; no paid, attribution-required or restricted-use textures.

License: https://polyhaven.com/license — CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. These allow modification and commercial use without attribution. CC0 is the practical interpretation of the user's request for free textures with no license restrictions.

The original downloads are retained unmodified in cc0/. Their published MD5 values were verified after download; SHA256 and original download URLs are recorded in manifest.json. All six images are packed into the Blender source and retain project-relative paths.

Stylization occurs non-destructively in worn_surfaces.py: metric triplanar projection, grayscale-to-restrained-palette remapping, reduced scan chroma, broad tonal variation, controlled height bump and high variable roughness. Cloth, rubber, wood, glass and painted hardware use authored material-specific procedural wear. The source images are not applied indiscriminately to unrelated material families.
