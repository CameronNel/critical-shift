## BRIEFING

The warm lighting, shadowing and main composition improved substantially. The refreshment console is still cut off on the left, while the right plant is oversized and mechanically regular. The target remains denser in wall dressing and more naturally varied in its used-but-maintained materials.

Prioritized edits:

1. Widen the lens or trim the crop so the console is fully readable, the left wall edge has breathing room and the open right portal remains visible; keep the TV, both benches and architecture fixed.
2. Scale down and replace the right plant silhouette with fuller, less evenly spaced foliage; its repeated broad leaves currently cover the notes and read as a regular prop pattern.
3. Keep warm practical pools but lift the deepest blacks under benches and behind the plant so timber, teal wainscot and wall details remain readable.
4. Add roughness and edge variation to console, books, cups, benches, plaster and wainscot; several new props still read as plain uniform geometry.
5. Complete the target information density with the safety/map/pinned-note cluster and far-left discipline graphic after framing, while preserving exactly two benches and a clear entrance.

## HALL

The warmer corridor mood and operational dressing are closer. Both lateral edges are still cropped: the left poster and right first-aid/fire equipment are clipped. The left poster also has visible text overlap/garble at its top edge.

Prioritized edits:

1. Widen the camera to include the full left poster, update board and cart plus the complete right first-aid cabinet, extinguisher and pipe; leave corridor architecture and entrances unchanged.
2. Repair the left poster so text does not overlap or clip at the top edge; match the target's headline, landscape graphic and footer hierarchy.
3. Retain warm pools but lift dark ceiling and floor midtones enough to show tile wear, cart contents and door reveals; preserve warm contrast.
4. Replace oversized regular plant silhouettes with fuller, varied foliage in the target planters; avoid repeated identical leaf shapes and keep the route clear.
5. Give cart, safety cabinet, extinguisher, boards and shelf props restrained labels, roughness and edge wear so they do not read as plain blocks.

## LOCKER

Tile, locker color and room dressing improved. The foreground locker and pod still dominate the frame, deep shadows hide locker/pod detail, and the four-identical-locker group is not immediately legible enough near the entry.

Prioritized edits:

1. Widen or reframe to reduce foreground locker/pod dominance while showing complete pod, entry locker faces, rear locker bank and portal; do not move room architecture.
2. Make four identical locker bodies near entry immediately countable through matching dark teal proportions, vents, handles and labels. Preserve pod position, exactly two benches and clear portal.
3. Raise fill selectively in pod recesses and foreground locker face; keep warm ceiling pool/contact shadows while recovering dark storage details.
4. Reduce oversized repeated plant leaves and build fuller varied foliage around planter, shelf and laundry cart; add no suits.
5. Refine small bathroom-style tiles and props with warm matte roughness, subtle grout/scuff variation, locker edge wear, towel texture and legible labels.
