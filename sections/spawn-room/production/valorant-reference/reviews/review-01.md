## BRIEFING

The TV, handover board, crew image, two benches, rug and open entrance are recognizable. The tighter render removes most of the target's left console and right plant. Its neutral bright lighting flattens the worn plaster, teal wainscot and timber against the target's warm, dimensional room.

Prioritized edits:

1. Widen the lens or camera framing without moving architecture: show the full left console, both benches and more of the right plant/entrance, with the TV no longer clipped at the left edge.
2. Warm the practicals and lower pale exposure: add amber pools, deeper bench contact shadows and warm plaster bounce while retaining readable TV and board text.
3. Complete the newly visible dressing: left refreshment/work console props and right safety/map/pinned-note cluster with fuller plant silhouette. Keep exactly two benches and the portal clear.
4. Strengthen matte used-but-maintained materials: rougher plaster, scuffed teal wainscot, less saturated timber and a faded rug with believable edge wear.
5. Restore tonal hierarchy: TV and boards as focal points, benches in the softer midground, peripheral props readable without becoming overexposed.

## HALL

The corridor axis, end door, briefing opening and wall colors hold together. The view is too tight to show the full target wall program and safety equipment, while the render remains cleaner, brighter and less amber than the target.

Prioritized edits:

1. Use a wider lens/framing without moving architecture so the full left poster/update board, foreground cart, right safety equipment/pipe and open briefing entrance remain visible together.
2. Warm and shape the light: reduce pale flat exposure, add amber ceiling pools and retain darker door-frame and cart/bench reveals.
3. Finish the target wall program: left poster/update board, right first-aid cabinet, extinguisher and safety placard, plus shelf/coat-hook and bench cues near briefing. Keep all entrances open.
4. Make plant silhouettes fuller and more naturally layered at the end door and briefing-side shelf; current foliage is too sparse and graphic.
5. Add subtle worn pale tile, matte plaster and lightly scuffed teal paint with controlled roughness and edge wear.

## LOCKER

The pod, two benches, tile floor and clear portal are strong anchors. The foreground locker and pod are cropped, the room is pale and evenly lit, and the required four-identical-locker read is not yet clear enough near the entry.

Prioritized edits:

1. Widen the lens/framing to include the complete pod, left entry locker faces, rear locker bank and portal while keeping the pod on the right and both benches readable; do not move architecture.
2. Warm the practicals and restore shadow depth so the pod, benches and locker recesses regain the target's amber dimensionality.
3. Make four identical locker bodies near entry unmistakable through consistent dark teal proportions, vents, handles and labels. Preserve pod position, exactly two benches and clear portal.
4. Add restrained locker dressing—towels, labels, open storage, pegboard/shelf gear, fuller plants and laundry cart—but add no suits. Keep props subordinate to the four-locker read.
5. Tune small bathroom-style tiles and materials toward the target's warm matte finish: soften near-white floor, add restrained grout/green variation and increase roughness on walls and locker paint.
