Three most effective changes:

1. **Camera/framing — layout:** Shift framing slightly right or widen the lens so the console gains a left wall margin (target begins around x35 rather than pass x0) and TV/portal/plant align more closely. Do not move architecture or benches.
2. **Lighting/material — lighting + art style:** Add warm fill to console/lower walls while retaining fixture pools, then deepen matte plaster, teal paint and timber variation so the room has the target's amber depth.
3. **Actual content — art style:** Replace the oversized repeated-leaf plant with a narrower, fuller natural silhouette and keep safety/map notes readable. Pass foliage is about x1235-1455 versus target x1360-1530.
