1. **The new rear surface variation reads as cloudy procedural mottling.** Large soft dark patches spread across the rear wall and ceiling without an evident relationship to material, contact or maintenance. This is particularly visible around and above the cabinet field. The references use broad surface character but keep it controlled and related to believable material/use. Here the pattern risks reading as stains or camouflage added to defeat cleanliness. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: materials, lighting, environmental storytelling.

2. **Painted equipment still has a generalized soft-panel finish.** Door and filing-drawer rims have similar smooth inset treatment. The broad painted fields and rounded highlights remain exceptionally uniform. The new display's thick housing, flat colored screen and small row of controls are legible, but retain a schematic game-prop presentation compared with the more specific construction and tactile finish of the approved controls reference. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: shape/art direction, materials.

3. **Small-prop material credibility is still inconsistent.** The telephone handset and control assembly remain simplified geometric masses; the cloth remains thin and smoothly uniform; the foreground stamp still reads as a nearly pristine cylinder and base. The numbered case is readable through its label but has little physical character. These objects are recognizable, yet do not all sustain the same believable industrial standard as the door hardware. Affected: **S02_material**. Categories: shape/art direction, materials, storytelling.

4. **Lighting now exposes the office, but does not fully resolve its visual quality.** The warmer side wall and brighter rear volume are meaningful gains. The cloudy material pattern blurs the distinction between surface variation and shaped illumination, while the foreground wall remains broadly even. In the approved references, light falloff, plane changes and material response work together more clearly. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: lighting, materials.

5. **The rear task-lamp read remains visually fragmented.** The window member and display obscure parts of the lamp and its work area. The counter/display relationship is clearer than before, but this rear cluster still looks crowded in projection. This is an image-composition observation, not a claim of an actual geometry intersection. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Category: hierarchy.
