## Highest-impact observed defects

1. **The dominant finish is procedurally clean and visually underdeveloped.** Both images show very large, smooth wall/panel/counter surfaces and nearly pristine teal paint. The small kickplate scratches are isolated short parallel marks and do not change that dominant reading. The references instead make broad quiet surfaces feel tactile and maintained through controlled surface character and plausible selective use. A clean-looking shader graph or object count would not resolve this visible difference. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: materials, shape/art direction, storytelling. This is the present **procedural-cleanliness visual veto**.

2. **The service-window area lacks readable depth and function.** The broad opaque upper panel is the largest visual mass; below it, the opening presents mainly a dark floor grid and featureless darkness. It does not yet explain the human or equipment activity across this threshold. This is a pixel observation, not an assumption that the space is absent from the scene. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: hierarchy, lighting, storytelling.

3. **Material identities are insufficiently proven at the dedicated material view.** S02 is dominated by the same broad smooth panel, jamb and counter response. The cloth is not visible there; in S01 it reads as a very thin, regularly undulating pale sheet. The mug is extremely simplified, including the angular handle, and the visible wear is too sparse and diagrammatic to convey material-specific use. Concrete/plaster versus coated material is largely communicated by color. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: materials, shape/art direction.

4. **The illumination does not yet match the references' spatial hierarchy.** The front wall and door are broadly bright, the large panel catches a broad top highlight, and the service opening loses usable depth almost immediately. The practical and contact shadows are positive evidence, but they do not yet produce a convincing occupied workspace behind the checkpoint. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: lighting, hierarchy.

5. **The evidence composition leaves important slice claims weakly inspectable.** S01 clips the practical and most floor/threshold contact; S02 repeats the blank panel while cropping much of the actual human prop cluster. The images prove a partial door/counter corner well enough to critique, but not a complete inspection work area or local movement. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: hierarchy and review coverage; circulation remains explicitly unreviewable.
