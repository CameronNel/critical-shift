The **procedural-cleanliness veto remains active in the dominant read**; this is not a request for noisy grunge. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: materials, shape/art direction, storytelling.

2. **The counter damage remains visibly schematic.** Short dark horizontal marks repeat across the counter lip with similar scale and treatment. They register more as a pattern than as believable contact history. The kickplate scratches remain similarly isolated and diagrammatic. Affected: **S02_material**, also visible in **S01_style**. Categories: materials, storytelling.

3. **The secondary props still reveal a lower finish level than the door.** The filing drawers are exceptionally pristine with simple uniform fronts; the phone's housing, controls and handset have a strongly simplified block-like read; the orange records remain rigid pristine slabs. The cloth has improved, but still looks thin and uniformly smooth. These are observed individual asset deficiencies, not an absence of sufficient prop count. Affected: **S02_material**, visible at smaller scale in **S01_style**. Categories: shape/art direction, materials, storytelling.

4. **The light still describes the rear office poorly.** The visible rear practical is bright, yet the back and side walls remain dark and nearly featureless. The front wall is broadly even. The approved references make local practical influence and surface planes more apparent while retaining readable secondary depth. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: lighting, materials.

5. **The rear working cluster remains partially interrupted by its framing.** The horizontal window member crosses immediately above/in front of the task-lamp shade and fragments the office read. This is a compositional consequence visible in both images, not proof of an actual collision. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Category: hierarchy.
