## Gate sequence

1. **Style slice first.** Gameplay-height evidence must contain the protocol's architectural transition, door, primary prop cluster, practical light, utility element and 3–5 human storytelling props. Visible style categories must independently pass. Any unavailable whole-room circulation or technical evidence is explicitly unreviewable and is not implied to pass. Full production remains blocked while the slice fails.
2. **Fixed-camera baseline.** Ten named evaluation cameras must cover entry, hero function, reverse, primary route, circulation pinch points, machinery, secondary space and material/lighting weaknesses. Record transform, focal length, framing, settings and resolution. Changes require a justified new baseline.
3. **At least four full reviews after visual completion.** Each full review requires all ten images and current objective validation evidence. Slice reviews, partial batches, uninspected files and numerical rescoring alone do not count. Defects identify affected image names. Follow-up comparisons record improved, unchanged or regressed findings against the same views and the approved art references.
4. **Stable final pair.** The final two complete review cycles must remain materially stable, with every relevant category at least 90 and no critical failure. Passing one flattering image cannot compensate for another failing image.
5. **Cold-start audit.** A fresh Blender process opens or rebuilds the authoritative final state, resolves dependencies, reruns objective checks and renders the ten final cameras. Compare the actual cold-start pixels to the approved stable set. Completion stays pending until that evidence passes.
