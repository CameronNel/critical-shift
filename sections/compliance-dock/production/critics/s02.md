## Highest-impact remaining observed defects

1. **The surface finish still reads like a clean procedural visualization.** The large wall, door paint, filing cabinets, desk and counter have little visible material-specific variation. Their distinctions rely heavily on color and broad shading. The reference images demonstrate tactile mineral surfaces, coated metal and selectively exposed metal even in quiet areas. This remains the principal art-direction gap and the **procedural-cleanliness visual veto remains active**. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: materials, shape/art direction, storytelling.

2. **The visible counter wear is repetitive rather than convincingly associated with use.** A row of similarly oriented dark capsule-like marks runs along the front edge. These look placed as a repeating decorative sequence. The door kickplate still shows fine short scratches with little other material history. The problem is the character and distribution of the visible marks, not a need for blanket grunge. Affected: **S02_material**, visible more weakly in **S01_style**. Categories: materials, storytelling.

3. **Several foreground and office props retain a demonstration-asset read.** The cloth remains extremely thin and has a regular, airy undulation rather than persuasive fabric behavior. The orange books/folders are exceptionally pristine, rigid masses; the telephone and lamp have recognizable purpose but little visible manufactured specificity beyond their basic silhouette. This is most apparent beside the more resolved staff-door hardware. Affected: **S02_material**, with the contrast apparent in **S01_style**. Categories: shape/art direction, materials, storytelling.

4. **Lighting depth has improved but remains unevenly informative.** The counter and paperwork receive good readable light; the rear wall and side wall still appear as dark broad surfaces with little spatial or material information. The front wall is broadly even in exposure. Compared to the reference controls view, practical light, form and contact do not yet produce the same convincing local dimensionality across the slice. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Categories: lighting, materials.

5. **Some compositional competition remains in the working cluster.** The horizontal window member crosses the desk-lamp area, and the upper check-in sign is clipped in the wide image. Neither is a proven construction defect, but both reduce clarity of the review evidence. Affected: **S01_style, S02_material**. Category: hierarchy.

The actual functional read has improved meaningfully. The current limitation is visual finish and object/material credibility, not a lack of additional objects. This statement is diagnostic; the reviewer supplies no replacement geometry, shapes or coordinates.
