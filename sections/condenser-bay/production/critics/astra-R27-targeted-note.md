## Three most consequential visible defects

1. **C04_EXHAUST.png · entire frame, underside of condenser/hood · blocker ·** the required exhaust evidence is still a close underside view of dark horizontal members, fasteners, and a cropped lower body; the external U04 receive throat, neck, seal/flange relationship, and its support are not visually readable · a viewer cannot verify the defining turbine-to-condenser process connection from the named exhaust camera, so contract coverage, machinery logic, construction, and fidelity remain unproven.

2. **C06_COOLING.png · upper half of frame, ceiling and hanging instruments; lower right, cropped valve · major ·** the cooling camera looks upward into ceiling services and shows partial gauges/one valve while the supply and return run, isolators, deliberate terminations, and support landings are largely outside frame · the named cooling-system proof reads as a ceiling study rather than a complete service connection, leaving the functional story and construction evidence materially incomplete.

3. **C09_ROOF.png · full frame, condenser roof/underside and east gallery edge · major ·** the roof-service view is almost entirely black understructure with only small highlights on bolts and a distant rail; the roof assembly, lighting contribution, ventilation/services, and surrounding clearance are lost in shadow · the ceiling/roof cannot be judged as a finished, readable part of the room, and the darkness conceals construction and lighting defects.

## Other visible defects and residual limits

- **C08_MAINT.png · center and lower frame, maintenance deck · major ·** the deck is now visible, but the foreground yellow rail, vertical pipe, and crossing dark members dominate it; the service surface terminates into a tight, visually congested corner with little readable relationship to a removable machine face · maintenance intent is present but difficult to read at gameplay distance.
- **W08_GALLERY_TURN.png · left third, structural column beside the stair/gallery approach · major ·** the lowered gallery and connected rails are visible, but the column occupies the approach edge and the downward view compresses the landing/route into a narrow slot · the route is more credible than earlier packs, yet the image still does not provide a clean player-height read of the full gallery clearance.
- **W03_NW.png · right third, condenser side behind the foreground column · minute/major cluster ·** the vessel side and return fittings are repeatedly occluded by the large dark column and foreground vessel edge · side/support continuity is only partially inspectable across the player-height set.
- **C01_ENTRY.png · left edge, operator desk; right edge, condenser enclosure · minute ·** the entry is readable and grounded, but the hero body and enclosure crowd the first approach while the desk is clipped at the frame edge · first-volume circulation and the relation between entry, controls, and machine remain compositionally compressed.
- **C10_MATERIALS.png · center, motor guard/coupling · minute ·** the close crop shows useful bolt, cage, cast-orange, and dark-metal separation, but nearly all of the frame is one coupling assembly and does not expose glass, rubber, concrete, or fabric comparisons · the required material-detail claim remains narrow even where the visible metal treatment is credible.
- **C07_OPERATOR.png · lower center, console desk and mug · minute ·** labels and gauges are legible, though the desk and mug occupy the foreground and the lower selector/emergency-control region falls close to the crop edge · operator readability is good but the complete station context is limited.
