# Compact refinery — production pass

Authoritative geometry is the Blender source package. The attached brief in `../prompt/refinery-build-brief.md` supplies task-specific requirements; global gameplay and current grounded stylized art direction remain in `design/`.

The room is 14 × 12 m, with a 4.8 m ceiling underside. Shallow structure and practical fixtures hang locally below that plane. Nine distinct stations follow the mine-to-reactor chain around an open shared floor. The reserved central route is 2.2 m wide, extending from X −3.35 to +3.70 m. All fixed review cameras are at 1.68 m eye height.

The west mine portal has 2.6 m clear nominal width and 3.0 m height; the east reactor portal matches it. Personnel enter through a 2.4 × 2.6 m south threshold. These are local department connection points, not a claim that an entire facility has been assembled.

## Material flow

1. An authentic Gullet cart arrives at floor level on 1.10 m gauge rails. A new captive cradle rolls the entire unchanged cart 50° sideways about a horizontal trunnion. The source cart had no existing tipping mechanism.
2. Its discharge lip empties into a low open hopper. A size gate, brake, captive latch, bump stop, alignment guides and cleanup grate support the operation.
3. A cleated inclined belt lifts the consolidated ore batch beside the west wall into the crusher hood. Players never carry material upstairs.
4. Toothed rollers discharge into the short scanner belt. Its diverter has a visible south-facing reject chute and removable bin.
5. An enclosed powered screw lift feeds the shaped process vessel. Coolant, seal access, gauges and emergency dump have separate physical locations.
6. A sealed drying tunnel has its own blower, removable filter, exhaust and dried-material catch position.
7. Players carry dried components to the west-facing assembly bench. Casings, guide columns, press ram, die, guard and output cradle remain separate.
8. Inspection presents an estimate and confidence, with approve, reject, reprocess, blend, falsify and send-uninspected choices. It provides imperfect information.
9. Carryable fuel leaves inspection for a small trolley beside the visible reactor threshold.

## Visual construction

Warm institutional walls, teal painted machinery, charcoal structure and restrained yellow form the broad palette. Steel, rubber, plastic, paper, glass and fuel casings have distinct material responses. Concrete has matte broad variation, a replacement patch, limited cracks, seams and drains. Wear and work props occur in small service clusters. Three existing project CC0 concrete maps are retained, hash-checked, palette-remapped and packed; all other materials are procedural.

## Runtime boundary

Blender provides editable visual/physical structure and interaction locations. Unity still owns collisions, player movement, carried-object physics, cart motion controllers, network authority, production simulation, uncertain inspection logic, audio, VFX, material translation and optimization. The prescribed static previews are demonstrations, not a gameplay simulation.
