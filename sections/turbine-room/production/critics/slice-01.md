## Highest-impact observed defects

1. **Specificity of the hero work objects — C10_materials, C08_maintenance.** The bearing, tool ends, stand and vise expose their starting primitives. Smooth circular and rectangular forms dominate where functional construction should make these objects individually credible. Small hanging tools share the same overly schematic language. This is the principal anti-blockout failure.
2. **Material identity and use — both cameras.** The tabletop reads as a uniform tan slab; the metallic ring and wrench look overly soft and glossy; painted supports and door surfaces are consistently new and smooth. These do not show the controlled, differentiated response and sparse localized wear visible in the approved references.
3. **Lighting hierarchy — C08_maintenance.** The wall, tabletop, columns and door receive similarly broad brightness. The bench fixture has a bright diffuser but little distinguishable local influence. The scene lacks the reference images' shaped pools of light, quiet shadow areas and depth.
4. **Architectural/utility credibility — C08_maintenance.** The door frame and leaf remain blunt, simple masses with little readable operational hardware. The doorway terminates visually in a featureless dark plane. Several small conduit mounting tabs appear laterally detached from the conduit in the visible image, weakening support logic; this also needs geometric validation.
5. **Human presence — both cameras.** The maintenance story currently depends on a label and the mere presence of isolated pristine props. Paperwork, tools and surfaces offer little specific evidence of use, material age or an in-progress job.

No full-hall acceptance is implied. This is the initial style baseline, not one of the four required correction cycles after first full visual completion. Technical remains pending.
