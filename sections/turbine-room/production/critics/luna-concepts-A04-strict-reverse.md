## Review rule

This review applies the reinforced strict Valorant direction. A component is approved as concept guidance only when its own score is at least 90/100. Any score below 90 is **rejected as approval** and may be retained only as a defect or revision target. These are conceptual scores, not final production-category scores and not evidence that the saved room passes.

The authoritative layout remains the existing 14 × 24 × 7.2 m design and `interface.json`: no concept pixel can change the footprint, the no-mezzanine/no-stairs baseline, the portals, the central route, the equipment envelopes, the train axis, or the utility marker coordinates. ## Realism drift compared with A02 and A03

A02 is a strong turbine construction reference: the stepped casing, horizontal split line, flange bolts, oil piping and pedestal/base relationship are specific. Its main drift is the product-render treatment—fine grime and chipped paint across broad surfaces, dense repeated fasteners, realistic metal highlights and a photographic close-up that gives tertiary detail more weight than gameplay silhouette.

A03 is a strong generator reference: ribbed body, end ventilation, support feet, terminal-side hardware and guarded orange coupling are readable. It drifts further toward an asset showcase through dense cooling ribs, high-frequency roughness, many tiny fittings and a polished isolated framing that does not demonstrate route or player interaction.

A04 improves the concepts by placing the train in a navigable-looking hall, adding controls, floor drains, overhead crane logic and stronger value blocking. It retains the same realism drift in a wider composition: realistic concrete variation, repeated hardware, highly rich reflections, near-photographic wall lighting, and an expanded industrial architecture read. The image’s realism is useful for deciding how assemblies are built; it should be reduced before it becomes the room’s art language.

The strict target is therefore the shared high-level structure—specific silhouettes, visible supports, controlled ivory/charcoal/orange/yellow blocks, practical lights and purposeful negative space—rather than the concepts’ texture density or photographic finish. Do not chase the pictured windows, extreme apparent height, unmeasured aisle width, or ornamental upper architecture.

## Dimension and source authority

A04, A02 and A03 are concept images with no authoritative scale metadata. Their apparent dimensions cannot override the saved design: 14 × 24 m clear hall, 7.2 m clear height, no mezzanine, no stairs or raised thresholds on the main route, D01/D02 nominal 2.4 m × 2.7 m portals, and the documented turbine/generator envelopes and common axis. Keep U01/U02/U03 and interaction marker IDs unchanged until bound and verified. Concept imagery cannot establish collision, navigation, removal sweeps, support contacts, or runtime controls.
