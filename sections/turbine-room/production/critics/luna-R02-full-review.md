## Camera observations

### C01 entry

The machine dominates the right side while the left route remains readable. Cream casing, charcoal framing and orange accents establish the A05 relationship. The view does not make the D01 entry condition or complete route measurable; the central train still occupies most of the frame. The overhead utility system reads as supported visually, pending the saved audit.

### C02 hero

The broad turbine casing, orange LP section, dark coupling region and generator transition are visible and stronger than R01. The shell hierarchy is still broad enough that individual HP admission, LP expansion/exhaust, bearing and output stages can merge at gameplay distance. Use the casing seams and service elements to keep the train’s process order immediately readable.

### C03 reverse

The generator end and reverse direction read clearly, with a clean route to the dark opposite opening. The machine is heavily cropped at left, so this is useful for reverse hierarchy but not a complete generator-service view. The destination door and route should retain enough local signage to orient the player without relying on one small label.

### C04 route

This is the strongest route composition: yellow lines lead to `ELECTRICAL / 03`, the east-side machine edge is legible, and the maintenance/oil cluster sits to the left. The `MAINTENANCE / 02` sign is visually broken by a vertical structural member (`MA` on one side and `NCE / 02` on the other), which is a navigation/readability defect. R03’s inside-D01 sign correction must be judged from this same view.

### C05 east service

The east aisle is legible as a separate side service path beside the foundation, with the wall-side lower pipe and overhead steam/utility drop visible. It is visually sparse, which helps clearance read, but the pipe and valve assembly need a clear destination and support story. The image cannot replace the objective aisle/swept-volume audit.

### C06 throttle

The control bank now has stronger dark lower faces and orange access panels. Output, demand, reserve and safety read quickly, and the red trip control remains distinct. The left `HEALTH` label is cropped at the camera edge; the control group also reads as a row of modules rather than one tight operating cluster containing throttle, load, readback and trip. R02’s contrast change is visible but not yet sufficient for a final navigation score.

### C07 coupling

The yellow guard has immediate hazard readability and the shaft is visible behind it. The regular bar grid and centered `GUARD / 01` plaque dominate the frame; the guard’s removable/lift-off construction is not yet visually obvious. Keep the plaque attached and ensure the open/removed state remains readable in a later camera or runtime handoff.

### C08 maintenance

The orange oil stand, bench, vise, bearing cradle, toolcase and electrical doorway now form a coherent service corner in the no-teal palette. `ELECTRICAL` is readable. The maintenance sign remains split by the structural member, and the clean mug/paperwork/worktop still feel arranged for display. A07’s richer maintenance story is useful for prop placement, but its branding is prohibited as source material (see below).

### C09 generator

The generator reads as a distinct cream ribbed body with a dark end bell, supported feet and service cables. The close view remains dominated by repeated ribs and broad clean shell fields; the terminal/output area is not a strong focal cue. The floor cable loops are visible and require route/clearance confirmation before final construction approval.

### C10 materials

The bearing, cradle, wrench, cloth, paper and mug provide a useful close-material story, with an orange service case visible below the bench. Painted shell, dark steel, wood and fabric separate, and no teal appears. The worktop still has even grain and limited contact history; the bearing’s smooth highlight and clean props keep the image closer to a polished asset close-up than the restrained, used Cooling Plant finals.

## R02 blockers carried into R03

1. **Technical islands remain an objective FAIL.** The fresh CPU audit reports two visible contact islands: the hoist assembly (`Hoist chain`, `Hoist chain.001`, `Hoist hook`, `Hoist motor housing`, `Hoist travelling trolley`) with nearest measured gap 0.06 m, and `Oil header clamp.007` with nearest foot gap 0.025 m. R03 must correct or explicitly register intended suspension/contact and rerun the audit; visual plausibility is not enough.
2. **Inside-D01/maintenance signage is unreadable in route views.** The structural member splits the `MAINTENANCE / 02` read in C04/C08. The reported R03 correction must preserve wall construction and be rechecked from both route and maintenance cameras.
3. **Controls need another fixed-camera pass.** R02 improves backing/contrast, but C06 still crops `HEALTH` and does not present the full control hierarchy as one unmistakable operating station. Keep text dark enough against pale backing and preserve distinct throttle/load/readback/trip affordances.
4. **Surface history remains below target.** C08/C10 work props and R02 orange service details help, but broad shell, wall and worktop fields remain too clean and uniform. Add sparse contact/use cues with broad controlled response; avoid A02/A03 photographic grime drift.
5. **Close views need less product-shot emphasis.** C02, C07, C09 and C10 isolate individual assets effectively but do not always show function, access, route or state in the same frame. Use supplemental R03 walkthrough cameras as evidence, while retaining the ten fixed cameras as the acceptance set.
6. **Technical scope is still local Blender evidence.** R02 passes local dimension/interface-marker and route-volume checks, but the report explicitly does not prove engine collision/navmesh/physics, neighbor alignment, mechanical stability, runtime controls, incidents, audio, or cold-start render survival.

## Review result

**R02 visual result: promising but not accepted.** Machinery, construction and reference fidelity are provisionally strong; specification coverage, layout flow, navigation readability, materials and lighting remain below 90. The R02 technical report is a saved-file FAIL with two measured contact islands and topology REVIEW. A06 controls guidance passes its relevant visual component checks. A07 maintenance construction/story guidance passes selectively, while its branding, photographic treatment and layout implications are rejected. R03 must be reviewed from actual pixels and a fresh audit before any final category approval.
