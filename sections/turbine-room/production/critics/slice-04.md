## Comparison with slice 02

- **Object-specific construction: improved.** The pump no longer has the same broad lower block, and the bearing's support reads as service equipment. The maintenance case is visibly functional rather than random filler.
- **Material identity and wear: mostly unchanged.** The basic material families were already present in slice 02. A small mug-base mark and minor finish differences do not substantially change the pristine dominant impression.
- **Lighting: improved, with a local regression.** The work zone has better contrast and the electrical threshold receives useful light. That new fixture visibly masks the right portion of the destination lettering.
- **Doorway depth/utility: partly improved, partly unchanged.** The threshold has more interior surface detail. Its background still looks like a featureless dark plane. The short element below the junction box remains without a visible destination in C10.
- **Human presence: improved, incomplete.** The case and taped paperwork add purpose. The cloth, cup and worktop still look unusually clean and presentation-ready.

## Highest-impact observed defects

1. **Tactile finish remains underdeveloped — both cameras.** Large painted surfaces, the wall, timber and primary metal object are still mostly uniform and pristine. The sparse pale slivers on the bench frame and isolated floor streaks feel like placed marks rather than accumulated contact/use. This is the largest remaining difference from the approved references' restrained but convincing material history.
2. **Destination text is obstructed — C08_maintenance.** The new doorway light overlaps the right side of ELECTRICAL and its very bright diffuser obscures the letters. This is a new visible readability defect.
3. **Architectural finish and secondary depth remain schematic — C08_maintenance.** The broad column, large door-panel fields and featureless dark doorway background read as simplified set construction. The maintenance props now contain more specific construction than the architecture supporting them.
4. **Light shaping is not consistently resolved — C08_maintenance and C10_materials.** C08 is improved, but the rear wall remains broadly illuminated and the doorway fixture pulls attention through glare. C10's tabletop has very even exposure, while the bearing's outer surface carries a broad bright strip.
5. **The repair scene still looks staged — both cameras.** A case and procedure paper make the activity more specific, but the clean work surface, clean rag and polished replacement part dominate. The visible evidence of worker use is too slight to overcome that impression.

This is not full-hall acceptance and does not count toward the minimum four full correction cycles after first complete room visuals. The expansion gate remains closed on visual grounds independently of the technical status.
