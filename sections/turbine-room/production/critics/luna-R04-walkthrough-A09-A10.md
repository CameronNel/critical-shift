## Review boundary and evidence control

A09 and A10 are isolated component studies. Their source pixels are 1536 × 1024 PNGs on a neutral studio background. They can guide the turbine and generator’s silhouette, construction, material grouping and no-teal Valorant treatment. They do not prove the 14 × 24 × 7.2 m room, portal alignment, route width, player reach, neighbor binding, or any saved-scene contact. Those subjects remain unscored for the concepts.

R04 is the complete 16-image local-room set: ten fixed views and six walkthrough views, each 1440 × 900, listed in `production/renders/review/R04/render_manifest.json`. The actual images were inspected directly. R04 is preliminary evidence, not final acceptance. The saved technical result is **FAIL** because the two west title lettering objects are 0.0055 m from their backing against a 0.005 m tolerance. `geometry.topology` remains **REVIEW** for authored open cloth/text and other open surfaces. R05’s physical title-gap correction, broader matte variation, more planar castings, actual readback dials and unified control top frame receive no credit here because its final pixels are not yet present.

The local integration scope remains authoritative. The unassigned condenser beneath U04, remote steam source, reciprocal electrical neighbor and other neighboring modules are open interfaces, not missing R04 deliverables. Their absence is not scored as a defect in this local-room review.

## Actual walkthrough and sign findings

### W01 entry return

The camera is so close to the dark D01 door that the frame contains almost no sign, threshold or route context. It cannot orient a player toward the room or establish a meaningful return path. The door surface itself is clean and unbranded, but the view is a navigation-evidence failure. Reframe the camera or provide visible authored entry context in a later evidence set; do not infer approval from a blank door panel.

### W02 controls full

This is the strongest control readability view. `SPEED`, `HEALTH`, `OUTPUT`, `DEMAND`, `RESERVE` and `SAFETY` are all legible, and the ledge labels for `THROTTLE`, `LOAD` and `TRIP` are visible. The dark cards currently read as simple state plates with dots and line marks rather than actual dial/pointer readbacks. R05’s actual readback dials and unified top frame remain pending. W02 does not erase the fixed C06 crop.

### W03 south cross

The central floor route and yellow guide lines are clear, and `REACTOR / D01` is readable at the left destination. The control bank is compressed at the far right and cannot carry detailed control evidence from this distance. The wall field is intentionally quiet but visually sparse.

### W04 north cross

`MAINTENANCE / 02` is centered and readable above the bench, which confirms the earlier sign-splitting issue is visibly corrected in this view. The `ELECTRICAL` destination sign is cut by the right edge, removing its `/ 03` suffix and weakening the destination read. The maintenance bay and route remain understandable despite that crop.

### W05 oil service

`LUBE / 01` is clear on the oil-service cabinet, and the guarded shaft and pipework remain visible around it. The close framing prioritizes the cabinet and does not prove the full apron or route clearance, but there is no branding or sign illegibility in this view.

### W06 steam service

The white steam service drop and valve dominate the foreground and mask much of the HP casing. The `REACTOR / D01` sign is partly hidden behind the pipe, so the service point and destination lose immediate readability. This is a visual occlusion finding; it does not claim that the pipe fails the saved clearance audit. The unassigned steam source remains an open interface as documented by `CONNECTIONS.md`.

## R04 style and source-safety observations

- No teal appears in any reviewed R04 image. Cream, charcoal, oxide orange, warning yellow and warm work materials remain coherent.
- No A07-style game/company branding or slogans are visible in R04, A09 or A10. The A07 rejection remains historical and unchanged.
- R04’s broad smooth fields, repeated casing panels and even product lighting are still less graphic and less materially varied than the approved A05 target. R05’s planned material and casting changes are not evidence until its pixels are inspected.
- A09 and A10 provide component language only. They do not authorize the A09 top lifting eyes, A10 orange stripe, extra cable riser, new platforms, stairs or any footprint/interface change in the saved room.

## Review result

**A09 turbine guidance: APPROVED. A10 generator guidance: APPROVED.** All depicted component categories independently reach 90 or above, with room fit intentionally unscored.

**R04 local-room result: preliminary, not accepted.** Machinery, construction and reference fidelity are provisionally strong. Specification coverage, layout flow, navigation readability, materials and lighting remain below 90. The two title lettering contacts remain a measured technical FAIL at 5.5 mm versus the 5 mm backing tolerance, with topology REVIEW for intended open surfaces. R05 has no credit until its saved pixels and fresh technical result are independently reviewed.
