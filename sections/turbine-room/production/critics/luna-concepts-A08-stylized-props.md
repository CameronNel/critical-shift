## Disposition

It gives the maintenance bay a more specific repair cluster without importing the branding problem from A07.

A08 is an isolated prop study. It cannot establish the maintenance bay dimensions, bench apron, oil-stand position, route clearance, portal relationship, or any whole-room category. Those subjects are intentionally not scored here. Existing source dimensions and `TAKEOVER_REQUIREMENTS.md`/`CONNECTIONS.md` remain authoritative for in-room placement.

The current target is integration-ready local Turbine Room scenery. The unassigned U04 underfloor condenser, remote steam source, electrical reciprocal module and other neighbor modules are documented open interfaces, not missing A08 props. Their absence is not scored as a defect here and must not be treated as a claim that the whole facility is complete.

## What A08 replaces from A07

A08 should replace A07 as the visual source for the bench and service-prop language. Its planar forms, matte response, and restrained orange accents fit the current strict Valorant direction. Its clean blank surfaces also make it easier to adapt to the room’s existing signage and interaction contracts.

A07 remains historically rejected for branding/source safety and for its photographic product-shot drift. That rejection is not erased by A08. No A07 logos, slogans, lamp/mug/toolcase marks or related branded text may enter the scene.

## Implementation limits

- Use A08 only for the depicted small service cluster: bench construction, bearing/cradle, tool rail, socket/toolcase, task light, rag and clipboard/checklist. Do not claim A08 depicts an oil-service stand.
- Preserve the separate oil-service stand requirement from the room design and review it in the actual maintenance camera; A08 provides no visual approval for that prop.
- Preserve the local requirements: maintained bench and oil-service cabinet, bearing interaction, repair props, and the authored maintenance story.
- Place the cluster inside the existing maintenance bay and preserve the recorded operator apron and central route. A08 itself does not prove those clearances.
- Keep labels and paperwork source-authored for Critical Shift; the image’s blank/illegible surfaces do not authorize new branding or copied text.
- Maintain no teal in the prop palette and avoid realistic micro-wear, excessive fastener repetition, or a glossy asset-showcase finish.
- Validate visible contacts, support chains, evaluated geometry, interaction reach and final camera readability in the saved room.
