## Correction comparison

- **Hero work objects: improved.** C10 no longer shows a toroidal toy bearing and smooth ring-ended tool as its dominant read; C08's vise construction is clearer. Finished construction specificity remains below target.
- **Material identity/use: improved, incomplete.** The tabletop has an identifiable material, and the bare metal is more controlled. Sparse wear remains visibly schematic and most surfaces lack evidence of use.
- **Lighting hierarchy: improved slightly.** Contact and lower shadows are stronger. Broad ambient illumination still dominates C08.
- **Door/utility credibility: improved, incomplete.** The door's operating system is more readable, and conduit mounting appears better aligned in C08. The doorway still ends visually at a featureless dark plane. C10 also shows a short loose-looking vertical element below the junction box with no visible destination.
- **Human presence: improved, incomplete.** A recognizable repair cluster replaces some generic props, but its condition and arrangement still resemble a clean display.

## Highest-impact remaining defects

1. **Incomplete object-specific construction — C08 and C10.** The useful revisions to the bearing, vise and door sit alongside a bearing cradle, pump enclosure and large surrounding members with blunt, undifferentiated surfaces. The quality gap between the functional details and their supporting assets remains obvious.
2. **Uniform newness and schematic wear — both cameras.** Broad teal and pale wall surfaces remain nearly untouched. C10's small pale angular marks appear as graphic slivers, while the main contact areas read clean. The timber's very even grain and consistent color maintain a showroom impression.
3. **Weak practical-light hierarchy — C08.** The fixture is visible, but the nearby working zone does not read as a distinct localized pool of light. Much of the wall and door receives similar brightness, limiting depth and focal guidance compared with the approved references.
4. **Secondary-space/utility credibility — C08, C10.** The electrical opening has no readable continuation beyond its dark flat-looking background. The short element hanging below the junction box in C10 has no visible functional termination. Pixel review cannot establish whether these are geometrically valid.
5. **Staged human evidence — both cameras.** The rag is a regular wavy sheet, the mug is pristine, and the wall paperwork is generic. Their presence supplies a maintenance theme but not a strong impression of a particular job and recent worker activity.
