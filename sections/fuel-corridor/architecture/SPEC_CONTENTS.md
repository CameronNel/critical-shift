# Fuel Corridor contents and scope

The assigned brief requires a modular freight route between the existing refinery and reactor, believable turns and thresholds, doors, a service bypass and service junctions, sized to measured fuel handling needs. GAME_SPEC23 establishes the15–30s refinery/reactor target and requires geometry, collision, lighting, navigation, spawn markers, incident hooks, audio zones, network boundaries and validation metadata for each module. These are requirements; the specific maintenance props below are original implementation decisions.

- Refinery-side threshold and approach, measured against its existing dispatch sill.
- Freight staging and turning bay with a clear carrier park and inspection position.
- Original1.60×.90m carrier: sealed current1.245×Ø.340 cartridge, contact saddles, retaining bands and latches, lifting eyes, push handle, rubber wheels, brake hardware and inspection tag.
- 3.40 × 3.40 m internal freight sliding gate with rigid editable leaf carriages, enclosed leaf pockets, a compact geared drive with terminal box and secured supply cable, seals, protective jambs and flush sill.
- Cross passage, second turning bay, delivery leg and widened reactor approach to the existing reactor stub.
- Separate service bypass around the freight gate, returning beyond it.
- Reserved plant-services, clean/medical/compliance and waste-transfer headers. Destination rooms and their final adapters are outside this section.
- Supported pipework and cable trays, isolation valve, pressure gauge, regulator/filter bowl, coupler and stored hose. The service board occupies a dedicated recess; its handwheel extends into the gross wall margin while remaining 109.5 mm outside the dressed bypass route.
- Floor-supported maintenance bench, toolboard, usable spanners and driver, tool/consumable cases, gloves, wiping cloth, grease tin, worklight and flask. Small props serve the maintenance story without filling the travel path.
- Dispatch paperwork, service kit locker, extraction grilles, route signs, labels, safety markings and impact rails. Mounted distribution/isolation boxes, a first-aid case, clipped permit and spare-fuse packet give the branch routes purposeful service detail.
- Finished floor, wall and ceiling construction: joints, steel framing, bearing plates, gussets, fasteners and practical lights with supported fixtures.
- Dimensioned plan, interface contracts, fixed cameras, technical evidence, independently scored reviews and a cold-reopen render set.

The scene's content is not proof of runtime operation. Final delivery must distinguish authored engine handoff markers from tested engine collision/navigation/interaction. The neighboring reactor doors remain closed; refinery-to-reactor payload packing semantics and shared world placement remain unverified. The completed mine uses its authoritative shallow decline, despite legacy GAME_SPEC23's older lift wording.
