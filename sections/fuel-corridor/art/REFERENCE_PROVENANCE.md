# Visual authority and provenance

All geometry is authored originally in Python from a factory-empty Blender scene. No neighboring .blend, FBX, GLB or mesh is imported. Three original generated albedo images are used and packed: T01 floor, T02 wall and T03 woven cloth. Their source/packed hashes are checked independently of visual scoring. Other material variation and localized geometry wear are authored in the build. Text uses Blender's built-in font.

The user's corrected visual authority is preserved under art/reference/current:

- reactor-a05.png, copied from the reactor's generated/reference-a05-user-edited-orange.png.
- mine-entry.png, mine-heading.png and mine-sump.png, copied from the current mine's valorant-pass/01-entry,03-heading-slate,04-sump images.

These guide grounded Valorant construction, offwhite/mineral surfaces, charcoal steel, restrained orange, tactile material separation and warm practical-light hierarchy. Their layouts are not copied. Earlier A02/B01 and wrong teal corridor concepts are historical and do not establish current acceptance. Current ART_DIRECTION.md and ART_REFERENCE_INDEX.md from the reactor worktree override older wording on the starting branch.

The user subsequently authorized autonomous generation and approval after honest scale/equipment/flow/style/material/light/filler review, overriding the older brief's requirement to await user ratings. New C01–C10 concepts and full-view paintovers were generated with the built-in image tool. Complex carrier, gate, compact drive motor and utility machinery received separate images. Rejected generations were not silently approved: exact prompts, original images, scoring and regeneration outcomes are recorded in art/concepts/REVIEW.md and independent critic reports. Generated bitmap concepts are never presented as Blender renders.

Headless Blender 5.2 LTS is the authoritative authoring pipeline. Source hashes and named cameras are recorded with each render batch. Blender MCP live scenes were not opened or claimed; all other workers' sessions remain untouched.
