Large `FUEL TRANSFER` and `REACTOR` panels, dark leaf construction, handles, perimeter hardware, caution placards and localized scuffs provide the authored focal treatment missing from the actual blank-door crop. This remains a closed boundary concept; no passage opening is implied.

## Bounded integration cautions

- Verify the bypass's promised 2 m clear route and flush equipment fit against measured collision and circulation evidence; the concept cannot prove those conditions.
- Preserve the clear central lane and keep service props attached and visually subordinate to branch direction.
- Use verified labels, identity strings and ratings in the build rather than copying fictional concept text.
- Maintain C09 as a closed reactor boundary until neighboring route and interface evidence independently passes.

No coordinate recipe, replacement geometry prescription, GPU render, load-rating validation or full-scene acceptance is supplied in this review.
