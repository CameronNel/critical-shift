## Visible progress over style07

- The current smaller payload reads as proportionally plausible when judged against the doors and workbench; its size is not, by itself, a toy-like defect.
- C09 adds restrained localized shell wear and clearer carrier deck/parking cues.
- The service-air panel and hose read more clearly in C03.
- Lighting and contact shadow give the staged carrier more weight.

## Biggest gaps versus C06

1. **Carrier specificity at gameplay distance:** C06's current-payload treatment communicates a richer cradle, restraints and handling state. Style08 still shows a simplified trolley, with the brake/lock state not fully legible in C03/C09.
2. **Material richness:** The cask has improved localized marks, but the shell, wall panels and floor still have restrained-to-flat responses. C06 distinguishes painted metal, rubber, shell and institutional surfaces more convincingly.
3. **Focal hierarchy:** The physically correct compact payload reads small in the room. C06 compensates with stronger local detail and light hierarchy; style08 has not yet made the carrier equally important without inflating its dimensions.
4. **Worker-use scale:** The bench, tools, grease, note and inspection cues are present but collapse to small marks in C03. The maintenance story is therefore less immediate than in C06.
5. **Transfer-side finish:** The fuel-transfer portal is readable as a destination, but its side of the frame remains less detailed and less strongly lit than the service/cask area.

## Required next correction

Close the remaining visible gap through authored carrier/brake-state readability, deeper material differentiation, stronger gameplay focal hierarchy and larger-scale worker-use evidence. Preserve the measured current payload proportions; do not enlarge the cartridge to solve a composition problem. Keep wear localized and purposeful rather than adding repeated procedural noise or blanket grunge.

No numerical technical acceptance, replacement coordinates, geometry recipe, GPU render or runtime claim is supplied in this review.
