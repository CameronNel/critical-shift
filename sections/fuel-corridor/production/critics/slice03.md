## Observed defects

1. **High — material/storytelling evidence still falls short (C09).** The draped item has a broad, almost planar silhouette and papery surface. It does not establish believable fabric thickness/folds. The shell scratches are a small family of similar dark horizontal marks spread across an otherwise uniformly clean body. The result indicates use but lacks the plausible, varied contact history visible in the references.
2. **Medium — hero shape refinement is uneven (C09).** The cask's lifting/retention hardware is much more specific. In contrast, the shell has abrupt angular changes near the right end, and the carrier supports remain very plain solid forms. The close view still reveals a gap between authored functional hardware and less refined larger surfaces.
3. **Medium — broad architectural fields have limited tactile identity (C03).** The large gate/wall/floor areas are cleanly separated but remain smooth and uniform enough that the scene feels staged. A few gate kick-plate marks improve use evidence without fully changing that dominant impression.
4. **Medium — lighting hierarchy remains broad (C03).** The new station light is effective locally, but overhead fill still gives the broad architectural fields comparable visibility. The bright valve loses material/form information. This is improved lighting, not a >=90 lighting pass.
5. **Medium — threshold evidence remains partial (C03).** As in slice02, the right outer jamb is beyond the image edge. The visible gate height, leaves, header and floor contact are useful, but the complete threshold cannot be verified from this image. This is an evidence limitation, not a claim that the scene lacks that construction.
