## Observed strengths

- The concept moves full01 C02 toward the approved facility language through the A05-like neutral panel field, charcoal framing and controlled orange accents.
- The large `04`/reactor marking, chevrons and floor arrow establish destination intent quickly.
- Ceiling cable routing and practical fixtures provide a believable overhead service layer, and the clear floor retains transport circulation.
- The visible construction language is coherent with the current corridor slice and does not drift toward generic photo-grunge or glossy plastic.

## Regeneration blockers

- The far-left reactor branch must read as spatial circulation from the image, rather than being communicated only by arrows and a destination label.
- The crossing needs slightly stronger, purposeful junction construction and environmental service cues so the empty field does not dominate the composition. Keep the central route clear.
- Preserve the restrained A05/C06 material and color treatment while adding specificity; broad clean surfaces alone do not supply the same authored construction density as the references.

Because logical flow is 88 and filler/environmental detail is 89, this concept is below the required 90 threshold and should be regenerated before use as the full01 crossing visual target. Exact geometry, coordinates, topology, technical gates and route clearance remain unverified here.

No coordinate recipe, replacement geometry prescription, GPU render or full-scene acceptance is supplied in this review.
