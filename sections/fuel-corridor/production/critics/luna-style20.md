## Visible progress over style16

- Dark backing behind the carrier gives the compact payload a stronger silhouette and focal role.
- The floor remains quiet and no longer competes with the route or cart.
- D02 gloves now read as dark fabric with volume/folds rather than flat or molded tokens; the open/ring tools retain a clear forged-tool silhouette.
- D03 gauge, regulator and utility fittings have stronger construction and material separation.
- The supported task-light and repaired labels/attachments improve localized work hierarchy.

## Remaining visible cautions

- The compact carrier remains modest in the C03 wide frame by design; its D01/C09 close views must remain part of the evidence set.
- The transfer-side architecture has less prop density than the service/workbench side. Preserve its route readability without filling the quiet field with decorative clutter.
- The cask shell is a broad smooth painted surface; keep its subdued wear and roughness differentiation consistent in the final material setup.
