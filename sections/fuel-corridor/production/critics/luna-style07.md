## Visible progress over style06

- Ceiling panels and fasteners read more intentionally.
- Wall framing and utility brackets have stronger rhythm.
- Warm light and contact shadow better ground the carrier.
- C09 retains the current-payload proportions and clearer cartridge identification.

## Biggest gaps versus C06

1. **Procedural-looking panel mottling:** C09 shows broad repeated cloud shapes across the wall panels. C06 uses broad clean fields with selective, plausible wear; the current pattern reads like an applied noise layer and weakens the material identity.
2. **Carrier specificity at gameplay scale:** C06 gives the smaller current cartridge a more authored cradle, restraint and handling read. Style07 still shows a small, simple trolley, with brake/lock operation absent from the fixed views.
3. **Functional focal balance:** C06 connects the service recess, staged cask and transfer portal through light and mass. Style07's transfer side remains visually dark and the cart does not carry enough presence at C03 distance.
4. **Human-use readability:** The bench and maintenance cues exist, but they collapse to tiny marks from the hero camera. The room therefore still feels lightly occupied compared with C06.
5. **Material and construction finish:** The palette is right, but broad smooth shell/frame surfaces, limited localized wear and repeated architectural forms remain below the required A05/C06 finish.

## Required next correction

The next slice must visibly approach C06 in restrained authored wear, carrier specificity and gameplay-scale worker-use readability while preserving the clear route and current payload. Remove or substantially reduce any repeated procedural-looking wall mottling. Do not compensate with blanket grunge, extra labels, random clutter or repeated bevelled boxes.

No numerical technical acceptance, replacement coordinates, geometry recipe, GPU render or runtime claim is supplied in this review.
