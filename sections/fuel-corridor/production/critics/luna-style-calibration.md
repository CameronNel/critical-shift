## Calibration decision

The rejected corridor concepts should be replaced with grounded stylized industrial art. `reference-a05-user-edited-orange.png` is the primary authority for the Valorant-style visual language: clean authored architecture, strong panel rhythm, specific equipment silhouettes, neutral light surfaces, charcoal structure and deliberate orange accents. The mine plates provide the rough-material and maintenance context: dark framed excavation, warm practical pools, restrained orange safety hardware and broad, painterly rock variation. They are context references, not permission for a cave-only palette or photographic grunge.

## Pixel-observed characteristics to preserve

- **Value structure:** approximately two-thirds of the visible area is quiet neutral material (warm off-white, concrete, slate or restrained grey); charcoal framing, recesses and utility runs create the dark counterweight. Large surfaces are readable as broad fields with seams or structural rhythm, not as noisy texture sheets.
- **Accent discipline:** orange is a small functional accent family, visually about 5–10% of a typical frame: edge bands, handrails, hazard blocks, trim, labels and selected equipment panels. It is concentrated in connected routes and work points. Emergency red is sparse. Cyan is a reserved machine-state/emissive cue and should remain a small focal area rather than a general room wash.
- **Construction language:** important forms have a readable primary silhouette plus visible secondary construction: collars, frames, brackets, panel breaks, fasteners, doors, rails, housings and service connections. The A05 control banks are tall, layered assemblies with a clear functional center; the mine supports and pipes show believable attachment to ribs and walls. Bevels finish a designed form; repeated bevelled cubes do not establish the style.
- **Material response:** painted metal is mostly matte/semi-matte with broad value and roughness variation; bare steel has controlled highlights; rubber is dark and quiet; concrete and rock are low-frequency and tactile; wet service surfaces reflect only where water explains it. There is no uniform satin plastic response across walls, machinery and props.
- **Lighting:** practical fixtures visibly affect nearby surfaces. Warm amber pools sit against neutral/cool ambient structure, with contact shadows and falloff giving equipment weight. A05 uses brighter architectural fields and controlled cyan machinery; the mine uses warmer, darker service pockets. The corridor should retain a clear focal hierarchy without flattening every surface to the same exposure.
- **Wear:** use is localized and authored: edge scuffs, repaired paint, damp staining near a sump, worn rail touch points, replacement panels and selective grime at service interfaces. The mine plates show broad rock variation and wetness around the basin; they do not show uniform dirt sprayed over every object.
- **Signage:** sparse, aligned, blocky industrial labels that identify a route, hazard or work function. A focal zone may have one primary sign and a small supporting marker; text must not replace weak silhouettes or become wall wallpaper.
- **Human scale:** doors, handrails, carts, work surfaces and control panels must provide immediate adult scale anchors. The reference proportions read as a worker-built facility: equipment is substantial but operable, rails are protective rather than decorative, and routes retain visible walking space.

## Required read for Fuel Corridor concepts

At gameplay camera height, a replacement concept must read immediately as a maintained fuel-handling/work corridor with a clear route, a functional freight or service focal point, and adjacent architecture that belongs to the same facility as A05. It should show quiet wall or structural areas between denser work clusters. Props need plausible support and use: a trolley, restraint, tool, panel, hose, record or PPE item should explain the work in the frame.

## Automatic style vetoes

Reject a concept if its dominant pixel read is generic low-poly, a glossy web-demo, uniform plastic, photoreal photographic grunge, flat evenly lit grey, random orange colour confetti, dense sci-fi greebling, floating/unsupported props, toy-like PPE, or signage used as decoration. A technically complete build cannot pass this calibration if the screenshot still looks like a placeholder industrial kit.

## Luna review gate for replacement concepts

Score only what is visible in the submitted pixels. Require at least 90 for scale/circulation, shape/art direction, hierarchy, materials, lighting, colour and environmental storytelling, with zero critical defects. Do not award points for claimed objects, hidden scene structure or intended future polish. Once a full Blender build exists, review the fixed cameras and technical evidence independently; this calibration does not approve expansion or runtime integration.
