# Luna independent full-corridor review — F9ci

## Decision

**F9ci is the strongest reviewed full pass so far, but it does not pass the art gate.** It makes several of the corridor’s work steps read as actual destinations: C01 and C04 now show the full transfer board clear of the structural post; C05 differentiates the reactor return from the waste handoff; C08 groups the linen return/log into a mounted bay; and E01 stages a tagged cartridge at the receipt point beside its matching B/017 bin. The freight mechanism and reactor threshold remain legible in their close and wide views. These are visible gains over F8ci, not acceptance by effort or build status.

The comparison is about shared art qualities, not matching room functions: this freight/service corridor must preserve its protected footprint and closed external portals, and does not need spawn’s briefing or locker rooms. F9 has good individual stations, formed portals and real material changes, while several views still resolve as a sequence of large smooth painted fields, regular ceiling cassettes and tile runs between the stations. Spawn also has quiet wall fields and repeated floor tiles; its advantage is how framing, varied material response, soft goods, paper and warm/cool light make its local groups feel composed together at more than one depth. F9 can express the same refinement through industrial thresholds and service bays. Its remaining gap is more consistent route-level differentiation, not a missing human-room type. Quiet space is appropriate; broad coverage or extra props are not the remedy. Scores of 98 do not pass: every category and area must be strictly greater than 98, and two materially stable accepted cycles are required.

I opened all 19 full F9ci views at 1280×853, the closed freight-leaf diagnostic, and all four actual 1280×720 spawn references. I independently verified the native checkpoint SHA256 (`68d9f2e66a8ed36fd395691a8f0ead9c726a619116000d6e04d6fdf7bbd7ac96`), the 19 full-image hashes against the full manifest, and the closed-leaf image hash against its manifest. The cold-validation record matches the native and recipe hashes and reports PASS with zero failures. Cold checks and geometry/build counts add no art points and do not establish Unity/runtime behavior.

## Highest-impact remaining repairs

1. **Build a more coherent process threshold around the existing C02 extractor.** Keep the clear central machinery and existing route. Use the adjacent wall returns and current panel breaks to form one visibly bounded process/service bay, carrying its established blue-gray casing and local light treatment into a limited part of the surrounding field. In E03, let the same process-bay treatment read through the freight frame. Do not texture every panel or add unrelated props.

2. **Give the bypass a wider-view destination using its existing clean threshold.** In C07, shape the existing wall returns and ceiling-spine termination into a recognizable clean-service threshold that leads to the S02 approach shown in E02. Retain open floor, the existing CLEAN / WIPE cues and the necessary quiet tile field; do not add an aisle-spanning frame or generic dressing.

3. **Carry material distinction beyond the close task objects.** In C02/C04/C08, use a few connected construction surfaces already adjacent to the extractor, cart-repair bay and linen-return station to repeat their process-appropriate materials: formed service steel at the extractor, replaceable impact timber/metal at cart height, and washable ceramic/metal at linen handling. Keep each use localized and keep unrelated work areas distinct.

4. **Improve route-level lighting progression without flattening contact.** Preserve the working warm entry/refinery pools, cool clean zone, and D05 flood. Across C02/C05/C07/C10, vary the practical fill and wall bounce so each task threshold has a readable approach and falloff into the next zone. Retain dark recesses and contact shadows where geometry occludes the light.

5. **Keep the successful task identities separate.** Preserve the C05 reactor return and waste handoff as independent bays. Keep C08 clean stock opposite its linen-return/log point, relying on the existing clean-air ceiling spine to connect the route. The F9 framing helps; additional cross-system or cross-aisle structures would weaken the functional reading.

## Evidence and limits

The native SHA256 and all 20 full/closed image hashes match their recorded evidence. The full views are 1280×853 RGB at 32 samples; the spawn references are 1280×720 RGBA. The closed freight image is a static leaf diagnostic. It does not prove runtime motion, collider behavior or continuous sweep. The fixed renders do not establish hidden contacts, Unity lighting/performance or gameplay interaction. No art points are given for code, counts, checks, hidden objects or claimed work outside the opened pixels.

**Formal decision: FAIL.** No category or area exceeds 98. **A first stable accepted full cycle does not exist**; this F9ci cycle is below the required threshold, and two stable accepted cycles remain unestablished.
