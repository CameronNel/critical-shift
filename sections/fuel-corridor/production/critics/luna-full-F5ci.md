# Luna independent full-corridor review — F5ci

## Decision

**F5ci is a clear visual improvement over F4, but it does not pass the art gate.** All seven categories and all nine areas remain below the required strict score of 98. The most convincing gains are the large cool-blue process bay and readable directional beam at the east turn (`C05_EAST_TURN`), the visibly tiled ceramic clean route and stocked return shelf (`C08_SERVICE_JUNCTION`, `E02_CLEAN_APPROACH`), a more coherent service/cask staging wall (`C01_ENTRY`, `C03_HERO`, `C09_MATERIALS`, `D01_CARRIER_OPERATION`), and a more useful warm bench task light and timber surface (`D02_WORKBENCH`). The reactor doors have a more distinct formed-panel rhythm, and the plant endpoint now has a practical hose reel and connected overhead runs (`C06_REACTOR_THRESHOLD`, `D04_REACTOR_WIDE`, `C10_PLANT_HEADER`). These are visible built changes, not technical-validation credit.

Spawn remains much more varied and human across the whole viewed space. Its four references sustain strong warm/cool room changes, personal and work items, notice boards, lockers, PPE, benches, and deliberate focal lighting. F5's more varied focal assemblies still sit inside long runs of the same cream upper wall, navy lower panels, black framing, ceiling grid, and beige/blue floor cadence. The improvement is material and area hierarchy; the remaining gap is still the large-scale route composition, differentiated architectural masses, broader lighting hierarchy, and distributed human use.

I opened all 19 full F5ci fixed views, the closed-leaf `E03_FREIGHT_LEAF` diagnostic, and all four spawn reference renders. The full manifest binds the 19 1280×853, 32-sample Cycles images to `checkpoints/fuel_full_F5ci.blend`, SHA256 `5f7de9035b966bf1e152f338e25d9cab1b8d5922db36b1e49303b9562aee6536`; all 19 PNG hashes match. The closed-pose diagnostic binds to the same native SHA and its image hash matches its manifest. I independently calculated SHA256 for all four reference PNGs and opened their pixels. The spawn room remains fixed at 100. Cold validation and geometry counts add no art points.

## Highest-impact remaining corrections

1. **Give the long route views one more level of architectural hierarchy.** In `C02_PRIMARY_ROUTE`, `C04_REVERSE`, and the distant end of `C08_SERVICE_JUNCTION`, redesign only the largest blank wall/ceiling masses as area-specific service bays or stepped frames with visible connected runs. Preserve the clear lane and quiet negative space. The current repeated cream upper fields, navy dado, black framing, and ceiling grid still dominate these images; small scattered props will not address that scale of gap.

2. **Finish the two route-scale clean signs.** In `C07_BYPASS`, enlarge or simplify the `CLEAN TRANSFER` plate lettering enough to read in the wide camera. In `E02_CLEAN_APPROACH`, keep the corrected fully framed header but increase lettering/background value separation and face illumination. The words exist in both views; the remaining problem is the read at the actual approach distance.

3. **Resolve the freight mechanism's hard light/shadow split.** In `D05_GATE_MECHANISM`, lower the beam hotspot or broaden the fixture/fill so the motor remains focal while the long lower guard, rail, brackets, and supports retain readable value separation. Reduce the lower channel's visual weight with a formed, more open guard profile. Preserve the visible drive, auxiliary beam, support path, and the closed diagnostic's leaf construction.

4. **Add one functional step to delivery/waste and one to bypass use.** In `E01_WASTE_APPROACH`, pair the bin with a wall-mounted or floor-supported transfer/sorting fixture and a visible tether/label workflow that remains outside the travel lane. In `D06_SERVICE_RECESS`/`C07_BYPASS`, make the pressure panel's service purpose legible through its connected line and one supported maintenance/handover item. These are two bounded functional additions, not a general clutter pass.

5. **Separate the plant and reactor thresholds at the large-form level.** Keep the new formed leaves, apertures, service fittings, and `C10` hose reel. Give `C06`/`D04` and `C10` distinct adjacent process frames or major service silhouettes so their current shared paired-leaf/panel grammar does not make the different stations feel like variants of the same door bay.

6. **Carry material contrast beyond focal props.** Keep the clean ceramic, warm bench timber, orange safety group, and cool east bay. Add only a small number of functional large-surface changes in `C02`/`C04`/`C08` and the threshold approaches so the main route has more tactile and value variation than smooth cream paint, navy paint, and the repeated beige/blue tile system. Do not use uniform wear or texture noise to simulate this.

## Evidence and limits

Visual findings use only the opened pixels from `production/renders/review/full-F5ci` and `closed-F5ci`, plus the four opened spawn references in `production/renders/reference`. All 19 full image hashes and the closed diagnostic hash match their manifests; both manifests bind to native `fuel_full_F5ci.blend`, SHA256 `5f7de9035b966bf1e152f338e25d9cab1b8d5922db36b1e49303b9562aee6536`. The reference PNGs were also hashed directly. Build/cold status, triangle counts, and support validation are not used to award art points.

The closed freight view is a rigid leaf diagnostic. It supports visual inspection of the constructed leaves and their apertures; it does not prove runtime actuation, collision, or a continuous sweep. The normal open views correctly conceal parked leaves. Render views also cannot establish unseen contact behavior, gameplay traversal, Unity lighting/performance, or appearances outside these fixed views.
