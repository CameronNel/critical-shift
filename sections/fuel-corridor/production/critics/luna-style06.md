## Visible improvements over style05

- Local warm lighting and darker recesses give the room more depth.
- Additional brackets and panel hardware improve the wall rhythm.
- C09 shows clearer cartridge labeling, strap/lifting-eye construction and carrier contact shadow.
- The route arrow and staging condition remain legible.

## Biggest gaps versus C06

1. **Carrier specificity and scale:** C06 makes the current smaller payload a deliberate, detailed freight object. Style06 still presents a small simplified carrier, and its brake/lock state is absent from the gameplay evidence.
2. **Material response:** C06 visibly separates painted metal, rubber, shell, floor and panel wear. Style06 has the right palette but remains too smooth and uniform in both C03 and C09.
3. **Readable work story:** C06's gloves, grease, tools and paperwork form a legible cluster. Style06 contains related props but they collapse into tiny marks at the hero distance.
4. **Functional focal hierarchy:** C06 balances service recess, staged cask and transfer portal. Style06's portal side is dark and the carrier lacks enough visual mass or local detail to carry the same sequence.
5. **Art-directed construction:** C06 has more specific brackets, fasteners, housings and layered transitions. Style06 still relies on repeated beam/panel language and therefore remains below the anti-blockout gate.

## Required next correction

The next slice must visibly approach C06 in carrier specificity, materially differentiated surfaces, localized lighting, readable worker-use cues and functional focal hierarchy while preserving the clear route. Do not address these observed gaps with blanket grunge, extra labels, random clutter or repeated bevelled boxes.

No numerical technical acceptance, replacement coordinates, geometry recipe, GPU render or runtime claim is supplied in this review.
