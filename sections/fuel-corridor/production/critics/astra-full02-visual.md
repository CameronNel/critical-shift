1. **Major — destination graphics lose meaning through occlusion (C02/C07).** In C02, the far reactor label is small and thin, with its initial portion and directional cue interrupted by the central vertical post. The two bright wall lights mainly emphasize the blank field to its left. In C07, the plant direction at the left opening is largely hidden behind projecting steel, leaving orange chevrons; the MEDICAL / CLEAN subtitle on the forward wall sits directly across a horizontal panel seam. The approved crossing and bypass concepts provide stronger, unobscured destination information.

2. **Major — clean-junction identity remains weak (C08; supporting C01).** C08 shows repeated wall bays, a largely edge-on door assembly and a small distant W mark, without a clearly readable clean-service destination in the view. Edge paint gives orientation but does not name the branch. C01 still gives stronger visual priority to the straight SERVICE route than to freight continuation. These observations concern what is visible from the supplied approaches, not whether labels exist elsewhere.

3. **Moderate — recessed station loses function in shadow and framing (D06).** Gauge and orange wheel are readable, but the dark backplate, pipework and hose merge with surrounding steel. A horizontal crossmember and large gussets occupy the upper identity area; no SERVICE AIR heading is visible. D03 shows how much more clearly the same type of equipment can communicate material and function. This is an observed visibility problem; no collision conclusion is inferred from the image.

4. **Moderate — gate diagnostic does not clearly expose its stated subject (D05).** The image is dominated by the FUEL TRANSFER sign, lamp and intense horizontal reflection. The orange drive housing is cropped at the far left. A readable roller/hanger/drive relationship is not established. Mechanism geometry is not declared absent; this view provides limited evidence of it.

5. **Moderate — expanded door and corridor treatment remains less specific than references (C06/D04, C07/C08).** Reactor identity is now clear, but the broad grey leaf areas and repeated small plates remain visually simple relative to the approved reactor-door concept. Similar framing, panels and rails recur across service approaches without equally clear local purpose. The developed carrier/bench area still carries more of the scene's individual character.

6. **Minor — close-detail finish can feel arranged/coarse (D02).** Tools and bench contents remain neatly presented; textile weave is pronounced relative to the glove forms. These are smaller concerns than route hierarchy, and do not negate the successful material separation.
