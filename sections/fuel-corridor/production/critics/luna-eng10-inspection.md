**Decision: REJECT eng10 visual component for another art/material correction.**

I inspected the exact frozen image `production/renders/review/eng10-inspection/D05_GATE_MECHANISM.png` and compared it with the approved `art/concepts/C10-gate-drive-r01.png`, reactor A05 and the mine entry reference. This is a bounded inspection of the gate-drive component only; it receives no full-cycle or full-room credit.

The render now exposes a readable motor, longitudinal fins, nameplate, terminal box, cable route, mounting shelf, safety plate and surrounding drive housing. Edge-specific wear, machined-versus-coated separation and the compact authored contrast are understated relative to A05 and the mine reference.

## Concrete visible deficits

- Material identity is below threshold because the motor, shelf and surrounding steel collapse into similar diffuse gray values; the required coated, machined and worn distinctions are not strong enough in the pixels.
- Art fidelity is below threshold because the component lacks the concept’s crisp edge hierarchy and localized authored wear, reading as a clean generic industrial motor within the otherwise grounded facility.
- Equipment is inspectable, but the coupling/guard transition is less immediately legible than the motor body, so the component should retain a clear visual relationship to the horizontal drive housing.

No coordinate recipe, source edit or replacement geometry is prescribed. The review diagnoses visible finish and hierarchy only. The stated 0.30 m motor length, 0.18 m body diameter and 0.20 m fin envelope are not proven by this image and require separate engineering measurement.

## Review boundary

This report scores only the supplied eng10 inspection pixels against the approved visual references. It does not infer support, clearance, collision, packed assets, route behavior, technical correctness or full-scene acceptance. Eng11 engineering changes may improve construction evidence, but this report identifies material and art-direction deficits that remain visible in eng10.

**Eng10 component result: REJECT.** Equipment readability, construction and lighting pass locally; material identity (88) and Valorant art fidelity (89) remain below the required 90 threshold.
