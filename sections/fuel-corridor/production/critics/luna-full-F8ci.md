# Luna independent full-corridor review — F8ci

## Decision

**F8ci is a strong and visible improvement over F7ci, but it does not pass the art gate.** The larger service assemblies and connected human-use nodes make the corridor more purposeful: the refinery has a transfer-check board and cart-impact boards, the process bay has a formed extractor with filter/fan/instrument and continuous riser, the reverse corridor groups cart repair with its access break, the reactor return/isolation points carry lockout cues, the clean route has a used-linen station, and D05 now has a roof-supported soft flood. The C10 water valve is also visually separated from the ceiling fitting, and D06’s hose no longer crosses the purge/check label.

The remaining difference from the 100-point spawn reference is not a shortage of detail on every wall. Spawn itself has broad smooth pink/navy fields and a repeated tile floor. Its advantage is how its framed side openings connect those quiet fields to readable human-use groups: briefing material, lockers/PPE, a handover board, storage and furniture. F8’s task groups now move toward that model, but several wide route views still read as long institutional wall/ceiling runs punctuated by highly localized fixtures. Materials and route-wide light variation also remain less rich than spawn. Scores of 98 do not pass: acceptance requires strictly greater than 98 for every category and every area.

I opened all 19 F8ci images at 1280×853, the closed freight-leaf diagnostic, and the four actual 1280×720 spawn references. I independently verified the native checkpoint SHA256 (`3629551c379a87f6ee2983f5ad535036b9a34f783cc0ba9365c210a2c05fa80a`), all 19 full-render hashes, and the closed diagnostic hash against their manifests. Cold validation reports PASS with zero failures; geometry/build evidence adds no visual points. The cold check does not establish Unity/runtime lighting or interaction.

## Highest-impact remaining repairs

1. **Strengthen the route sequence with a few framed task nodes, not general wall coverage.** Keep the successful C01/C04 repair grouping, C05/D04 reactor lockout points and C08 linen return. In `C04`, contain the existing transfer board, ring spanners and cart-height impact-board run within one framed or backplated cart-service bay; let the board run terminate cleanly at the frame and add a modest task strip. In `C05`, mount the isolation tag/key plate directly on the return-louver/pipe frame beneath the REACTOR / 02 beam, with one route-readable state cue. In `C08`, consolidate the clean shelf, used-linen hood/bag and WIPE / LOG cue into one slim framed linen-return module, with a USED LINEN plaque above the bag. Preserve the broad quiet planes, walkway, and all fixed external connections. The goal is a connected sequence of work areas like spawn’s visible side-room layers, not more isolated props.

2. **Finish route-scale material distinction.** Keep the timber cart-impact boards and soft linen bag as function-specific accents. In `C01/C04/C08`, let those materials and the existing clean/refinery finishes influence a few adjacent construction surfaces (for example a replaceable timber/metal guard assembly at the cart service point or a washable return cabinet at linen handling). Avoid blanket texture noise or unrelated color patches; most wide-view shell still reads as similarly smooth painted panels.

3. **Resolve the clipped C01 transfer-check board.** Reposition or resize its mount within the fixed camera composition so the board’s outer frame and useful checklist are visible, while retaining the handover tools and clear passage. Do not move the camera.

4. **Make two already useful focal labels survive route distance.** `C05/D04` lockout tags and `C08`’s used-linen identification are visible as small details but not yet strong wide-view task cues. Increase only the key state/area word or icon and keep secondary text at its current restrained scale.

5. **Hold the D05 lighting balance.** Keep the angled flood and readable motor/frame planes. Do not fill the natural contact shadows under supports or inside the far recess; no repair is required there based on these pixels.

No art points are given for code, counts, hidden objects or claimed work outside the opened pixels.
