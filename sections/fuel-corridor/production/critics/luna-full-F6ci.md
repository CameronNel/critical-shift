# Luna independent full-corridor review — F6ci

## Decision

**F6ci is a visible improvement over F5ci, but it does not pass the art gate.** All seven visual categories and all nine areas remain below the required strict score of 98. F6 adds more legible threshold zoning, makes the new waste seal/receipt station visible in its approach, gives the plant leaves a clearer ventilation and trim language, and physically breaks the freight lower channel into open bays. Those are pixel-visible repairs. The largest continuing gap is the corridor as a complete composition: multiple fixed views still show long cream/navy wall bands and regular ceiling panels around isolated focal fixtures, with far less area-to-area silhouette, material, and human-use variation than the spawn reference.

The comparison to spawn is based on sustained visual qualities—warm/cool room changes, composed thresholds, material variety, clear object silhouettes, and purposeful human-use traces—not on requiring the corridor to duplicate irrelevant spawn props or fill every quiet wall. F6's distinct clean finish, orange process wall, workbench, carrier, waste station and plant fittings each serve the corridor's use. Their improvement does not yet bring the whole route to spawn's density of authored composition and finish.

I opened all 19 full F6ci images, the closed freight-leaf diagnostic, and all four spawn reference renders. The full manifest binds the 19 1280×853, 32-sample Cycles images to `checkpoints/fuel_full_F6ci.blend`, SHA256 `ae37ef4b7011d32170ca6a95c461f255670df7db7fd9d8ce98db72a438824d0f`; all 19 image hashes match. The closed-pose manifest uses the same scene SHA and its image hash matches. I independently calculated SHA256 for the four reference PNGs and opened their pixels. The native checkpoint file itself matches the supplied SHA. Cold validation reports PASS with zero failures; it and the geometry counts add no art points.

## Remaining repairs, bounded to visible views

1. **Give the route views one or two larger, connected architectural differences.** In `C02_PRIMARY_ROUTE` and `C04_REVERSE`, the broad near walls and standard ceiling bays still dominate the view around the improved orange station and threshold floor. In `C08_SERVICE_JUNCTION`, the far clean run remains long and sparsely articulated. Preserve clear travel space and existing room ports; develop a small number of deep service/portal silhouettes with visible connected runs so the major functional stations change the architecture, not only the colored wall fields.

2. **Finish D05's light and frame read without rebuilding the repaired lower silhouette.** Keep the three visible open bays and pale flanges. Move or reduce the upper-left fixture so it sits inside the image composition, then add enough soft fill or local value separation to reveal the rear web, brackets and rail. The remaining issue is lighting/framing, not the former continuous-box mass.

3. **Make the E01 handoff legible as a sequence.** Keep the new mounted receipt unit and bin. Add one clear relation between them—such as a supported transfer surface or restrained label/route cue—so the viewer understands where an item is recorded and placed. Avoid turning the broad door approach into a prop cluster.

4. **Integrate the bypass service story into the reviewed frame.** `D06_SERVICE_RECESS` remains a well-lit panel/hose close-up but shows no separate service kit; if that kit is meant to resolve the isolated-fixture concern, place a useful portion inside this existing view and connect it to the panel's maintenance task. The C07 direction lettering can also be scaled for approach distance while preserving its clean-transfer plate.

5. **Differentiate the plant and reactor around their portals.** Keep the improved plant louvers and hose reel and the reactor's formed shields/apertures. In `C10` and `D04`/`C06`, strengthen one large surrounding process silhouette or adjacent connected assembly at each station so their different uses read from the broad views without adding another generic door layer.

6. **Carry functional surface changes across the long walls selectively.** Retain the warm refinery floor patch and cool clean floor as functional transition cues. In `C02`/`C04`/`C08`, give the largest still-plain shell zones a small number of meaningful material/value changes tied to process use. Their current smooth wall fields and ceiling grid are the main parity gap; wear noise or unrelated decorative props would not address it.

All 19 full image hashes and the closed diagnostic hash match their manifests; both manifests bind to native `fuel_full_F6ci.blend`, SHA256 `ae37ef4b7011d32170ca6a95c461f255670df7db7fd9d8ce98db72a438824d0f`. The native file hash matches, and the cold-validation record reports PASS with zero failures. Technical/build checks receive no visual points.

The closed freight view is a rigid leaf diagnostic. It supports visual inspection of the constructed leaf faces and lit apertures, but it does not prove runtime actuation, collision or a continuous sweep. The normal open-state views correctly conceal parked leaves. Render views cannot establish hidden contact behavior, gameplay traversal, Unity lighting/performance, or appearance outside these fixed cameras.
