# Luna F04 preview review — Fuel Corridor

**Status:** partial visual feedback only; no final category or acceptance score  
**Pack:** `production/renders/review/final-F04-preview`  
**Available at review:** C01_ENTRY, C02_PRIMARY_ROUTE, C03_HERO (960 × 640 preview renders)

I inspected each arriving preview PNG directly. The complete 1440 × 960 pack and cold evidence remain required for the 17-category final decision.

## Confirmed improvements over F00

- C01 has a clearer first read: the freight floor arrow, carrier and new orange F02 / REACTOR cue share the route composition, while SERVICE / BYPASS no longer overwhelms the entry.
- C02 removes the competing near-left foreground F02 board. S03 / WASTE and F02 / REACTOR are now readable as separate destination cues, and the route no longer shows the F00 black floor patch.
- C03 keeps the strong carrier, service-bench and F02 threshold composition while retaining the added orange destination cue. The S02 / CLEAN board is visible from the approach instead of being lost at the far edge.
- Across C01–C03 the palette remains grounded warm off-white, charcoal and orange, with no visible teal drift.

## Bounded remaining observations

- C01's foreground corridor still carries a large quiet wall/floor field, so the freight cue depends heavily on the floor marking and distant carrier. Confirm that this remains intentional in the full-resolution hero and entry views.
- C02 has strong sign legibility, but the side jambs and orange guards occupy substantial edge area. Check the full-resolution composition for any remaining sign occlusion and verify that the branch arrow reads as one route direction rather than overlapping marks.
- C03's top-right `FG01 / FREIGHT` header is clipped by the image frame, with no text running off its backing and no physical occlusion. Because C03 is the fixed staging/bench/service/freight composition rather than a front elevation of FG01, this is an incidental crop; the dedicated gate approach and other views must carry the full FG01 navigation proof.
- The previews show the black floor artifact absent in these views; C07 and C08 still need direct inspection in the complete pack before the F00 lighting/circulation hold can be closed.

**Luna preview result:** F04 direction is materially improved in the first three views. No score or approval issued from this partial pack.
