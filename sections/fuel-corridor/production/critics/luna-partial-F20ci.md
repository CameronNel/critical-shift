# Luna partial rejection — F20ci

## Decision

**F20ci is rejected for this cycle; no full quality scores are assigned.** The actual assembled-map C05 east-turn view contains a visible bright opening beside the closed waste/handoff portal. In the 960×640 image, the ROI at x=697–725, y=412–455 reads as a tall slit into a much brighter exterior connection, interrupting the closed leaf and jamb silhouette. It is a concrete integration finish defect, not a request for generalized coverage.

The supplied ray diagnosis confirms the visible cause: rays 710/420, 710/430 and 710/450 pass around the inset waste jamb and first hit the exterior connection floor at x=16.80–18.43; they do not hit fuel geometry. The waste portal sits inset 0.50 m from the protected outer-wall front and its frame spans the opening, but it lacks full-depth side returns. At the oblique C05 view, the resulting reveal exposes the exterior. The seam overlaps added to the leaves do not close this separate jamb-side sightline. A fitted full-depth jamb return and roof closure should block the exterior sightline while preserving the protected wall cores, leaves, interface and real fixtures.

The F20 native full views, closed freight diagnostic and four native-lit detail views were reviewed, along with 11 of 19 actual assembled-map views: D01 and C01–C10. Their image hashes match the hosted manifests. The archived F20 native, build manifest and cold validation pair to SHA-256 `bd497c63408a406086bbfb744f68fe1c67b2375e65bc1104750836dc2d0f4177`; all seven recipe-input hashes and recipe fingerprint `947f7742ec32595eabb4b000560b9615fd1d2251dbb623c595a40273797d6c25` match the published source, and cold validation passes with zero failures. These establish evidence pairing only; they do not offset the visible defect.

F21 is not a confirming stability cycle. I opened and hash-checked all 24 F21 native/full, closed and detail captures against manifests paired to native SHA-256 `4168eef541ff47c1a63ca21587d084355a29a5ce5200d7032389da2e3f3de2d7`, but no F21 assembled-map images were available. Its native images therefore cannot establish that the F20 actual-map defect has been repaired or that a full materially stable accepted pair exists. F20 itself has only 11 of 19 actual-map views. Both cycles remain incomplete for all-seven-category/all-nine-area scoring.

In the supplied pixels, the repair stiles at the waste, clean and plant doors read as continuous fitted meeting hardware, and the entry route, work stations, damaged floor margins, carrier, utility assemblies and local practical pools remain legible. Those positive observations do not clear the C05 jamb reveal. I used the four actual spawn references as the craft and finish standard and PR54 reactor references only for the intended worn atmosphere; I do not transfer prior scores.

## Evidence limits

This is a bounded rejection of incomplete F20/F21 evidence, not a numerical full-cycle grade. F20's other eight actual-map views were not supplied, and F21 has no assembled-map pack. The C05 pixel defect and ray diagnosis support the described exterior sightline; they do not establish unrelated whole-map, Unity, runtime, controller, performance or motion behavior.
