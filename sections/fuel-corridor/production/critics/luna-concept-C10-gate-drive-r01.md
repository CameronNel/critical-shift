# Fuel Corridor Luna independent concept review — C10 gate drive r01

**Decision: PASS concept for build, with dimensional verification still required.**

I inspected the exact `art/concepts/C10-gate-drive-r01.png` pixels against the actual eng07 inspection frame `production/renders/review/eng07-inspection/D05_GATE_MECHANISM.png`, current reactor A05 and the mine entry reference. The concept presents one coherent close inspection view rather than a collage. It preserves the existing head, horizontal drive housing and gate-above-aperture relationship while adding a compact geared motor, supported terminal box, cable gland/routing, guard/bracket parts, safety markings and a small warm task light.

The concept resolves the main visible weakness in eng07: the actual frame reads as a dark, plain cylindrical drive with little construction or operational identity. The concept gives the motor clear end bells, cooling fins, flange bolts, a mounted terminal box, a guarded coupling transition and a readable inspection plate. The broad charcoal/off-white/orange treatment, localized wear and controlled practical lighting stay within the grounded Valorant language established by A05 and the mine reference. It does not read as generic photo grunge, glossy plastic or an oversized sci-fi machine.

## Observed remaining risks

- The concept visually suggests the requested compact scale but cannot establish the exact contract dimensions. Build measurement must independently verify the motor envelope and keep it above the aperture.
- The coupling/guard relationship is readable in the concept, but its physical continuity and attachment must be confirmed in the built render; no acceptance is inferred from the generated image alone.
- Preserve the restrained task-light intensity and the visible terminal-box mounting. Losing either would return the view toward the dark, generic eng07 silhouette.

No visual sub-90 defect is present in this concept. These are verification points for implementation, not coordinate or replacement-geometry prescriptions.

## Review boundary

This is an independent concept-pixel review only. Exact dimensions, clearances, support, collision behavior, route proof, packed assets, technical correctness and the final Blender render remain unverified here. The concept is approved as the visual direction for C10 gate-drive construction because every requested visual criterion scores at least 91.
