# Luna independent visual review — style S3

## Gate decision

**No dominant hard visual veto, and the visual direction is safe to keep developing. The style-validation slice is not yet a PASS for full rollout.** The required door/freight-gate construction is not visually inspectable in the supplied set: `C03_HERO.png` shows the opening mostly at a distance and `D05_GATE_MECHANISM.png` is dominated by a broad header face, hiding the leaf-to-track/motor relationship. The review protocol requires a validation slice that demonstrates a door from readable gameplay-scale views. Keep the saved evaluation camera fixed and adjust the geometry/guard placement or lighting so the existing view reveals the drive and hanger relationships; a supplemental oblique image may add evidence but should not replace the fixed view.

This is a visual-evidence decision. Reported cold checks (supports, attached items, protected bounds, cameras, markers and packed resources) remain separate evidence and do not resolve this visibility gap. S2 remains rejected history and is not scored. This report scores the S3 images only, against the same spawn references. It is not final acceptance: all category scores remain below the owner's >98 target, and this slice does not establish whole-corridor quality or coverage.

## Improvements that are visibly successful

- `C03_HERO.png`: the route arrows are reduced, leaving a clearer floor field around the cart and service bay. The open staging area reads more calmly than S1.
- `D01_CARRIER_OPERATION.png`: the cask seam and restrained handling marks break up the long cylinder without turning it noisy. The `FC-017` plate and bolted/clasped restraint hardware are legible; the shaped saddles seat the load more convincingly.
- `D02_WORKBENCH.png`: the raised fitted socket tray gives the opened case a believable purpose. The pegboard now shows a wrench, handled driver, pliers and another wrench, so the tool set is no longer a row of identical forms. Surface variation on the top helps separate the workbench from the clean architectural panels.
- `D03_UTILITY.png`: the `AIR / 07` identifier reads, and the collector/union, connected pipe branches, gauge stem and parked hose make a more credible utility assembly than the isolated board in S1. The new branching is visible and understandable.

## Corrections and evidence requests

1. **Make the gate construction legible — `D05_GATE_MECHANISM.png` and `C03_HERO.png`.** `D05` is dominated by an enormous dark horizontal header face and broad foreground leaf; the hangers, rail and motor drive inside the pocket blend into shadow. `C03` shows the open bay from too far away to resolve those same relationships. Keep the fixed evaluation camera and revise the header/guard geometry or lighting so this view reveals the full leaf edge, cassettes, hangers and motor drive together. If the leaf is open, show enough of its edge and pocket to prove where it parks. A separate oblique view may supplement, but not replace, the fixed view. Until the mechanism is legible, I cannot pass the door portion of the style slice.

2. **Keep highlight detail on the utility area — `D03_UTILITY.png`.** The pipework and collector are improved, but the upper-left wall clips to nearly featureless white and competes with the pale `AIR / 07` label. Reduce the local wall exposure or shift the practical light so the panel edge and pipe supports retain separation. Preserve the new functional connections.

3. **Add a small amount of human-use context without filling surfaces — `D02_WORKBENCH.png` and `C03_HERO.png`.** The tool kit and cup now tell a maintenance story, but the board and broad wall fields remain very pristine and there is no visible shift/handover cue. One restrained checked-work tag or folded maintenance note near the open case would add a human action and distinct paper material. Avoid adding more labels to the general wall.

4. **Maintain gameplay-distance readability on the cask — `D01_CARRIER_OPERATION.png`.** The `FC-017` text and seam read in this close view. Confirm the seam, handling marks and label still register in a new gameplay-distance view; they are small against the large light-colored barrel in `C03_HERO.png`. Do not increase scratch frequency to solve a scale/readability issue.

## Reference comparison and limits

The spawn references `VALIDATE_Spawn.png` and `VALIDATE_Material_A.png` remain the reference at 100. They show multiple room uses, stronger warm/cool separation and a broader visible material set, including wood, tile, fabric, rubber, painted locker steel, glass and paper. S3 is directionally compatible and better fabricated than S1, but still lacks the same breadth and lived-in finish. Corridor subject matter does not earn fidelity points by itself.
