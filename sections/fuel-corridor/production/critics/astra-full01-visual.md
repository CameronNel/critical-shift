## Principal visible defects

1. **Major — destination faces are anonymous (C04, C05, C10; supporting C06/C08).** C04's far closure, C05's right-side closure and C10's front-on plant closure show plain/back-like door faces and no readable destination on the visible header. In C05 the small upper plate is visibly blank. C06 shows broad plain reactor leaf panels without readable identity in frame. The names in the filenames provide more location information than the images do. This is a visible wayfinding failure; it does not prove no label exists on another side of the object.

2. **Major — turn and junction priority is weak (C01, C02, C07, C08).** C01's strongest sign is SERVICE, with the freight turn largely outside the visual emphasis. C02 presents a bright blank wall at the primary turn, without a readable continuation cue. C07's bypass turn looks like another unmarked wall corner. C08 offers a protruding door surround at left and a small distant W mark, but no legible clean-service destination. These repeated conditions reduce route comprehension even though open floor is visible.

3. **Moderate — expanded areas read as repeated corridor kit rather than distinct operating locations (C02, C04–C08, C10).** Similar wall-bay rhythm, bumper rails, grey leaf fields, ceiling bars and orange trim recur with little visible functional differentiation. A quiet connector is appropriate, but the current repetitions do not carry the specific service/handling identities present in the approved service concept and reactor/mine references. This is a composition/story observation, not a prescription for replacement geometry or extra clutter.

4. **Moderate — dark upper structure and bright blank fields weaken lighting hierarchy (C02, C04, C07, C10).** Ceiling beams merge into broad dark bands around the fixtures; blank wall/door planes receive much of the visible brightness. C03's header faces remain more legible and its task pools more purposeful. This contrast between the developed vignette and the rest of the route is visible across the set.

5. **Major evidence limitation — C06 does not show the complete reactor threshold.** The top of the portal/header and the floor/sill are outside the image; almost the entire frame is leaf surface. The image therefore cannot establish full portal proportions, threshold transition, head integration or destination readability. Those parts are not declared absent. The frozen framing provides inadequate evidence for this camera's stated outlet/transition purpose.

6. **Minor — close-detail finish remains coarse in places (D02/D03).** Glove weave competes with small folds and patches, wrench ends remain blunt, and some metal variation is broad rather than localized. The bench objects are neatly aligned and feel arranged. These details retain recognizable function and are smaller issues than the full-route deficiencies.

The earlier pale floor slivers are not apparent in these views. These strengths remain useful foundations; they do not establish quality in route views that show different geometry.
