## Observed strengths

- The image now belongs to the current facility family. Warm off-white wall panels, charcoal structural framing, restrained orange safety accents, dark service recesses and visible practical light pools are close to A05's authored Valorant-style language.
- The cask and its trolley read as a freight-handling assembly rather than a decorative cylinder. The long cradle, four wheels, end restraint and orange/white cask bands communicate a plausible handling task at a glance.
- The large `FUEL TRANSIT` opening gives the image a clear destination. The separate `SERVICE` opening and the left-hand tool/record cluster imply a useful personnel/support relationship without turning the frame into a prop catalogue.
- The floor bands, drain channels, overhead utility trays and portal framing provide readable route structure. The broad foreground leaves room for the cart and an operator-scale circulation read.
- The rendering avoids the rejected generic low-poly and uniform glossy-plastic read. The panel seams, dark recesses and localized warm fixtures provide shape and value hierarchy consistent with the inspected references.

## Corrections required

1. **Make freight flow unambiguous.** The cart is presented broadside in the central work area, while the visible arrows and `FUEL TRANSIT` opening imply movement toward the right/rear. It currently reads equally as parked display equipment or an obstruction. The next concept/build evidence should make the staging position, travel direction and unobstructed gate approach visually explicit, while preserving a real operator path around the cart.
2. **Strengthen human scale without adding a character.** The cart, bollards and door frames provide useful anchors, but there is no direct hand/waist/shoulder reference in the frame. A small, purposeful worker-use cue beside the work zone (for example PPE, a clipboard or a service tool in contact with a surface) would make the cask size and trolley handle height easier to judge. Do not use an oversized prop as a scale shortcut.
3. **Keep the floor from drifting into generic grunge.** The large floor field has broad mottling and repeated pale/orange directional marks. A05's floor is a clean panel system with selective wear; the mine plates reserve roughness for rock, wet service and traffic contact. Retain the panel rhythm and route marks, but keep variation broad, sparse and tied to use so the floor does not become a photographic dirt layer.
4. **Reduce decorative repetition in the next pass.** The several large chevrons, repeated bright portal strips and repeated orange edge lines are mostly functional, but together they approach visual wallpaper. Keep the strongest route marker and let the architecture, cart silhouette and lighting carry more of the hierarchy.
5. **Give the freight cluster one more specific maintenance cue.** The cask has strong bands and restraint, but its surrounding work context remains clean and generic compared with the mine references' attached valves, trays and damp service evidence. One plausible, localized handling/inspection cue would improve specificity; avoid random decals, extra screens or blanket scratches.
6. **Preserve reference contrast.** The warm practical lights and neutral panels are correct, but the broad floor and wall fields are close in exposure. Maintain visible falloff and contact shadow around the trolley and portal supports so the scene retains A05's depth instead of becoming a uniformly bright showroom.

## Interface read and limits

The concept plausibly communicates the F01 freight inlet and a service branch. The cask/trolley silhouette appears compatible with the declared 1.95 m cartridge and 2.20 m × 0.90 m carrier envelope at a conceptual level. A single generated frame cannot prove the nominal 2.60 m inlet, the smaller sampled 2.40 m × 2.20 m refinery evidence rectangle, the turn allowance, or the bypass restriction. Those must be measured in the authored Blender section. The concept should therefore be used as art-direction evidence, not as clearance evidence.

No replacement geometry, coordinate recipe, GPU render or numerical score is supplied in this review.
