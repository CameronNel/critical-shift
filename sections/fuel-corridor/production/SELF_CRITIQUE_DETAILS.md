   and saddles. A floating cable or decorative socket is not sufficient.
2. Replace identical centered handling chips with varied, contact-specific wear;
   give broken tile edges smaller secondary fractures rather than long clean
   geometric cuts. Keep all fragments at or below the walking surface.
3. Give the existing analog instruments sparse scale numerals, controlled face
   contrast and their existing pointers. Preserve physical materials and local
   lighting rather than making their faces glow.
