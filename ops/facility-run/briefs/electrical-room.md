# Electrical distribution room

Create a legible switchgear and breaker sequence, bus ducts with believable junctions and supports, backup/transfer distribution, manual operation/repair points and a comprehensible walkable aisle. Avoid repeating the reactor's small local breaker panel as the whole room. Own sections/electrical-room only.
