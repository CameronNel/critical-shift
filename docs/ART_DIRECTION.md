# Art Direction

## Core Direction

Critical Shift uses stylised industrial science fiction with adult human characters, grounded machinery, readable silhouettes, and strong environmental storytelling.

Characters must retain believable adult anatomy. Stylisation may simplify surfaces, amplify workwear, and strengthen silhouettes, but must not use oversized childlike heads, tiny torsos, stubby limbs, mascot faces, glossy toy materials, or nursery-like proportions.

Physical comedy comes from responsive animation, ragdolls, collisions, awkward teamwork, heavy equipment, rushed decisions, and the contrast between ordinary workers and enormous industrial systems. It must not depend on infantilised character design.

## Environment

The mine, refinery, reactor, turbine systems, medical area, locker rooms, compliance spaces, and transport infrastructure belong to one connected industrial site. The facility should feel engineered, used, layered, and physically coherent rather than arranged as a theme park.

Visual readability remains essential: department colours, hazard markings, silhouettes, lighting states, machine motion, steam, alarms, and contamination should communicate gameplay without turning the facility into a brightly coloured playset.

## Characters

Use varied adult body types, ages, faces, ethnic backgrounds, uniforms, and department equipment. Helmets and protective suits should look functional and worn. Faces may be expressive, but expressions should remain grounded in the game's industrial-comedy tone.

## Prohibited Drift

Future concept art and generated assets must avoid super-deformed anatomy, plush-toy styling, mascot-led branding, juvenile proportions, and overly polished mobile-game presentation.
