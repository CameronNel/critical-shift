# Canon and Setting

<!-- ART_DIRECTION_RESET_2026_09 -->
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Art-direction canon:** Critical Shift uses **grounded stylized semi-realism**. Valorant-style environment principles are the primary rendering influence; PEAK contributes readability and restraint only. The target is believable, tactile and simplified, **not** generic low-poly, toy-like, Three.js-looking, glossy sci-fi, or modern AAA photorealism. [ART_DIRECTION](/design/ART_DIRECTION.md) and [ART_REFERENCE_INDEX](/design/ART_REFERENCE_INDEX.md) override conflicting legacy style wording in this file.


The player characters are openly human employees working for an AI-run civilisation. They are not pretending to be machines.

Most humans live in material comfort because the machine administration provides housing, healthcare, food, entertainment, and freedom from most routine labour. Dangerous infrastructure work is presented as prestigious public service and meaningful human contribution. The crew begins broadly loyal to this system and influenced by its propaganda.

Worker uniforms and sealed suits are industrial safety equipment, identity systems, and telemetry platforms. They protect against radiation, contamination, chemicals, heat, pressure, dust, and machinery.

The disguise mechanic belongs to the human resistance. Infiltrators steal or forge employee uniforms, badges, paperwork, credentials, voice filters, and telemetry signatures so they can pose as legitimate workers and sabotage the facility.

The deeper truth behind the administration's energy demands remains deliberately unresolved for future narrative development.


## Visual canon status

Narrative canon does not prescribe a rendering style. All visual interpretation of canon follows [ART_DIRECTION.md](ART_DIRECTION.md). The facility is a real workplace in-fiction, so props, rooms and PPE should have believable use and construction even when visually simplified.
